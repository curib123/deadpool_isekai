# A Monster Called Luck

**Author:** minuszeroo  
**Repository:** `curib123/deadpool_isekai`  
**Genres:** Action • Adventure • Dark Fantasy • Comedy • Mystery • Isekai

*Every miracle makes him look more dangerous.*

Luck Everhart wants food, freedom, and the occasional convincing entrance. He survives impossible-looking situations through passive luck he never recognizes as a supernatural power. Competent witnesses connect the evidence into plans he did not make. Their belief gets him work, attention, enemies, and responsibilities his performance cannot safely guarantee.
