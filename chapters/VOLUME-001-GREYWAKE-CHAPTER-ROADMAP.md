# Volume 1 Chapter Roadmap — Greywake Arc

**Range:** CH001–CH026  
**Status:** CURRENT STRUCTURAL ROADMAP — 2026-10-03  
**Authority:** `planning/LUCK-EVERHART-STRUCTURAL-STORY-BIBLE.md`

Every chapter below uses distant third-person limited centered on Luck Everhart. Fortune Distortion is passive only.

# Movement I — The Undefined Stranger

## CH001 — Wrong Forest, Wrong World
**Goal:** survive the forest and find civilization.  
**Obstacle:** unfamiliar terrain, predator, damaged memory, no local knowledge.  
**Luck-specific beat:** he performs confidence for himself even while frightened.  
**Change:** survives an improbable causal chain and chooses the public name Luck Everhart.  
**Carryover:** shallow injury, sore ankle, three unknown coins, unreadable world.  
**Ending:** follows the road toward Greywake.

## CH002 — Undefined
**Goal:** enter Greywake and secure basic safety.  
**Obstacle:** every identity field fails.  
**Luck-specific beat:** tries to make administrative impossibility sound intentional.  
**Change:** admitted only under limited/unverified status.  
**Carryover:** officials now have a file/problem called Luck Everhart.  
**Mystery:** F-001 / identity failure only.  
**Ending:** Maelis's interest and restricted status create the need for further testing.

## CH003 — The Things They Can Measure
**Goal:** obtain a usable explanation or identity result.  
**Obstacle:** physical measurements work while registry identity does not.  
**Luck-specific beat:** prefers dramatic explanations; Maelis refuses unsupported claims.  
**Change:** anomaly becomes documented rather than dismissed as gate error.  
**Carryover:** Hesk sees a worker who cannot fit normal contracts.  
**Mystery:** F-002 / bounded identity anomaly.  
**Ending:** Hesk offers an irregular work path.

# Movement II — Frontier Work

## CH004 — Off the Books
**Goal:** get money, food, supplies, and freedom.  
**Obstacle:** Hesk's offer has no normal worker protections.  
**Luck-specific beat:** knowingly accepts a bad arrangement because it benefits him now.  
**Change:** Luck becomes irregular Road Guild labor.  
**Carryover:** legal vulnerability and first reputation side-effect.  
**Ending:** first road assignment at dawn.

## CH005 — A Better Hammer
**Goal:** complete bridge work and get paid.  
**Obstacle:** damaged structure, tool/equipment problems, opportunistic danger.  
**Luck-specific beat:** tries to look more capable than he is.  
**Fortune Distortion:** only through staged tool/material/terrain failure; no conjured equipment.  
**Change:** crew completes the job and begins overestimating Luck's engineering/timing.  
**Carryover:** payment, rumor, equipment damage or repair consequence.  
**Mystery:** F-004 remains bounded.  
**Ending:** Luck decides road work may be profitable/interesting.

## CH006 — The Road That Works
**Goal:** complete caravan duty and prove useful.  
**Obstacle:** abnormal animal tracks and a live chase crossing the road.  
**Luck-specific beat:** initially wants the impressive position; learns the useful position matters more.  
**Change:** Tavian establishes that animals are moving through the wrong corridors.  
**Carryover:** Luck now has a specialist he cannot dismiss.  
**Ending:** “the animals are moving wrong” becomes the investigation question.

## CH007 — You Should Be Dead
**Goal:** survive an unexpected predator attack and get injured people home.  
**Obstacle:** displaced predators, injury, Luck's reckless positioning.  
**Luck-specific beat:** his survival does not make the choice safe for others.  
**Change:** Sela/Tavian force injury and collateral-risk consequences onto the page.  
**Carryover:** wounds, treatment, reduced mobility, damaged trust.  
**Mystery:** F-003 / observational anomaly only.  
**Ending:** surviving is separated from choosing well.

## CH008 — Outside the Lanterns
**Goal:** help an outer-route community move/repair safely.  
**Obstacle:** poor maintenance, late warnings, fewer guards/resources.  
**Luck-specific beat:** his legend is less useful than actual labor.  
**Change:** route inequality becomes visible rather than theoretical.  
**Carryover:** Luck's view of Guild priorities changes.  
**Ending:** warning markers do not match Guild claims.

# Movement III — The Road Is Lying

## CH009 — Wrong Tracks
**Goal:** determine why incompatible species use the same corridor.  
**Obstacle:** physical evidence contradicts official migration maps.  
**Luck-specific beat:** must rely on Tavian's expertise.  
**Change:** repeated redirected movement becomes a pattern.  
**Carryover:** field evidence now challenges Guild records.  
**Ending:** movement points away from the profitable road toward old routes.

## CH010 — The Ledger Doesn't Balance
**Goal:** reconcile casualty/work records with field evidence.  
**Obstacle:** records look procedurally valid while totals do not match reality.  
**Luck-specific beat:** cannot read the evidence himself and must depend on Maelis/Jessa.  
**Change:** a statistical pattern links safer main-road reports with outer losses.  
**Carryover:** Maelis's audit becomes adversarial.  
**Ending:** record fraud/omission becomes a concrete lead.

## CH011 — Broken North
**Goal:** verify whether something physical is altering migration.  
**Obstacle:** dangerous washout, ridge-hounds, abandoned-road terrain.  
**Luck-specific beat:** passive lucky mechanics help survival but specialists identify the evidence.  
**Change:** first hidden movement-lure stake is found.  
**Carryover:** preserved evidence, road hazard remains, suspicion shifts toward Guild access.  
**Ending:** the mystery becomes deliberate infrastructure.

## CH012 — Someone Put This Here
**Goal:** determine what the lure does and whether it is isolated.  
**Obstacle:** incomplete evidence, political sensitivity, ownership claims.  
**Luck-specific beat:** realizes his own assignments overlap the manipulated corridors.  
**Change:** route overlay shows the main road is protected while outer communities absorb pressure.  
**Carryover:** Brynn/Kellan/Maelis have actionable but dangerous evidence.  
**Ending:** the “safe” side and “danger” side become undeniable.

## CH013 — Where They Die
**Goal:** establish who knew, who benefited, and whether redirection was deliberate.  
**Obstacle:** Hesk has a credible survival rationale and institutional authority.  
**Luck-specific beat:** recognizes Hesk evaluates people—including Luck—by utility.  
**Change:** hidden policy becomes an open political dispute.  
**Carryover:** trust in Road Guild authority fractures.  
**Ending:** the council must decide what to do with a system it now knows exists.

# Movement IV — Necessary Losses

## CH014 — Necessary Losses
**Goal:** decide whether/how to change the lure network.  
**Obstacle:** abrupt removal may endanger Greywake and other communities.  
**Luck-specific beat:** wants a clean moral answer and discovers the ecology will not provide one.  
**Change:** temporary freeze/mapping plan established; east homesteads report immediate danger.  
**Carryover:** time pressure makes patience politically and emotionally costly.  
**Ending:** Tavian names the risk: backflow.

## CH015 — One Stake Too Many
**Goal:** stop an immediate lure threat near civilians.  
**Obstacle:** waiting risks lives; changing the system risks unknown redirection.  
**Luck-specific beat:** acts because he refuses to watch the same people remain designated losses.  
**Change:** one lure is disabled/altered.  
**Carryover:** distant movement changes; responsibility becomes personal.  
**Ending:** monster calls/migration direction shift.

## CH016 — Backflow
**Goal:** evacuate people and contain migration pressure released by the intervention.  
**Obstacle:** multiple species move into previously protected corridors.  
**Luck-specific beat:** sees a morally understandable choice produce broader harm.  
**Change:** crisis expands; Hesk gains political leverage.  
**Carryover:** injuries/damage, damaged credibility, urgent planning need.  
**Mystery:** M-007 tonal hint only.  
**Ending:** Greywake now faces the consequence, not just the argument.

## CH017 — The Price of Being Right
**Goal:** stabilize casualties and prevent restoration of Hesk's sacrificial solution.  
**Obstacle:** Hesk can point to the backflow as proof.  
**Luck-specific beat:** cannot joke or claim intentionality around real injury.  
**Change:** coalition commits to finding a third option.  
**Carryover:** Kellan/Sela injuries and political cost persist.  
**Ending:** Luck chooses responsibility without accepting Hesk's premise.

# Movement V — No Acceptable Loss

## CH018 — No Acceptable Loss
**Goal:** design a third option before the migration surge arrives.  
**Obstacle:** incomplete maps, damaged routes, limited time/resources.  
**Luck-specific beat:** willingly lets specialists lead what he does not understand.  
**Change:** Broken North redirection plan is selected.  
**Carryover:** everyone now owns a concrete task and deadline.  
**Ending:** plan requires rebuilding the wrong road.

## CH019 — Build the Wrong Road
**Goal:** make Broken North viable as a migration corridor.  
**Obstacle:** poor materials, damaged works, attacks, time.  
**Luck-specific beat:** does unglamorous work instead of seeking spectacle.  
**Change:** corridor becomes barely usable.  
**Carryover:** resource depletion and dependence on precise timing.  
**Ending:** remaining lure network must be controlled.

## CH020 — The Last Lure
**Goal:** prevent Hesk from restoring the old sacrificial route.  
**Obstacle:** retained lure material, loyalists, evidence risk.  
**Luck-specific beat:** cannot solve Jessa's moral choice for her.  
**Change:** Jessa exposes internal evidence; coalition secures most resources.  
**Carryover:** Hesk retains one final practical leverage point.  
**Ending:** final lure site becomes the remaining threat.

# Movement VI — Greywake Holds

## CH021 — Before the Herd
**Goal:** evacuate exposed people and position defenses/crews.  
**Obstacle:** limited carts, injuries, fear, damaged roads, hours remaining.  
**Luck-specific beat:** serious mode—less performance, more concrete work.  
**Change:** preparation becomes irreversible as the surge arrives.  
**Carryover:** civilians and crews are committed to specific routes.  
**Ending:** first prey mass reaches the lantern line.

## CH022 — First Wave
**Goal:** keep the corridor open through the first migration wave.  
**Obstacle:** barricade failures, scattering animals, predators exploiting panic.  
**Luck-specific beat:** follows Tavian's timing instead of improvising too early.  
**Change:** the plan survives the first wave but a route begins pulling wrong.  
**Carryover:** infrastructure is weakened before the largest threat arrives.  
**Ending:** major territorial predator enters the compressed corridor.

## CH023 — Greywake Holds
**Goal:** hold the choke long enough for the migration body to commit to Broken North.  
**Obstacle:** strongest predator and failing infrastructure arrive together.  
**Luck-specific beat:** Fortune Distortion creates openings; collective action converts them.  
**Change:** physical line holds.  
**Carryover:** exhaustion, damage, and Hesk's final lure still threaten the plan.  
**Ending:** migration begins bending toward the outer route again.

## CH024 — No One Gets Chosen
**Goal:** stop Hesk's final lure without collapsing the migration solution.  
**Obstacle:** immediate danger competes with pursuing Hesk and preserving the route.  
**Luck-specific beat:** refuses Hesk's logic of assigning victims while accepting that choices still have cost.  
**Change:** final lure policy is stopped and the corridor receives the migration.  
**Carryover:** Hesk is defeated operationally; evidence and casualties remain.  
**Ending:** Greywake survives, but aftermath cannot be skipped.

# Movement VII — What Greywake Counts

## CH025 — What Greywake Counts
**Goal:** establish casualties, responsibility, and institutional change.  
**Obstacle:** Hesk's argument still has supporters; damage/trade losses are real.  
**Luck-specific beat:** receives contradictory reactions rather than universal praise.  
**Change:** Road Guild authority is stripped/restructured; outer communities gain representation.  
**Carryover:** wounds, repairs, testimony, reputation, and political division persist.  
**Ending:** Luck now has enough freedom/resources to choose whether to stay.

## CH026 — Before They Decide What I Am
**Goal:** decide whether Greywake becomes home or another role that owns him.  
**Obstacle:** staying means increasing expectations and labels.  
**Luck-specific beat:** chooses movement over being defined by his first legend.  
**Change:** leaves Greywake voluntarily and alone.  
**Carryover:** money, injuries/recovery, contacts, rumors, unresolved identity mystery.  
**Ending:** wider world; no named destination forced in the final Greywake beat.

# Final Lock

The Greywake arc must preserve the backflow consequence and collective climax. Removing those would collapse the arc's central argument about responsibility.
