# Merrowgate Chapter Roadmap

**Revision:** 2026-10-02. **Chapters:** CH027–CH050. **Prose complete:** CH027 only.
**Climax:** CH047–CH048. **Departure:** CH050. Destination DEST-002.

Rook arrives wanting paid work and passage. Merrowgate prices identity as liability: somebody must guarantee a person, cargo or contract. Cassian Venn profits from disruptions that make private security and an emergency monopoly charter look necessary. Rook unknowingly delivers altered brake assemblies for pay, so the investigation eventually puts his own receipt at the center of the accusation.

Saving a worker does not prove innocence. A useful accident cannot erase a delivery, debt or witness. The arc should force Rook to protect people while contesting a public version of his actions that contains uncomfortable truth.
