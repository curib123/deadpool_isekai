# Movement I — A Name Arrives Before the Man

## CH-027 — Salt in the Air
**Objective:** reach Merrowgate with enough money/resources to continue.  
**Conflict:** fares, baggage fees, unreadable notices, residual recovery, unfamiliar trade scale.  
**Luck engine:** ordinary cargo-transfer timing may make Luck look more experienced than he is; no supernatural construction.  
**Change:** Luck reaches the coast and sees Merrowgate.  
**Ending pull:** the port promises work, passage, and another bureaucracy.

## CH-028 — The Man on the Manifest
**Objective:** enter Merrowgate and gain a usable local status.  
**Conflict:** independent verification confirms physical presence but no valid native identity.  
**Supporting owner:** Captain Iria Voss owns procedure.  
**Luck engine:** none required.  
**Misunderstanding:** Luck tries to make the repeated failure sound deliberate; Iria treats it as liability.  
**Change:** he is admitted under limited practical conditions.  
**Ending pull:** ordinary bonded employment still needs a guarantor/workaround.

## CH-029 — Heard About You
**Objective:** turn his name into money before funds run low.  
**Conflict:** Greywake stories attract curiosity but make reliable employers cautious.  
**Supporting owners:** Dalen Marr and Nessa Quill introduce the rumor/work economy.  
**Luck engine:** a small coincidence may make one rumor seem true at the wrong moment.  
**Change:** Luck learns his reputation arrived before him.  
**Ending pull:** irregular work becomes the easiest option.

# Movement II — Everything Has a Price

## CH-030 — Everything Has a Fee
**Objective:** establish lodging, food, passage options, and legal access.  
**Conflict:** every service has a fee, deposit, sponsor, bond, or liability rule.  
**Luck engine:** none required.  
**Change:** the port's commercial logic becomes concrete.  
**Ending pull:** Luck needs steady work, not just travel money.

## CH-031 — Load-Bearing Reputation
**Objective:** complete legitimate dock/transfer work despite limited status.  
**Conflict:** crews are competent and skeptical; reputation is useful only if Luck can actually follow instructions.  
**Luck engine:** existing equipment/timing creates an improbable but plausible save.  
**Supporting owner:** dock foreman/crew owns the actual procedure.  
**Change:** one employer begins treating the Greywake stories as potentially useful evidence.  
**Ending pull:** reputation gains commercial value.

## CH-032 — Security, Privately
**Objective:** understand why private security has such visible influence in Merrowgate.  
**Conflict:** Cassian Venn's network solves real problems and has genuine public support.  
**Luck engine:** none.  
**Antagonist function:** Cassian is persuasive before he is suspicious.  
**Change:** Luck sees that "private control" is not automatically treated as corruption here.  
**Ending pull:** another port incident strengthens the security argument.

## CH-033 — The Port Keeps Score
**Objective:** help contain a warehouse/platform incident and protect active contracts.  
**Conflict:** damaged infrastructure, missing cargo, overlapping public/private authority.  
**Luck engine:** a rope, wedge, brake, or shifting load behaves at an improbably useful moment; workers still execute the rescue.  
**Change:** Luck sees that a brief accident creates long commercial consequences.  
**Ending pull:** private responders seem to benefit unusually quickly after damage.

# Movement III — Off the Manifest

## CH-034 — Cash, No Questions
**Objective:** secure enough money for better lodging and future passage.  
**Conflict:** a high-paying client explicitly wants work outside bonded inspection.  
**Luck engine:** none.  
**Character function:** Luck's vanity about being unclassifiable makes the offer feel flattering.  
**Change:** he knowingly accepts suspicious work.  
**Ending pull:** delivery route begins.

## CH-035 — Off the Manifest
**Objective:** move sealed counterweight brake assemblies and collect payment.  
**Conflict:** awkward route, legal risk, insistence that seals remain untouched.  
**Luck engine:** timing/route coincidence helps the group avoid one inspection point but does not erase the choice to bypass inspection.  
**Locked mistake:** Luck and Nessa knowingly avoid accountability while not knowing the sabotage purpose.  
**Change:** altered components enter the system.  
**Ending pull:** the job is complete, but the consequence is now waiting elsewhere.

## CH-036 — Somebody Else's Accident
**Objective:** rescue workers during a separate dry-dock/equipment failure.  
**Conflict:** moving machinery, water, damaged supports, confused authority.  
**Supporting owner:** Samir Kesran owns technical diagnosis and rescue geometry.  
**Luck engine:** an existing line catches, a brake bites, or a load shifts at the exact useful moment; Luck converts the opening physically.  
**Change:** Samir sees signs that not every recent failure looks like ordinary wear.  
**Ending pull:** technical suspicion enters the broader pattern.

## CH-037 — Cheap Cargo, Expensive Silence
**Objective:** decide whether recent incidents form a meaningful pattern.  
**Conflict:** each incident has a plausible independent explanation; records are fragmented.  
**Supporting owners:** Dalen/Nessa/Samir.  
**Luck engine:** none.  
**Change:** "who benefits?" becomes more useful than "what broke?"  
**Ending pull:** old/current records need comparison.

# Movement IV — The Risk Is Being Sold

## CH-038 — Old Words, New Ledger
**Objective:** trace how emergency authority and liability language changed over time.  
**Conflict:** old terminology, overlapping jurisdictions, records Luck cannot read himself.  
**Supporting owner:** archive staff/Dalen/Iria.  
**Luck engine:** a misfiled or surviving document may surface through plausible chance, but experts must interpret it.  
**Mystery:** F-010 may be planted here only after manuscript QA.  
**Change:** an old terminology mismatch becomes bounded evidence.  
**Ending pull:** money/authority patterns now need direct comparison.

## CH-039 — Who Profits From Panic
**Objective:** compare losses, debt purchases, assurance claims, contracts, and berth closures.  
**Conflict:** beneficiary pattern is strong while direct proof of Cassian's authorship remains incomplete.  
**Luck engine:** none.  
**Change:** the coalition identifies a Venn-linked network repeatedly gaining after disruptions.  
**Ending pull:** Cassian moves politically before proof is complete.

## CH-040 — The Charter
**Objective:** delay or narrow the Unified Harbor Security Charter long enough to investigate.  
**Conflict:** Cassian's proposal solves genuine coordination problems; accusation without proof would help him.  
**Luck engine:** none.  
**Misunderstanding/reputation:** Cassian uses Luck's irregular status and legend as evidence that the port needs stronger control.  
**Change:** Luck realizes his reputation can be commercial/political property.  
**Ending pull:** evidence from CH035 resurfaces.

# Movement V — The Useful Suspect

## CH-041 — Receipt With My Name On It
**Objective:** determine exactly what Luck/Nessa moved before others define the story.  
**Conflict:** records prove deliberate inspection bypass but not sabotage knowledge; an intermediary attempts to seize/destroy evidence.  
**Luck engine:** chance affects pursuit/traffic timing, not guilt or evidence meaning.  
**Change:** surviving records identify the component class and Luck's real involvement.  
**Ending pull:** investigators race to find affected machinery.

## CH-042 — Dangerous by Contract
**Objective:** save people when altered brake assemblies fail during crowded cargo transfer.  
**Conflict:** heavy cargo, structural motion, civilians, cascading equipment failure.  
**Supporting owners:** Iria commands perimeter; Samir diagnoses; crews operate machinery.  
**Luck engine:** one or more existing safety elements catch at improbable moments, creating rescue openings.  
**Hard consequence:** at least one death and real injuries/infrastructure damage remain.  
**Change:** Luck's "I didn't know" defense becomes morally incomplete because he chose not to know enough.  
**Ending pull:** he becomes politically toxic and legally vulnerable.

## CH-043 — I Could Leave
**Objective:** decide whether to disappear through informal routes.  
**Conflict:** leaving protects freedom but shifts more blame onto Nessa and strengthens Cassian's narrative.  
**Luck engine:** none.  
**Character function:** Luck stays because he refuses to let someone else own the meaning of his choice.  
**Change:** he accepts continued involvement without becoming a conventional hero.  
**Ending pull:** local cast must coordinate openly.

# Movement VI — Nobody Owns the Harbor

## CH-044 — Four Bad Plans and One Port
**Objective:** build one workable coalition plan from incompatible partial solutions.  
**Conflict:** Iria, Samir, Nessa, Dalen, and Luck each control different information/access.  
**Luck engine:** none required.  
**Change:** roles become explicit; no one person can solve the whole crisis.  
**Ending pull:** vulnerable infrastructure must be inspected without alerting saboteurs.

## CH-045 — The Weak Point
**Objective:** identify likely next sabotage points and prepare ordinary countermeasures.  
**Conflict:** repairs/preparation must look routine.  
**Supporting owner:** Samir owns engineering.  
**Luck engine:** an improbable observation or minor mechanical coincidence can reveal an existing weak point; Samir verifies it.  
**Change:** team narrows the likely attack window/locations.  
**Ending pull:** weather/tide and political timing converge.

## CH-046 — Before the Tide Turns
**Objective:** place trusted crews, evidence routes, emergency equipment, and communications before the crisis.  
**Conflict:** movement must remain quiet enough not to trigger panic or tip Cassian's network.  
**Luck engine:** minimal.  
**Change:** preparation is in place but fragile.  
**Ending pull:** conditions turn and coordinated failures begin.

# Movement VII — Merrowgate Stays Open

## CH-047 — Harbor Closed
**Objective:** prevent mass casualties and keep enough public routes open for responders.  
**Conflict:** weather, cargo, cranes, chain bridges, contradictory orders, private-security interference.  
**Supporting owners:** Iria/crews/Samir.  
**Luck engine:** two major probability-created openings maximum, each using pre-established equipment/environment and each requiring human conversion.  
**Change:** harbor remains partially functional; crisis exposes who is trying to seize control.  
**Ending pull:** evidence route is threatened during the physical emergency.

## CH-048 — Keep It Moving
**Objective:** keep the harbor functional long enough to preserve/seize evidence linking sabotage and emergency planning.  
**Conflict:** simultaneous infrastructure and evidence threats; Luck cannot be everywhere.  
**Luck engine:** multiple small causal turns may help, but no magical barriers/anchors/constructs.  
**Character function:** Luck chooses where to intervene and trusts specialists elsewhere.  
**Change:** evidence establishes deliberate Venn-linked coordination.  
**Ending pull:** physical survival gives way to public/legal judgment.

## CH-049 — What the Harbor Owes
**Objective:** establish responsibility without pretending one exposed scheme fixes the port.  
**Conflict:** Cassian has real public-service history; Luck's own off-manifest role is true; factions still want reform.  
**Luck engine:** none.  
**Change:** Cassian's network is exposed/limited, the charter is rejected or fundamentally reshaped, and Luck remains both participant and suspect.  
**Ending pull:** his identity still lacks a practical long-term contract solution.

## CH-050 — A Name Is Enough
**Objective:** settle debts, secure travel/payment options, and leave on his own terms.  
**Conflict:** Merrowgate still cannot create a native System identity.  
**Solution:** local witnessed-contract mechanism records Luck's declared name without claiming to define his soul/System status.  
**Luck engine:** minor departure irony only.  
**Change:** Luck gains practical access without surrendering self-authorship.  
**Ending pull:** wider route; Volume 3 remains unselected.
