# 3. Locked Structural Beats

**Red's Key Mistake:** CH-035 — Red knowingly accepts a suspicious off-manifest delivery and moves a sealed crate of replacement **counterweight brake assemblies** through an unregistered warehouse route because the pay is high and avoiding paperwork benefits him.

**Midpoint:** CH-039 — records from multiple incidents establish that sabotage, distressed debt, insurance/assurance claims, emergency-security contracts, and berth closures repeatedly benefit the same Venn-linked network.

One dockworker dies, several are injured, major infrastructure is damaged, and surviving broker/warehouse evidence links Red and Nessa to the off-manifest movement. ## CH-027 — Salt in the Air

**Movement:** I — A Name Arrives Before the Man  
**Location:** Avarran road/river transfer route; Brasswater approach; Merrowgate outer arrival district.  
**Characters Present:** Red Jackal; ordinary travelers, boat/road crews, minor merchants.

**Primary Objective:** Reach a major trade center where he can earn money and secure future passage without returning to Greywake.

**Exploration Purpose:** Make travel real; transition from frontier roads to Brasswater commerce, larger traffic flows, ships, cranes, and foreign accents/customs.  
**Character Beat:** Red discovers that leaving Greywake did not magically make food, lodging, or transport free.

**Supporting Development:** No major local cast required yet.  
**Antagonist Pressure:** Cassian's scheme exists independently; Red has not entered it.  
**Action Progression:** Travel hazard only; no major combat.  
**Play Logic:** None.  
**Regeneration Consequence:** Residual stiffness/fatigue from Greywake may still be noticed but does not dominate the chapter.  
**Aura-Farming Opportunity:** He tries to arrive at the Brasswater coast looking like a mysterious traveler; exhausted porters do not care.  
**Comedy Opportunity:** Large-scale trade bureaucracy begins before he has even reached the city proper.  
**Mystery / Foreshadowing:** None.  
**Continuity Dependencies:** CH-026 departure; no Greywake companion; Red still cannot read Veyran normally.  
**Knowledge Dependencies:** Red knows only general travel facts learned on the route, not Merrowgate politics.  
**Ending Beat:** Merrowgate's layered docks and chain bridges come into view, and Red chooses the port because work and passage are visibly abundant.
