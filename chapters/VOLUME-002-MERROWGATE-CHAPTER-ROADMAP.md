# Volume 2 Chapter Roadmap — Merrowgate Arc

**Range:** CH027–CH050  
**Status:** CURRENT STRUCTURAL ROADMAP — 2026-10-03  
**Current manuscript:** CH027  
**Authority:** `planning/LUCK-EVERHART-STRUCTURAL-STORY-BIBLE.md`

All chapters use distant third-person limited centered on Luck Everhart. Fortune Distortion is passive only. No chapter requires a supernatural display.

# Movement I — A Name Arrives Before the Man

## CH027 — Salt in the Air
**Goal:** reach a major trade center before travel money becomes a problem.  
**Obstacle:** fares, unreadable notices, transfers, lingering Greywake injuries.  
**Luck-specific beat:** attempts a mysterious arrival; busy workers mostly ignore it.  
**Change:** Merrowgate becomes the practical destination.  
**Carryover:** reduced funds, healing injuries, no local status.  
**Ending:** Merrowgate comes into view.

## CH028 — The Man on the Manifest
**Goal:** enter Merrowgate and gain usable local status.  
**Obstacle:** another independent verification fails to produce a normal identity record.  
**Luck-specific beat:** tries to make UNDEFINED sound intentional; Iria treats it as liability.  
**Change:** admitted under limited conditions but still barred from ordinary bonded employment.  
**Carryover:** Iria knows the anomaly; Luck needs a guarantor/workaround.  
**Mystery:** M-002 cross-jurisdiction reinforcement only.  
**Ending:** legal entry without normal employability.

## CH029 — Heard About You
**Goal:** turn his name into money.  
**Obstacle:** contradictory Greywake rumors make some employers curious and others cautious.  
**Luck-specific beat:** enjoys reputation until he sees other people pricing it.  
**Change:** Dalen/Nessa enter his working orbit.  
**Carryover:** first Merrowgate reputation expectations.  
**Ending:** Nessa offers casual work outside stricter verification.

# Movement II — Everything Has a Price

## CH030 — Everything Has a Fee
**Goal:** earn enough for lodging, food, and future passage.  
**Obstacle:** fees, liability, bonded/unbonded rules, crew boundaries.  
**Luck-specific beat:** discovers knowing which rule matters is a real skill.  
**Change:** informal dock work becomes viable.  
**Carryover:** Nessa/Dalen assess him commercially.  
**Ending:** he chooses to keep working the port.

## CH031 — Load-Bearing Reputation
**Goal:** complete difficult repair work and prove his reputation useful.  
**Obstacle:** wet surfaces, moving loads, real structural limits.  
**Luck-specific beat:** Samir rejects a visually impressive but unsafe idea.  
**Fortune Distortion:** if present, only as staged load/timing coincidence that workers still solve.  
**Change:** Samir respects usefulness, not mythology.  
**Carryover:** independent yards' rising costs become visible.  
**Ending:** Samir warns the economics are becoming unsustainable.

## CH032 — Security, Privately
**Goal:** collect/pay/work near a public security debate.  
**Obstacle:** Cassian offers a credible answer to real coordination failures.  
**Luck-specific beat:** initially prefers Cassian's polite commercial treatment.  
**Change:** Cassian/Iria ideological conflict becomes personal to the arc.  
**Carryover:** Luck enters Cassian's awareness as a useful irregular actor.  
**Ending:** Venn money/security looks attractive rather than obviously evil.

## CH033 — The Port Keeps Score
**Goal:** help clear a warehouse/infrastructure incident and protect active contracts.  
**Obstacle:** damaged structure, missing cargo, competing public/private authority.  
**Luck-specific beat:** focuses first on what broke; Dalen asks who profits from the loss.  
**Change:** first commercial-beneficiary pattern is noticed.  
**Carryover:** Venn-linked services gain from disruption.  
**Ending:** one example is suspicious but not proof.

# Movement III — Off the Manifest

## CH034 — Cash, No Questions
**Goal:** get a large payday.  
**Obstacle:** client demands secrecy and bypass of bonded inspection.  
**Luck-specific beat:** plausible deniability appeals to his dislike of bureaucracy.  
**Change:** Luck accepts a knowingly suspicious job.  
**Carryover:** moral/legal responsibility exists before he knows sabotage.  
**Ending:** higher pay secured; job scheduled.

## CH035 — Off the Manifest
**Goal:** move the sealed crate and collect payment.  
**Obstacle:** awkward route, legal risk, sealed cargo, no ordinary manifest.  
**Luck-specific beat:** chooses not to inspect because the deal depends on not asking.  
**Change:** altered brake assemblies enter the target system.  
**Carryover:** Luck/Nessa become part of the evidence trail.  
**Ending:** intermediary disappears after payment.

## CH036 — Somebody Else's Accident
**Goal:** rescue trapped workers and stop secondary collapse.  
**Obstacle:** heavy equipment, water, damaged supports, confused command.  
**Luck-specific beat:** cannot turn rescue into performance while people are trapped.  
**Fortune Distortion:** may create a physically plausible opening; Samir/Iria/workers convert it.  
**Change:** Samir finds signs the failure may be deliberate.  
**Carryover:** injuries/damage and Venn-linked financing pressure.  
**Ending:** Dalen reports the damaged operator is already being offered emergency financing.

## CH037 — Cheap Cargo, Expensive Silence
**Goal:** determine whether recent incidents form a pattern.  
**Obstacle:** each failure has a plausible independent explanation; records are inaccessible to Luck without help.  
**Luck-specific beat:** literacy limitation forces cooperation.  
**Change:** investigation shifts from mechanics to beneficiaries/contracts.  
**Carryover:** Nessa starts worrying about her CH035 client.  
**Ending:** older port records become the next lead.

# Movement IV — The Risk Is Being Sold

## CH038 — Old Words, New Ledger
**Goal:** trace how emergency incidents shift authority and ownership.  
**Obstacle:** changing terminology, overlapping jurisdictions, old records.  
**Luck-specific beat:** must trust other people to read evidence he cannot personally verify.  
**Change:** older records establish a recurring pattern of authority moving toward Venn-linked entities.  
**Mystery:** F-010 may be planted here only in bounded obsolete terminology; no cosmology explanation.  
**Carryover:** Venn representatives create time pressure by seeking the same records.  
**Ending:** commercial pattern becomes repeatable, not anecdotal.

## CH039 — Who Profits From Panic
**Goal:** decide whether the pattern is strong enough to act on.  
**Obstacle:** beneficiary pattern is clear but direct authorship proof remains incomplete.  
**Luck-specific beat:** must accept that coincidence can be suspicious even when he personally lives inside coincidences.  
**Change:** coalition recognizes a Venn-linked network.  
**Carryover:** Nessa identifies a shell-client mark tied to CH035.  
**Ending:** the suspicious job reconnects to the central scheme.

## CH040 — The Charter
**Goal:** delay concentration of emergency authority long enough to investigate.  
**Obstacle:** Cassian's charter solves genuine coordination problems and has real support.  
**Luck-specific beat:** Cassian uses Luck's own irregular status as an argument for stronger control.  
**Change:** opposition becomes political and public.  
**Carryover:** Cassian gains momentum while the coalition still lacks decisive proof.  
**Ending:** Nessa confirms the CH035 intermediary vanished and route records were erased.

# Movement V — The Useful Suspect

## CH041 — Receipt With My Name On It
**Goal:** determine exactly what Luck/Nessa moved before others define the story.  
**Obstacle:** evidence proves intentional inspection bypass, not sabotage knowledge; someone tries to destroy surviving records.  
**Luck-specific beat:** cannot honestly claim total innocence.  
**Change:** preserved evidence identifies the cargo as counterweight brake assemblies.  
**Carryover:** legal pressure on Luck/Nessa rises.  
**Ending:** investigators now know what system the parts could affect.

## CH042 — Dangerous by Contract
**Goal:** save people and prevent cascading failure when altered assemblies fail.  
**Obstacle:** moving cargo, failing brakes, crowded platforms, structural motion.  
**Luck-specific beat:** the consequence of CH035 becomes irreversible.  
**Fortune Distortion:** may reduce or redirect one hazard, but cannot undo death or injury.  
**Change:** one worker dies, others are injured, infrastructure is damaged, Luck's role becomes public.  
**Carryover:** Cassian converts the disaster into charter momentum.  
**Ending:** Luck becomes politically toxic before the rescue is even fully over.

## CH043 — I Could Leave
**Goal:** decide whether to flee or stay.  
**Obstacle:** staying threatens freedom; leaving abandons Nessa and cedes the public narrative.  
**Luck-specific beat:** chooses responsibility without adopting a hero identity.  
**Change:** Luck stays and joins the counter-effort.  
**Carryover:** he is now voluntarily entangled in the port's outcome.  
**Ending:** coalition can finally plan with him as a committed participant.

# Movement VI — Nobody Owns the Harbor

## CH044 — Four Bad Plans and One Port
**Goal:** combine separate expertise into a workable plan.  
**Obstacle:** every single-character solution fails alone.  
**Luck-specific beat:** accepts a defined role without treating it as ownership.  
**Change:** coalition plan exists with clear responsibilities.  
**Carryover:** each member risks reputation/resources.  
**Ending:** likely sabotage points and proof routes are identified.

## CH045 — The Weak Point
**Goal:** inspect vulnerable infrastructure and prepare subtle countermeasures.  
**Obstacle:** visible preparation would warn compromised actors.  
**Luck-specific beat:** must describe practical outcomes rather than hiding behind mystique.  
**Change:** likely failure points are mapped and limited safeguards placed.  
**Carryover:** Venn contractors increase presence.  
**Ending:** Nessa reports unusual key/personnel movement before worsening weather.

## CH046 — Before the Tide Turns
**Goal:** place crews, evidence routes, cargo lanes, and safeguards before the crisis.  
**Obstacle:** deteriorating weather and political time pressure.  
**Luck-specific beat:** does not chase every problem; specialists own their domains.  
**Change:** counter-plan becomes operational.  
**Carryover:** one coordinated failure begins before preparation is complete.  
**Ending:** harbor crisis starts.

# Movement VII — Merrowgate Stays Open

## CH047 — Harbor Closed
**Goal:** prevent mass casualties and keep public response routes open.  
**Obstacle:** simultaneous failures, weather, moving cargo/ships, private-security interference.  
**Luck-specific beat:** follows specialist timing instead of seeking the most dramatic intervention.  
**Fortune Distortion:** only opens plausible windows; Iria/Dalen/Samir/workers convert them.  
**Change:** immediate collapse is prevented.  
**Carryover:** crisis continues; evidence route becomes reachable.  
**Ending:** Nessa identifies the route to proof of coordination.

## CH048 — Keep It Moving
**Goal:** keep the port functional long enough to secure decisive evidence and prevent private seizure.  
**Obstacle:** simultaneous infrastructure, authority, and evidence crises.  
**Luck-specific beat:** chooses where not to intervene so others can finish their roles.  
**Change:** decisive records/agents are secured and public responders retain command.  
**Carryover:** Cassian's factual basis for emergency consolidation collapses.  
**Ending:** physical crisis is controlled enough for legal/commercial resolution.

## CH049 — What the Harbor Owes
**Goal:** establish responsibility and reform without pretending the port's security problems were imaginary.  
**Obstacle:** Cassian has legitimate service history; Luck/Nessa really bypassed scrutiny; trade damage is real.  
**Luck-specific beat:** accepts responsibility for his choice without accepting authorship of Cassian's scheme.  
**Change:** Venn network is constrained/exposed; monopoly charter is defeated or stripped.  
**Carryover:** death, injuries, repairs, legal/commercial consequences remain.  
**Ending:** Merrowgate begins repair under divided authority.

## CH050 — A Name Is Enough
**Goal:** settle debts, secure legal payment/travel options, and leave.  
**Obstacle:** System identity still cannot be validated.  
**Luck-specific beat:** accepts a narrow practical identity solution without pretending it answers who he truly was.  
**Change:** witnessed-contract workaround recognizes the declared name Luck Everhart locally.  
**Carryover:** contacts, money, unresolved origin, reputation.  
**Ending:** Luck departs; Volume 3 remains unselected.

# Final Lock

Merrowgate's central consequence is CH042. No rewrite may soften the death/injury/public responsibility enough to remove the reason CH043–CH049 exist.
