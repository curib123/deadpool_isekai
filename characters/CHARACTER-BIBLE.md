# CHAR-001 — Red Jackal
**Owner:** A043 Red Jackal Guardian  
**Status:** LOCKED identity, expandable history.

## Identity
Anti-villain; theatrical; self-centered; playful; dangerous; unpredictable; meta-aware; capable of looking genuinely cool or genuinely ridiculous.

He may help for selfish reasons, manipulate enemies, ignore authority, or accidentally look heroic. Do not flatten him into a conventional pure-hearted hero.

## Aura Farming
He may choose dramatic entrances, calculated silence, intimidating positions, slow walks through chaos, visually excessive manifestations, or pretend accidents were intentional. Aura farming is optional, not a quota.

## Serious Red Jackal
Because he often plays around, genuine silence/seriousness should signal that something important changed.

## Storytelling
Red Jackal tells the novel in first person. Reader-talk exists from Chapter 1 onward and is not a staged relationship arc. He also talks to himself; keep reader-talk, self-talk, and spoken dialogue distinguishable.

## Golden Comedy Rule
Never ask: "Where can I insert a joke?"
Ask: "What would Red Jackal genuinely find ridiculous about this situation?"
If the answer is nothing, do not add a joke.

## References
Earth pop-culture references may be brief, transformed, situational, and understandable without source knowledge. Do not reproduce substantial dialogue, lyrics, scenes, designs, or franchise lore.

# Supporting Cast Rule
Supporting characters need independent goals, histories, flaws, skills, relationships, and conflicts beyond reacting to Red Jackal.
