# Volume 1 Knowledge State — Greywake Arc

**Status:** CURRENT — 2026-10-03  
**Authority:** `planning/LUCK-EVERHART-STRUCTURAL-STORY-BIBLE.md`

# 1. Luck — Opening Knowledge

Luck knows:
- he survived an Earth explosion or similarly catastrophic final memory;
- his autobiographical memory is damaged;
- Veyr is not familiar;
- his chosen public name is Luck Everhart;
- several improbable accidents have helped him survive;
- he cannot read local writing;
- he needs food, shelter, money, and information.

Luck does not know:
- Soul Drift as a technical explanation;
- why the System has no record of him;
- Fortune Distortion as his supernatural ability;
- protected cosmology;
- Greywake's lure network.

# 2. Luck — What He May Learn in Greywake

He may learn:
- the System and independent appraisals fail to classify him;
- physical measurements still work;
- Greywake depends heavily on roads and local production;
- outer routes receive less protection;
- migration patterns are wrong;
- lure equipment is deliberately influencing movement;
- Hesk authorized/defended the policy;
- changing a system without understanding it can cause backflow;
- his public reputation can create obligations and suspicion;
- his decisions can put other people at risk.

He may not learn that Fortune Distortion is his personal supernatural power.

# 4. Mystery Boundary

Allowed in Volume 1:
- repeated identity failure;
- no-record anomaly;
- observable improbable survival;
- bounded hints that causes and consequences reorganize in unusual ways.

Not allowed:
- full Soul Drift explanation;
- Fortune Distortion explained to Luck as fact and accepted by him;
- Great Design truth;
- technical Fate/cosmology exposition.

# 5. End-of-Volume Knowledge

By CH026 Luck knows what Greywake did and why Hesk defended it.

He still does not know:
- the cause of his Earth death;
- the mechanism of his arrival;
- why the System lacks him;
- that passive probability distortion is his supernatural power.

His ignorance is informed, not amnesiac: he remembers evidence and competing theories but rejects certainty where proof is missing.
