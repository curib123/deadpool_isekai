# 1. Luck — Opening Knowledge

At CH001 Luck knows:
- almost nothing reliable about Veyr;
- his autobiography is damaged;
- an explosion preceded his arrival;
- he retains ordinary Earth concepts and social instincts;
- he likes theatrical presentation.

He does not know his original reliable full name.

He chooses **Luck Everhart** in CH001.

# 2. Luck — What Greywake Teaches Him

By the end of CH026 Luck knows:
- Veyr has a World System;
- the System cannot classify him normally;
- independent interfaces fail consistently;
- physical instruments can still measure him;
- Wayfarer Tongue Tokens translate speech but not writing;
- he still cannot read ordinary Veyran fluently;
- Greywake survives through roads, trade, workers, militia, and outer production;
- Hesk manipulated migration with hidden lure infrastructure;
- records can hide people by classifying losses as ordinary frontier cost;
- disabling part of a system without understanding the whole can cause backflow;
- his own choices can contribute to harm even when his intention was defensible;
- people tell increasingly elaborate stories about his actions.

Luck still does not know:
- Fortune Distortion is a supernatural power;
- the exact reason coincidences cluster around him;
- the exact Soul Drift mechanism;
- the Earth explosion cause;
- protected cosmology.
