# Merrowgate Continuity and Knowledge State

**Current prose:** CH027 only. **Remaining:** CH028–CH050 planned.
**Revision:** 2026-10-02.

Rook leaves Greywake voluntarily and reaches Merrowgate after several days of travel. His stitches and bruises are healing normally. He keeps Sela's bandage packet because pain makes it useful. Ferry fare and a separate baggage fee reduce his purse; travel does not create money.

The ferry load is saved by five people, wedges and a rope. Rook's boot finds useful leverage on an existing timber brace. No manifestation, superhuman lift or automatic restoration occurs. Nobody at the ferry learns the origin of his luck.

Wayfarer Tongue Tokens translate speech only within range. Rook cannot read fare boards or port signs. He still has Greywake's wooden entrant tag; it is not a Merrowgate license, a bonded-work guarantee or a native registry root.

CH027 ends at passenger intake after he hears a guarantor restriction for bonded work. He has not yet met Iria, Dalen, Nessa, Samir or Cassian in prose. No port conspiracy, mastermind identity or new F-ID is established by that hook.

CH028 may confirm repeated UNDEFINED results and grant limited local status. CH029 may let rumors arrive before reliable work. F-010 is planned only for CH038 and remains unplanted. Later injuries, arrests, receipts and political consequences must be earned in their chapters, not promoted from this plan to current facts.
