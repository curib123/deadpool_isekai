**Owner:** A060 Destination Director  
**Supporting Owners:** A011 Geography Editor, A012 Nations & Politics Editor, A013 Culture Editor, A014 Economy & Travel Editor, A020 World History Director, A050 Mystery Director, A062 Destination Culture QA, A100 Continuity Director, A111 Logic Editor  
**Phase:** 4 — Destination Inventory  
**Status:** CANON SUPPORT RECORD — NO ROUTE ORDER

This file catalogs existing World Bible locations that could support future destination arcs. It does **not** select Volume 1, create an arc order, assign characters, lock antagonists, or plan chapters/scenes/manuscript prose.

## Classification

- **HIGH POTENTIAL** — location can support a full destination arc with strong local identity and multiple conflict paths.
- **RESERVED** — strong location, but its mystery/scale makes it better protected for later use.
- **UNUSED** — valid location with future potential, but currently less structurally rich than stronger candidates.
- **NOT SUITABLE** — useful world location, but currently too broad, too narrow, or better used as travel/transition/background rather than a full destination arc.

## Naming Law

If a destination is later selected, the arc name must be:

**[Destination Name] Arc**

The inventory does not itself create any arc.

# AVARRA

## DEST-001 — Greywake

**Classification:** HIGH POTENTIAL  
**World Bible Source:** PLACE-U01; Hollow March / Avarra / Valedorn frontier.

**Geography:** River-edge frontier settlement where old roads, monster migration routes, wet forest, and unstable northern tracks meet.

**Culture / Social Identity:** Valic frontier culture rather than central river nobility. Practical hospitality, guild survival, caravan dependence, militia duty, and resentment toward distant taxation.

**Visual Identity:** Timber palisades, river fog, lantern bridges, guild boards, muddy monster roads, half-abandoned stoneworks older than the settlement.

**History:** Built and rebuilt around failed northern expansion routes. Its survival depends on seasonal monster patterns and trade traffic.

**Local Factions:** Frontier guilds, settlement council, Valedorn tax agents, caravan interests, local wardens, independent hunters.

**Primary Dangers:** Monster migration, missing travelers, collapsing roads, isolated ruins, political neglect.

**Travel Entry:** River barge, northern Valedorn road, caravan route.

**Travel Exit:** Deeper Hollow March routes, Crownspine detours, return south by river.

**Possible Local Conflict Types:**
- frontier survival crisis;
- guild corruption or resource monopoly;
- monster-route displacement;
- conflict between settlement autonomy and Valedorn authority;
- abandoned infrastructure awakening.

**Possible Antagonist Types:** Local Power, Institutional Containment, Survival/Opposing Force.

**Exploration Potential:** High — roads, ruins, forest, river, outposts, guild sites, abandoned settlements.

**Mystery Connections:** M-002, M-005, M-007 are easy to touch lightly; M-009 only if ruins support it without overloading the local story.

**Return Potential:** High — frontier conditions and politics can visibly change after Red Jackal leaves.

**QA Note:** Excellent shounen-adventure flexibility without requiring early endgame lore.

## DEST-002 — Merrowgate

**Classification:** HIGH POTENTIAL  
**World Bible Source:** PLACE-U02; Free Port of Merrowgate; Brasswater trade sphere.

**Geography:** Dense independent port at the meeting point of Avarran and Kharadi maritime trade.

**Culture / Social Identity:** Mixed Valic, Serekh, shipwright, mercenary, migrant, and merchant customs. Reputation is commercial rather than noble.

**Local Factions:** Harbor council, merchant guilds, Open Hand Caravans, smugglers, shipwright houses, private security companies, foreign agents.

**Primary Dangers:** Smuggling wars, port corruption, espionage, maritime monsters, privateers, trade sabotage.

**Travel Entry:** Brasswater vessel or Avarran coastal road.

**Travel Exit:** Morrowchain, Kharad ports, Avarran interior.

**Possible Local Conflict Types:** Trade conspiracy, criminal power struggle, political annexation pressure, cursed cargo, maritime blockade.

**Possible Antagonist Types:** Local Power, Ideological Absolutist, organized crime, foreign agent.

**Exploration Potential:** Very high urban exploration, docks, markets, shipyards, undercity storage, foreign districts.

**Mystery Connections:** M-002 through cross-border identification; M-011 through standards/guild systems; M-001 only indirectly.

**Return Potential:** Very high — trade consequences make change easy to show.

However, Phase 1 did **not** establish a specific canonical name, geography, culture, and political identity for a divine-domain destination.

Phase 4 therefore does **not** invent one merely to fill a category.

Before a future divine destination can receive a DEST-ID, the World Bible must add:
- canonical place name;
- continent/region;
- local population/culture;
- religious institution;
- divine-law behavior;
- travel route;
- historical role.

This is a world-expansion requirement, not a failure of the existing inventory.
