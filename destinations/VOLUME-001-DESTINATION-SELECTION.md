# Volume 1 Destination Selection

**Project:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Selection Stage:** Gate 5 — Destination Selection  
**Status:** SELECTED — PENDING QA AT CREATION  
**Selected Destination:** DEST-001 — Greywake  
**Future Arc Name:** **Greywake Arc**  
**Scope:** destination selection only; no volume architecture, chapter plan, scene plan, or manuscript prose

## 1. Selection Decision

The studio selects:

# **DEST-001 — Greywake**

as the destination for the **first major volume**.

Greywake remains classified **HIGH POTENTIAL** in the Phase 4 inventory. This document adds a separate selection state; it does not change the inventory classification system.

The selection committee reviewed all eligible HIGH POTENTIAL destinations. No full ranking or route order is canonized.

## 2. Why Greywake Works as the Opening Destination

### Opening Accessibility

Greywake is a frontier settlement rather than a major capital, imperial center, or mystery-heavy anomaly zone.

This lets the opening teach Veyr at human scale:

- guild boards;
- roads;
- caravans;
- local officials;
- militia;
- hunters;
- ordinary System use;
- common magic;
- monster danger;
- money;
- food;
- lodging;
- travel rules;
- settlement politics.

Readers can understand normal life before the series expands toward larger political and metaphysical structures.

### Shounen Adventure Value

Greywake naturally supports:

- wilderness travel;
- monster encounters;
- dangerous roads;
- guild work;
- survival;
- abandoned structures;
- rescues;
- chases;
- local rivalries;
- exploration;
- escalating action.

It can deliver frequent movement and combat without needing war, cosmic threats, or elite political stakes in the first volume.

### Action Frequency

The Hollow March environment creates believable reasons for action:

- monster migration;
- missing travelers;
- road failures;
- isolated outposts;
- dangerous ruins;
- forest threats;
- caravan incidents.

Action can therefore emerge from the environment and local society rather than requiring a contrived tournament or immediate world-ending enemy.

### Cultural Introduction

Greywake introduces **Valic culture from the frontier**, not from its royal center.

This is useful because readers can encounter:

- hospitality customs;
- guild certification;
- System use;
- river trade;
- frontier charters;
- Valedorn authority;
- local resentment toward distant taxation;

without immediately needing to understand the Nine River Houses, royal succession, court politics, or every major institution in Veyrhold.

### Local Conflict Capacity

Greywake can sustain a complete local problem through several already-canonical pressures:

- survival;
- guild authority;
- settlement autonomy;
- monster-route displacement;
- resource control;
- political neglect;
- abandoned infrastructure.

**No single local conflict is selected in this phase.**

The Local Story Editor will choose and canonize the actual Volume 1 conflict only during Volume/Arc Architecture.

### Red Jackal Compatibility

Greywake creates immediate contrast with Red Jackal.

The settlement depends on:

- practical cooperation;
- preparation;
- predictable rules;
- local reputation;
- survival discipline.

Red Jackal is:

- unregistered;
- theatrical;
- self-centered;
- improvisational;
- unpredictable;
- willing to break procedure;
- interested in spectacle.

That creates natural friction without requiring the world to become hostile solely because he is the protagonist.

His aura farming can succeed, fail, or become ridiculous in a frontier environment where people care more about surviving the next road than admiring dramatic poses.

### Supporting-Cast Entry Potential

Greywake can logically intersect with multiple **types** of Phase 2 character without assigning anyone yet:

- a System investigator;
- a Warden or frontier professional;
- a caravan/logistics specialist;
- a guild-certified adventurer;
- a healer;
- an independent mercenary or traveler.

This keeps cast deployment flexible.

**No supporting character is assigned to Greywake by this selection.**

Character entry must be justified during Volume Architecture.

### Antagonist Capacity

Greywake supports several antagonist frameworks without locking one yet:

- Local Power;
- Institutional Containment;
- Survival/Opposing Force;
- criminal/resource exploitation;
- frontier authority abuse.

This lets the first antagonist arise from Greywake's actual local conditions rather than being imported solely to challenge Red Jackal.

**No main antagonist is selected in this phase.**

### Early Mystery Compatibility

Greywake is especially safe for **surface-level mysteries**:

- M-002 — UNDEFINED status;
- M-005 — regeneration;
- M-007 — early Fate irregularity, if used lightly.

M-009 ancient-history material may appear only as background-level ambiguity if approved later.

Greywake does **not** require:

- Savael;
- Exterior Needle revelation;
- Great Design exposition;
- Eidrathi explanation;
- Exception Covenant;
- Exterior Echo confirmation.

This protects the reveal ladder.

### Foreshadowing Compatibility

The location could eventually support early planned clues such as:

- F-001 — independent appraisals fail consistently;
- F-002 — physical data without identity data;
- F-003 — healing encounters an already-correcting body;
- F-004 — Play Logic follows meaning more than spell grammar.

However:

**No F-ID is planted by this selection document.**

All foreshadowing remains:

**PLANNED — NOT YET PLANTED**

until actual manuscript text exists.

### Play Logic Demonstration Space

Greywake is well suited to early-stage Play Logic because problems can be physical, immediate, and local.

Examples of *capacity*, not locked scenes:

- improvised tools;
- strange defensive objects;
- unconventional mobility;
- absurd but practical monster responses;
- short-lived environmental solutions.

This matches Stage I — Improvised Manifestation without forcing early conceptual-scale reality manipulation.

### Power Balance

The frontier remains dangerous despite regeneration and Play Logic because:

- Red Jackal begins with poor control;
- wilderness threats may attack repeatedly;
- civilians cannot regenerate;
- routes and supplies can fail;
- restraint, exhaustion, poison, magic, and numbers still matter;
- collateral damage matters.

The opening therefore demonstrates that being anomalous is not the same as being unbeatable.

### Travel Logic

Greywake has multiple established entry routes:

- river barge;
- northern Valedorn road;
- caravan route.

Its exits preserve future freedom:

- deeper Hollow March;
- Crownspine routes;
- return south toward the Sable Basin.

This makes Greywake an effective starting node without defining where Volume 2 goes.

### Series Expansion

Beginning in Greywake keeps larger settings available for escalation.

The story can later grow into:

- major cities;
- international ports;
- deserts;
- imperial politics;
- demonic territories;
- supernatural forests;
- islands and maritime travel;
- polar survival;
- anomaly zones;
- divine locations once canonized.

Greywake therefore establishes the floor of the adventure without prematurely establishing its ceiling.

## 3. Destination Identity Lock

It does not define:

- chapter count;
- exact local conflict;
- antagonist;
- supporting cast;
- climax;
- mystery clue;
- ending;
- next destination.

Those belong to Phase 5 Volume/Arc Architecture.

## 4. Known Risks

### Risk A — Greywake and Hollow March Overlap

Greywake sits inside/near the broader Hollow March frontier identity.

**Control:** Greywake must remain the actual destination. Hollow March is surrounding geography, not a second simultaneous arc name.

### Risk B — Generic Frontier Fantasy

Frontier towns are common genre settings.

**Control:** later Volume Architecture must emphasize canonical Greywake-specific identity:
- river-edge geography;
- Valic frontier customs;
- settlement autonomy;
- lantern bridges;
- half-abandoned stoneworks;
- monster migration;
- guild/caravan dependence;
- Valedorn tax pressure.

### Risk C — Too Much Mystery Too Early

Old ruins could tempt the story to expose Eidrathi history immediately.

**Control:** M-009/M-010/RL-3+ material remains restricted by the Mystery Bible. Ancient structures may exist without providing their true explanation.

### Risk D — Red Jackal Solves Frontier Problems Too Easily

**Control:** use Stage I Play Logic, poor reliability, consequence rules, and threats to people/environment that regeneration cannot trivialize.

## 5. Selection Boundary

This decision canonizes only:

- **Volume 1 destination:** Greywake;
- **DEST-ID:** DEST-001;
- **arc naming form after QA:** Greywake Arc.

Everything else remains for Phase 5.

No other destination is assigned a volume number.

No Volume 2 destination is selected.

No route order beyond Greywake is created.
