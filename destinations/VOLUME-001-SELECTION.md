# Selection Rationale

Greywake is the approved opening destination because it can introduce the world at human scale before the series expands into capitals, empires, supernatural domains, ancient anomaly zones, or high-level metaphysical conflicts.

It gives the opening volume a contained community surrounded by dangerous wilderness. That makes the setting understandable quickly while still showing that Veyr is much larger beyond the settlement.

## Opening Accessibility

**PASS.**

Greywake is a frontier settlement rather than a major capital or highly specialized magical domain.

Readers can learn Veyr through immediately understandable structures:
- a settlement;
- roads;
- guilds;
- caravans;
- militia/wardens;
- local authority;
- taxes;
- monsters;
- travelers;
- dangerous wilderness.

The opening does not require readers to understand imperial politics, advanced metaphysics, ancient history, or international diplomacy first.

## Shounen Adventure Value

**PASS.**

Greywake naturally supports:
- exploration;
- monster encounters;
- road missions;
- wilderness danger;
- guild activity;
- rescue situations;
- rival adventurers;
- dangerous ruins;
- escalating threats outside the walls;
- physical travel.

The location allows action and adventure to begin early without making the entire world feel immediately apocalyptic.

## Action Frequency

**PASS.**

The Hollow March already contains:
- monster migration routes;
- failed roads;
- abandoned settlements;
- broken forest;
- frontier travel;
- isolated ruins.

Action can arise from the environment and local survival conditions rather than requiring a world-ending villain.

No specific battle sequence is locked by this selection.

## Cultural Introduction

**PASS.**

Greywake introduces **Valic frontier culture** rather than the full political complexity of Veyrhold.

This allows readers to encounter:
- Valic hospitality;
- guild practices;
- frontier independence;
- caravan dependence;
- settlement councils;
- Crown/Valedorn authority at a distance;
- tension between center and frontier.

The cultural frame is distinct enough to feel real but simple enough for an opening volume.

## Red Jackal Compatibility

**PASS.**

Greywake creates immediate friction with Red Jackal's personality.

The setting contains:
- practical people who value survival over performance;
- local authorities who may demand identification;
- guild structures that rely on System records;
- frightened civilians;
- dangerous wilderness where improvisation matters;
- situations where his theatrics can either help morale or make things worse.

This supports both sides of his identity:
- absurd, theatrical, aura-farming Red Jackal;
- genuinely dangerous and serious Red Jackal when consequences become real.

The location does not require him to become a conventional hero.

# Destination Identity Lock

For future Volume 1 planning, Greywake must remain recognizably:

- a **river-edge frontier settlement**;
- part of the **Hollow March**;
- politically connected to **Valedorn** but distant from central authority;
- dependent on **guilds, caravans, militia/wardens, and seasonal routes**;
- threatened by **monster migration and failing/abandoned infrastructure**;
- visually defined by **timber defenses, river fog, lantern bridges, muddy roads, forests, and older stoneworks**.

If later Volume Architecture replaces these with generic city material, Greywake fails Destination Identity QA.
