# 4. Why Merrowgate Fits the Current Series State

Merrowgate is an existing independent Brasswater trade city between Valedorn and Serekh influence.

It provides an immediate structural contrast with Greywake:
- frontier settlement → dense independent port;
- road survival → maritime/commercial movement;
- relatively bounded local population → mixed migrant, merchant, shipwright, mercenary, and foreign communities;
- frontier guild abuse → overlapping commercial, criminal, civic, and foreign interests.

This expands the world without escalating directly into capital politics or endgame anomaly territory.

# 5. Red Jackal Compatibility

Merrowgate can involve Red Jackal without forcing conventional heroism.

Natural involvement paths may arise from:
- transport;
- temporary work;
- money;
- access to passage;
- disputes involving his lack of valid System identity;
- unwanted attention created by his growing reputation;
- personal inconvenience becoming entanglement.

The arc must not begin from a generic duty to save strangers.

His deadpan first-person voice, anti-villain identity, self-authored agency, and optional aura-farming remain locked.

# 6. Power Compatibility

Volume 2 begins with the same bounded Stage I Play Logic established in Greywake unless later architecture earns a controlled progression.

Merrowgate naturally supports Stage I utility through:
- cargo handling;
- rigging;
- temporary barriers;
- rescue access;
- dock/ship improvisation;
- short-lived practical tools.

Play Logic must not become effortless reality rewriting.

Regeneration remains painful, exhausting, and unable to restore stamina or protect other people.
