# Red Jackal Phase 1 Foundation Design

## Goal

Build the complete macro world for *This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.* under the Red Jackal Light-Novel Studio workflow. The result must be usable as the authoritative foundation for later destination selection, while deliberately creating no arcs, chapters, scenes, or manuscript prose.

## Scope

The Phase 1 team consists of the Series Director, World Bible Director, Geography Editor, Nations & Politics Editor, Culture Editor, World History Director, and Systems team. Their shared deliverable is a coherent world that is larger than the first route and capable of supporting a long-running destination-based series.

In scope:

- A named world with cosmological position, scale, known and unexplored reaches, dimensional boundaries, and supernatural zones.
- A connected geography registry covering continents, seas, islands, regions, places, and landmarks.
- Major nations and political structures with distinct geography, succession, military, economy, religion, and diplomatic pressures.
- Distinct cultures expressed through values, family, hierarchy, food, clothing, architecture, etiquette, festivals, art, naming, taboos, religion, magic, and historical trauma.
- An original historical chronology with public narratives, false beliefs, and writer truth.
- Factions, currencies, trade, travel times, border controls, and ecological rules that make the world operational rather than decorative.
- A core systems foundation distinguishing the World System, magic, reincarnation, Fate, Great Design, Red Jackal's anomaly, and Play Logic.
- A foundation QA record showing each gate's evidence and any intentionally deferred material.

Out of scope:

- Destination order, arc names, volume plans, chapter plans, scene sheets, and manuscript prose.
- Red Jackal's detailed backstory beyond what the locked constitution and systems foundation require.
- A fixed series roadmap. The roadmap may change state from blocked to eligible for later planning, but it must remain empty.

## Canon and file ownership

`world/WORLD-BIBLE.md` remains the sole authority for geography, nations, culture, history, factions, economy/travel, ecology, and knowledge layers. `systems/SYSTEMS-BIBLE.md` remains the sole authority for metaphysics and power-system mechanics. `series/SERIES-CONSTITUTION.md` and the locked character record remain higher-order constraints.

`qa/FOUNDATION-QA.md` records the review outcome; it does not redefine canon. `series/ROADMAP.md` records only the gate state and remains free of destination entries.

The studio roles collaborate through shared IDs and cross-references. Geography establishes physical constraints first; nations, cultures, history, and systems must explain their placement and effects rather than silently overwrite it. The Series Director resolves contradictions against the constitution, and the Systems team must preserve the locked Red Jackal registry failure.

## World design principles

The world will be built around a connected central landmass, a maritime belt, and an outer frontier so that routes can move between dense political spaces, culturally distinct regions, and genuinely unknown territory. Mountains, rivers, currents, climate, resource distribution, and supernatural boundaries must explain settlements, borders, trade, and danger.

At least four major cultures must remain distinguishable after their names are removed. Nations may share language families or religions, but their institutions and historical pressures must produce different behavior. The world must contain both ordinary material economies and supernatural economies; magic cannot replace every logistical constraint.

History will use original eras and distinguish public knowledge, expert knowledge, hidden knowledge, and false belief. The Great Design's deepest truth stays writer-only. The Foundation QA may confirm that it exists and has limits without turning it into an early story reveal.

## Systems design principles

The World System is a registration and regulation layer, not reality itself. Magic is a learned or inherited interaction with mana and local traditions, not a synonym for System permissions. Fate governs patterned convergence and probability, not every mundane action. Reincarnation governs souls and identity across lives. The Great Design is deeper than both System and Fate and remains bounded rather than omnipotent.

Red Jackal remains physically real but unregistered: no hidden normal level, class, or secret registry record may be introduced. Play Logic is imagination-based anomaly behavior with interpretation, focus, scale, duration, resistance, collateral, and recovery limits. The Systems team must define credible counters so Red Jackal cannot solve every conflict without uncertainty or consequence.

## QA acceptance criteria

Foundation QA passes only if the World Bible and Systems Bible contain named, cross-referenced macro structures; travel and ecology obey the map; cultures are non-interchangeable; systems are distinct; Red Jackal's locked status is preserved; enough unused places remain for later destination selection; and no arc/chapter content has been added. If any criterion fails, the roadmap stays blocked and the gap is recorded rather than papered over.
