# Art Bible

**Status:** MANHWA FOUNDATION

## Core Goal

Create an original, readable, cinematic dark-fantasy manhwa identity that supports action, adventure, comedy, horror, mystery, and Red Jackal's theatrical behavior without imitating a specific artist or existing series.

## Visual Priorities

1. character identity consistency;
2. readable acting and silhouettes;
3. clear action geography;
4. controlled value and color hierarchy;
5. environments that express local culture;
6. selective spectacle rather than constant spectacle;
7. comedy through timing and acting without breaking serious consequences.

## Character Rendering

Faces must remain recognizable across angle, distance, emotion, lighting, and damage. Hair, eyes, build, costume construction, accessories, and proportions follow approved model sheets.

## Environment Rendering

Locations must be designed as places that could be navigated repeatedly. Establish architecture, materials, weather, light sources, signage, roads, interiors, and recurring landmarks before relying on them across many panels.

## Power Rendering

Effects communicate mechanism and consequence. VFX must not imply abilities, scale, mastery, or system behavior beyond canon.

## AI Use

AI is a rendering and iteration tool inside the production pipeline. Prompts are implementation instructions, not creative authority. Human-directed selection, correction, composition, lettering, continuity checking, and final approval remain required.
