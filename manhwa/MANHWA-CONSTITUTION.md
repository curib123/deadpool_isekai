# Manhwa Constitution

**Series:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Author:** minuszeroo  
**Status:** LOCKED MANHWA FOUNDATION  
**Parent Canon:** repository novel canon

## 1. Authority

The manhwa is a derived adaptation of the canonical novel repository. It does not create a competing canon.

Authority order:

1. `series/SERIES-CONSTITUTION.md`
2. approved world, systems, character, mystery, destination, volume, chapter, scene, manuscript, continuity, and QA canon
3. this manhwa constitution and approved visual locks
4. approved storyboard and panel specifications
5. implementation prompts
6. generated images

A generated image, prompt, or storyboard may never silently override higher authority.

## 2. Adaptation Law

The manhwa may compress prose, split or combine visual beats, shorten dialogue without changing meaning, and replace narration with visible action when the same information is preserved.

It may not change outcomes, powers, knowledge states, character motives, mystery timing, injuries, consequences, identities, relationships, world rules, or chronology without an explicit canon revision upstream.

## 3. Originality Law

Red Jackal and all recurring designs must remain original. Broad genre inspirations may inform direction, but the production must not copy another character, franchise, living artist's signature style, costume, symbol, panel sequence, dialogue, lore, or recognizable design language.

## 4. Visual Lock Law

Every recurring character, creature, location, prop, weapon, costume, UI element, and persistent injury/state must have an approved visual specification before repeated production use.

Approved references guide generation. Random generations do not become canon automatically.

## 5. Continuity Law

A panel fails if it contradicts an approved visual lock without a canonical reason. Changes in face, body proportions, clothing, scars, equipment, scale, location geometry, lighting continuity, or injury state require correction before publication.

## 6. Format

Primary format: full-color vertical-scroll manhwa/webtoon.

The project may later create print/page adaptations, but vertical-scroll pacing is the production default.

## 7. Publication Gate

No episode is publishable until Adaptation, Continuity, Visual, Canon, and Final QA pass.
