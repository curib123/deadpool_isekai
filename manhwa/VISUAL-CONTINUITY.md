# Visual Continuity

## Continuity Record

For every recurring element maintain:

- Canon Fact
- Design Specification
- Approved Reference
- Allowed Variation
- Forbidden Variation
- Current Episode State
- Last Verified Appearance

## Character Check

Verify face, hair, eyes, skin, build, height relationship, costume, accessories, hands, scars, wounds, dirt/blood, props, and emotional state.

## Scene Check

Verify time of day, weather, light direction, location geometry, entrances/exits, distances, object placement, damage, crowd state, and character positions.

## Action Check

Verify handedness, weapon location, injury state, direction of motion, before/after damage, and whether the next panel can physically follow the previous panel.

## Color Check

Local lighting may alter perceived color, but canonical colors must remain recoverable. Do not treat lighting variation as a redesign.

## Drift Rule

If a generated panel conflicts with a lock, the generated panel is wrong. Regenerate, edit, composite, redraw, or reject it. Never weaken the lock merely to accept a convenient generation.
