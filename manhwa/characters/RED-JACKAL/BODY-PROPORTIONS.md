# Red Jackal — Body Proportions

**Status:** TEXTUAL BODY LOCK v1.0 — HEIGHT / IMAGE BOARD PENDING  
**Scope:** manhwa construction standard, not an in-world measurement sheet

# 1. Canon Constraint

Red Jackal is **lean-athletic rather than heavily muscular** and appears approximately 24.

The design should communicate:
- usable strength;
- mobility;
- quick recovery posture;
- adult proportions;
- no superhero-bodybuilder mass.

# 2. Manhwa Construction Standard

Use an adult figure approximately **7.5 heads tall** in neutral reference.

This ratio is intentionally established for visual consistency. It does **not** assign a canonical centimeter height.

## Relative Widths

Use these as ranges, not engineering measurements:

- shoulders: approximately 2.3–2.5 head-widths across;
- ribcage: athletic but narrower than shoulders;
- waist: clearly narrower than ribcage/shoulders without extreme V-taper;
- hips: natural adult male width, slightly narrower than shoulders.

# 3. Torso

- neck medium length and lean;
- trapezius visible but not bulky;
- shoulders rounded/functional rather than armored-looking;
- chest defined subtly;
- abdomen flat/conditioned rather than highly segmented at all times;
- torso length balanced against slightly long-looking legs.

Do not render him shirtless with exaggerated eight-pack anatomy unless a scene actually requires exposed anatomy.

# 4. Arms

- upper arms athletic but slim enough to preserve long line;
- forearms slightly more visually defined than biceps;
- elbows and wrists remain adult scale;
- hands neither tiny nor oversized.

Arm length in neutral pose:
- fingertips fall around upper-to-mid thigh, consistent with natural adult anatomy.

# 5. Hands

Hands are important for improvisation and acting.

Lock:
- long but believable fingers;
- visible knuckle structure at close range;
- no claw-like nails;
- no permanently gloved assumption—the costume determines glove state.

Handedness is not assigned here unless canon establishes it.

# 6. Legs

- legs read slightly long relative to torso;
- thighs athletic but not powerlifter-heavy;
- calves defined moderately;
- ankles remain lean;
- stance should support quick direction change.

Avoid fashion-model impossibly thin legs or massive combat thighs.

# 7. Shoulder / Waist Relationship

Primary silhouette:
- shoulders form the widest upper-body line;
- waist narrows cleanly;
- long coat must not artificially double shoulder width;
- costume layers should follow the body rather than hide its proportions.

# 8. Posture

## Safe / Neutral

- weight often settles slightly to one leg;
- pelvis and ribcage may counter-tilt subtly;
- shoulders relaxed;
- head often level or minimally tilted;
- hands loose rather than clenched.

## Alert

- weight becomes centered;
- knees unlock;
- shoulders remain low;
- head/eyes lead before the torso overreacts.

## Action

- movement becomes compact and practical;
- survival action should not default to poster poses;
- theatrical posing is a deliberate behavior, not his baseline fighting anatomy.

# 9. Relative Height Policy

**Exact height: PENDING.**

Reason:
The novel canon does not assign a number, and a useful final height should be locked against recurring cast relationships rather than chosen in isolation.

Until then:
- never portray him as unusually short or towering;
- use ordinary young-adult male scale relative to doors, roads, and other humans;
- maintain a stable relative size within an episode.

# 10. Damage / Regeneration Continuity

Injury changes:
- posture;
- guarding;
- weight distribution;
- range of motion;
- clothing damage.

Injury must not change:
- base limb length;
- shoulder width;
- face/body age;
- skeletal proportions.

Regeneration does not justify visual model drift.

# 11. Forbidden Drift

Reject/correct:
- bodybuilder bulk;
- giant shoulders;
- tiny waist superhero anatomy;
- teenage/child proportions;
- excessive height exaggeration;
- changing limb lengths;
- inconsistent hand scale;
- changing neck length;
- unexplained body mass change.

# 12. Image Board Requirement

First approved body board should show:
- front neutral;
- side neutral;
- back neutral;
- 3/4 neutral;
- relaxed weight-shift stance;
- alert stance;
- simple walking pose.

No dramatic lens distortion on the reference board.
