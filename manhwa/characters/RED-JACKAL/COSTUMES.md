# Red Jackal — Costumes

**Status:** TEXTUAL COSTUME LOCK v1.0 — IMAGE REFERENCES PENDING  
**Rule:** costume availability follows story continuity; design approval does not authorize early appearance

# 1. Costume Language

Red Jackal gravitates toward:
- charcoal and black;
- deep crimson lining or accents;
- long coats;
- layered belts;
- gloves;
- asymmetrical details;
- optional abstract jackal motif.

The visual identity should remain practical enough for a dangerous fantasy world. Avoid superhero bodysuits, tactical-military overload, excessive buckles, and large logo placement.

# 2. RJ-COSTUME-ARRIVAL-001 — Chapter 1 Arrival

**Story Use:** CH001 — `Wrong Forest, Wrong World`  
**Canon Anchor:** "dark fabric, fitted but flexible, nothing I remembered putting on before the crash."

This costume is intentionally simpler than the later signature persona design.

## Locked Manhwa Specification

### Upper
- charcoal-black fitted long-sleeve top;
- soft flexible fabric;
- close but not skin-tight;
- low-profile standing collar, short enough not to read as armor;
- subtle diagonal construction seam from character-left upper chest toward center torso;
- no chest emblem;
- no crimson outer panel;
- no armor plating.

### Lower
- dark flexible straight-taper trousers;
- mobility room at knees/hips;
- no cargo-pocket overload;
- minimal visible hardware.

### Footwear
- dark ankle boots;
- low practical sole;
- matte finish;
- no decorative metal shin armor.

### Accessories
- no mask;
- no visible jackal emblem;
- no signature long coat;
- no required gloves;
- no signature layered-belt persona arrangement.

## Purpose

The arrival outfit should make him look **wrongly dressed for his remembered Earth crash**, not already fully costumed as an established fantasy persona.

# 3. RJ-COSTUME-SIGNATURE-001 — Red Jackal Persona Baseline

**Story Use:** design-ready, but first canonical wear remains unassigned until continuity permits.

## Silhouette

- long charcoal coat ending around mid-thigh to just above knee;
- close upper-body fit with freer lower panels;
- rear split for movement;
- asymmetrical front overlap;
- no cape;
- no oversized shoulder pads.

## Collar

- medium-height structured-soft collar;
- can stand partially when dramatic, but does not frame the entire face;
- under-collar carries restrained deep-crimson fabric.

## Coat Construction

- outer shell: charcoal, matte or low-sheen;
- side/rear movement panels: near-black;
- inner facing/lining: deep crimson visible primarily during movement or open coat poses;
- character-left front hem extends slightly lower than character-right to create the recurring asymmetry;
- seams are functional-looking and sparse.

## Inner Layer

- near-black fitted shirt/tunic;
- simple neckline;
- no printed logo;
- no armor chest plate.

## Layered Belts

Two-belt maximum for the baseline design:

1. **Primary belt:** horizontal at natural waist; dark matte material; compact rectangular dark-metal buckle.
2. **Secondary belt:** narrower; shallow diagonal from character-right waist toward character-left hip.

The secondary belt should create an identifiable line without becoming a bandolier.

## Gloves

- fitted charcoal-black gloves;
- wrist length;
- flexible palm/finger construction;
- minimal reinforcement;
- no armored knuckle spikes;
- no red full-hand color blocking.

Optional small crimson stitch/tab may appear on **character-left glove only** as the recurring asymmetry.

## Trousers

- dark near-black;
- tapered;
- clean knee articulation;
- practical movement;
- minimal external pockets.

## Boots

- dark leather/composite appearance;
- above ankle, below mid-calf;
- low heel;
- front/side closure kept visually simple;
- no bright metal plates.

# 4. Crimson Accent Placement

Deep crimson is restricted to:
- coat inner facing/lining;
- under-collar;
- narrow selected seam/tab;
- optional character-left glove tab;
- optional motif application;
- mask slash/crack.

Forbidden by default:
- full red torso;
- red sleeves;
- red trousers;
- large red chest symbol;
- red/black superhero panel map.

# 5. Recurring Asymmetry

The baseline signature costume uses exactly these stable asymmetric anchors:

1. character-left coat hem slightly longer;
2. secondary belt slopes from character-right waist toward character-left hip;
3. optional small crimson glove/seam detail on character-left only.

Do not randomly move the asymmetry to the opposite side between panels.

# 6. Jackal Motif Placement

The abstract jackal mark is optional and small.

Preferred placements:
- interior coat tab;
- belt keeper;
- mask interior/edge;
- small personal token.

Avoid:
- giant chest logo;
- repeated pattern covering clothing;
- glowing magical emblem unless future canon establishes one.

# 7. Damage States

For every episode, record costume state:
- CLEAN;
- DAMP/MUDDY;
- TORN;
- BLOODED;
- REPAIRED/REPLACED.

Regeneration does not automatically repair clothing.

# 8. Costume Change Rule

A new outfit requires:
- story opportunity;
- new costume ID;
- updated clothing registry;
- front/back/3/4 reference;
- continuity start point.

Do not swap outfits because a generation produced a prettier design.
