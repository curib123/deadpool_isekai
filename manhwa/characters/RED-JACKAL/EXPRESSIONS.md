# Red Jackal — Expressions

**Status:** TEXTUAL EXPRESSION LOCK v1.0 — IMAGE SHEET PENDING  
**Face Base:** `FACE-REFERENCE.md`

Red Jackal must not collapse into one permanent smirk. His performance and genuine emotion are both canonically important.

# 1. Expression Anchors

## E01 — Neutral / Observant

- upper lids slightly relaxed;
- brows mostly level;
- mouth closed, near-horizontal;
- no visible teeth;
- reads attentive rather than bored.

## E02 — Faintly Amused

- one mouth corner lifts minimally;
- eyes narrow only slightly;
- brows remain relaxed;
- expression should still work in a serious scene without becoming comedy-face.

## E03 — Deadpan

- lids near neutral;
- brows flatten;
- mouth becomes fully level;
- head may tilt a few degrees while face remains emotionally economical.

## E04 — Mocking / Teasing

- one brow lifts;
- opposite brow stays neutral or lowers slightly;
- mouth corner lift more visible than E02;
- optional small open-mouth line when speaking;
- never a huge villain grin by default.

## E05 — Theatrical Grin

- controlled tooth visibility;
- cheeks lift;
- eyes remain focused rather than manic;
- use only when he is intentionally performing.

## E06 — Aura-Farming Confidence

- chin slightly raised;
- eyes calm and direct;
- mouth restrained—small confident line or faint smile;
- expression says "I meant to do that" even when staging is the joke.

## E07 — Suspicious / Evaluating

- lids narrow;
- brows draw inward slightly;
- mouth relaxes flat;
- eyes lead toward object/person before head turns fully.

## E08 — Focused / Dangerous

- brows lower;
- eyes fully engaged but not widened;
- jaw sets;
- mouth loses amusement;
- this is the "performance stops" face.

## E09 — Genuine Fear

- brows lift inward;
- eyes widen naturally, not chibi-wide;
- mouth parts;
- lower face tension visible;
- avoid comedic distortion unless scene explicitly calls for a reaction gag.

## E10 — Anger

- brows lower and pull inward;
- nostril/mouth tension increases;
- teeth may show only in stronger intensity;
- anger should not transform face proportions.

## E11 — Injured / Exhausted

- eyelids heavier;
- brow tension uneven;
- lips part for breath;
- neck/jaw tension and posture carry much of the read;
- dirt/blood/wounds tracked separately by continuity.

## E12 — Surprised / Caught Off Guard

- eyes widen moderately;
- brows lift;
- mouth opens briefly;
- recovery into control should be possible in the next beat.

## E13 — Emotionally Unguarded

- brows soften;
- eyes lose performative narrowing;
- mouth neutral or faintly downturned;
- no automatic tears;
- use sparingly for moments where the persona genuinely drops.

# 2. Reader-Facing Acting

Direct reader address does not require looking straight into camera every time.

Allowed:
- direct lens glance;
- slight side-eye toward reader;
- deadpan hold after a line;
- tiny brow lift;
- deliberately composed pose.

Avoid making every meta line a wink.

# 3. Comedy Distortion Limit

Temporary stylization may exaggerate timing, but:
- canonical face geometry must remain recoverable;
- serious injury cannot disappear for the gag;
- no permanent chibi design language;
- no facial deformation that would be mistaken for a power.

# 4. Generation Consistency

For all expressions preserve:
- eye shape family;
- iris color;
- jaw/chin proportions;
- nose length;
- hair groups H1–H6;
- apparent age.

# 5. Image Sheet Requirement

First expression sheet must contain at least E01–E13 using the same neutral lighting, camera distance, head angle family, hairstyle, and no mask.
