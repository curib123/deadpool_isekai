# Red Jackal — Face Reference

**Status:** TEXTUAL FACE LOCK v1.0 — IMAGE REFERENCE PENDING  
**Scope:** manhwa visual specification

# 1. Face Construction

## Overall Shape

- young adult male, approximately 24 in appearance;
- moderately narrow face, not gaunt;
- cheekbones are the widest point;
- sides of face taper gradually into the jaw;
- jaw is defined but not square/blocky;
- chin is compact with a softly flattened point rather than a sharp V.

Avoid both extremes:
- hyper-masculine brick jaw;
- delicate pointed "pretty-boy" jaw.

## Vertical Balance

Use natural adult facial thirds as the base:
- hairline/brow;
- brow/base of nose;
- base of nose/chin.

Minor stylization is allowed for manhwa readability, but the face must not visibly change age between panels.

# 2. Eyes

## Shape

- medium-narrow almond shape;
- upper lid carries more visual weight than lower lid;
- outer corner is near level or only slightly raised;
- lower lid remains relatively clean, not heavily outlined;
- eye size stays believable for an adult face.

## Spacing

Keep approximately one eye-width between the eyes in straight-on neutral reference.

## Iris

- deep crimson;
- circular human iris;
- normal dark pupil;
- normal sclera;
- no permanent glow;
- no slit pupil;
- no supernatural symbols.

Bright magical/environmental light may create reflections, but should not make the eye color read as neon red by default.

# 3. Eyebrows

- dark;
- medium thickness;
- mostly straight;
- slight natural taper toward the outer end;
- positioned low enough to support focused/deadpan expressions without looking permanently angry.

No eyebrow scar/notch is locked.

# 4. Nose

- straight bridge;
- moderate length;
- narrow-to-medium base;
- tip softly defined rather than pointed;
- nostrils understated in front view.

Do not exaggerate into a sharply hooked or tiny button nose.

# 5. Mouth

- medium width;
- upper lip thinner than lower lip;
- resting mouth is mostly horizontal with only minimal asymmetry;
- default amusement is created through a tiny corner shift or eye/brow acting, not a fixed grin.

Teeth should not be visible in neutral expression.

# 6. Ears

- ordinary adult proportions;
- top of ear roughly aligns with brow/upper-eye region;
- bottom roughly aligns near base-of-nose region;
- no pointed ears.

Hair may partially cover the upper ear depending on angle.

# 7. Hairline and Hair Mass

Hairline is natural and not dramatically low/high.

Stable hair groups for reproduction:

- **H1 — Crown Mass:** low, irregular crown volume; no vertical spikes.
- **H2 — Character-Right Forward Lock:** longest front lock; falls diagonally toward the right eyebrow/upper temple region without covering the eye completely.
- **H3 — Center Broken Fringe:** two shorter uneven groups breaking the forehead line.
- **H4 — Character-Left Open Fringe:** shorter sweep that leaves more of the left temple visible.
- **H5 — Side Masses:** soft layered pieces around upper ear level.
- **H6 — Upper-Nape Mass:** short irregular back edge ending around upper nape.

The six groups are silhouette anchors, not rigid individual strands.

# 8. Skin

Canon: light-to-medium complexion.

For visual production:
- use a natural neutral-to-warm undertone;
- avoid extreme porcelain white or heavily bronzed default;
- environmental lighting may shift apparent temperature;
- canonical complexion must remain recoverable under neutral light.

Exact color value remains pending approved color reference.

# 9. Neutral Reference Expression

The neutral face should read:
- observant;
- composed;
- slightly difficult to embarrass;
- capable of amusement without actively smiling.

Neutral construction:
- brows relaxed;
- upper lids slightly lowered from wide-alert position;
- mouth closed;
- one corner may sit imperceptibly higher, but never enough to become a permanent smirk.

# 10. Angle Consistency

At 3/4 and profile:
- nose length remains stable;
- chin projection remains compact;
- cheekbone remains the widest facial plane;
- jaw does not suddenly square out;
- eye size compresses naturally with perspective;
- hair groups H1–H6 preserve the same mass and direction.

# 11. Forbidden Drift

Reject/correct any generation that introduces without canon:
- different eye color;
- glowing red sclera;
- slit pupils;
- facial tattoos;
- scars;
- piercings;
- beard/stubble as a permanent feature;
- significantly longer hair;
- white/red hair streaks;
- childlike facial proportions;
- extreme square jaw;
- permanent grin.

# 12. Image Reference Requirement

The first approved face sheet should include:
- front neutral;
- front faint amusement;
- left profile;
- right profile;
- left 3/4;
- right 3/4;
- focused;
- afraid/unguarded.

Until that sheet is approved, this textual face lock is authoritative.
