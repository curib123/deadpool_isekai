# Red Jackal — Mask and Persona Props

**Status:** TEXTUAL MASK / MOTIF LOCK v1.0 — IMAGE REFERENCES PENDING  
**Canon:** optional pale mask with a single red slash/crack; prop/symbol, not magical requirement, not permanently worn

# 1. RJ-MASK-001 — Pale Persona Mask

## Overall Form

- full-face performance mask;
- human-face scale;
- matte pale bone/ivory tone;
- thin shell appearance;
- gently faceted planes rather than smooth superhero fabric;
- abstract jackal influence comes from cheek/temple geometry, not literal animal anatomy.

## Silhouette

- forehead follows human head shape;
- temples taper slightly;
- cheek planes narrow toward lower face;
- chin edge is compact;
- **no external jackal ears**;
- **no protruding animal muzzle**;
- **no horns**.

The mask should look intentionally designed by a theatrical, visual-minded person—not like a monster skull.

# 2. Eye Openings

- two narrow human-positioned apertures;
- enough opening to preserve crimson-eye glimpses at close range;
- no large black superhero eye patches;
- no painted white lenses;
- no permanently glowing eye sockets.

The apertures are approximately symmetrical. Asymmetry belongs to the red slash/crack rather than distorted eye geometry.

# 3. Mouth / Lower Face

- no painted grin;
- no teeth motif;
- no stitched-mouth motif;
- lower face remains a clean faceted surface with a minimal breathing gap/edge treatment if physically needed.

Do not make it resemble a comedy mask, slasher mask, skull, or armored helmet.

# 4. Single Crimson Slash / Crack

Lock one unmistakable mark:

- one irregular diagonal crimson fracture/slash;
- begins near the **wearer's upper-left temple/forehead edge**;
- travels diagonally downward across the mask;
- crosses toward center without perfectly bisecting the face;
- ends near the **wearer's lower-right cheek**;
- width varies slightly like a hand-made painted fracture;
- it is a single visual path, not multiple red stripes.

Default interpretation:
- painted/filled fracture motif or crafted surface mark;
- **not blood**;
- **not magical glow**;
- **not a wound on Red Jackal's face**.

# 5. Surface

- matte;
- very low ornament;
- minor hand-made irregularity is allowed;
- no ornate gold trim;
- no engraved magic runes by default.

Exact material remains visually pending; do not assign rare lore material without canon.

# 6. Attachment

- dark charcoal strap/hidden fastening behind head;
- attachment should be physically plausible;
- hair remains visible around upper/back edges;
- no hood is required.

# 7. Jackal Motif — RJ-MOTIF-001

Independent of the mask, the persona may use an abstract jackal-head mark.

Construction:
- three primary angular strokes;
- two upper diverging strokes imply ears;
- one lower converging stroke implies muzzle direction;
- open negative space in center;
- no circle around it;
- no skull;
- no realistic animal face.

Use small scale only unless future art direction explicitly approves a larger application.

# 8. Prop Continuity

When mask is present track:
- carried;
- worn;
- removed;
- dropped;
- cracked/damaged;
- lost/recovered.

Do not make it appear/disappear between panels because the generator forgot it.

# 9. Originality Guard

Reject designs that resemble:
- Deadpool mask geometry/eye treatment;
- literal Anubis/jackal animal helmets;
- recognizable anime organization masks;
- famous slasher masks;
- comedy/tragedy theater masks;
- existing superhero emblems.
