# Red Jackal — Mask and Personal Props

## Optional Mask

Canon permits an optional pale mask marked by a single red slash/crack.

The mask is:

- a prop and symbol;
- not a magical requirement;
- not permanently worn.

Before repeated use approve front, profile, 3/4, inside/strap logic, material, damage states, and exact red-mark geometry.

## Jackal Motif

A stylized jackal-head motif may appear when Red Jackal deliberately creates a persona. It must be an original project design and not imitate an existing franchise emblem.
