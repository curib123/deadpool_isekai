# 1. Canon Identity

- **Chosen Name:** Red Jackal
- **Earth Name:** Evan Calder
- **Apparent Age:** approximately 24
- **Build:** lean-athletic, never bodybuilder-heavy
- **Hair:** black; naturally untidy
- **Eyes:** deep crimson after arrival in Veyr
- **Skin:** light-to-medium complexion
- **Default Read:** relaxed, observant, faintly amused
- **Movement Read:** loose and unhurried until action becomes necessary

# 2. Visual Thesis

Red Jackal should read as **controlled asymmetry + understated theatricality + dangerous improvisation**.

He is not designed as a conventional armored hero, a masked superhero, or a permanently grinning trickster. His silhouette should remain recognizably human and practical. The theatricality comes from posture, coat movement, restrained crimson accents, deliberate framing, and occasional persona props rather than from an overloaded costume.

The design must remain readable in:
- close-up acting;
- medium dialogue shots;
- full-body action;
- silhouette;
- low-detail distant panels.

# 3. Primary Recognition Anchors

These are the fastest identity checks for repeated generation:

1. **Hair silhouette:** black layered hair with an intentionally uneven front fringe; the character's right side carries the slightly longer forward lock while the opposite temple remains more open.
2. **Eyes:** normal human sclera with deep-crimson irises; not permanently glowing.
3. **Face:** young-adult, moderately narrow face; defined cheek structure; tapered but not delicate jaw; restrained mouth line.
4. **Build:** lean-athletic with functional shoulders and long clean limb lines; no exaggerated chest or arm mass.
5. **Posture:** relaxed center of gravity, often weight settled slightly to one leg when safe.
6. **Signature clothing language:** charcoal/black base, long coat when canonically worn, layered belts, fitted gloves, asymmetrical details.
7. **Crimson usage:** accent color only—inner coat facing/lining, under-collar, selected small construction details; never a large superhero-like red body panel.
8. **Persona motif:** abstract jackal mark or optional pale mask; never required for normal recognition.

# 4. Head and Face Summary

Use `FACE-REFERENCE.md` for detailed construction.

At normal neutral view:
- forehead is neither unusually tall nor compressed;
- cheekbones form the widest facial region;
- jaw narrows cleanly toward the chin without becoming pointed;
- nose bridge is straight and moderate;
- eyes are medium-narrow almond shapes rather than oversized anime eyes;
- brows are mostly straight with a slight natural outer descent;
- mouth is medium width with a restrained resting line.

His default "amused" read comes from subtle eye/brow/mouth tension, not a permanent smirk.

# 5. Hair Summary

Hair is black with soft-to-medium textured strands and a stable mass.

Lock the hair as:
- layered;
- medium-short;
- covering the upper ears only partially depending on angle;
- back mass ending around the upper nape rather than forming long hair;
- irregular fringe, never a perfectly symmetrical center part;
- one longer forward lock on the character's right forehead;
- two smaller broken fringe groups toward center;
- character's left temple more exposed.

Do not turn the hair into:
- tall spikes;
- a long mullet;
- slicked-back hair;
- a bowl cut;
- a symmetric K-pop center curtain;
- white/colored streaks unless future canon explicitly changes it.

# 6. Body Summary

Use `BODY-PROPORTIONS.md`.

The manhwa reference proportion is an adult **approximately 7.5-head figure**. This is a visual construction standard, not an in-world height statement.

Key read:
- shoulders athletic but not broad enough to dominate silhouette;
- chest defined through posture rather than bulk;
- waist visibly narrower than shoulders;
- hips natural, not exaggerated;
- legs slightly longer in visual read than torso;
- hands adult and capable, not oversized weapons-hands.

**Exact in-world height remains intentionally unassigned** until a cast height chart is canonically useful.

# 7. Costume Families

Use `COSTUMES.md`.

Two visual states must remain separate:

### RJ-COSTUME-ARRIVAL-001
Chapter 1 arrival state. Canon only guarantees unfamiliar dark, fitted, flexible clothing. The manhwa design stays simple and does **not** force the later signature long coat, mask, gloves, or persona motif into the awakening scene.

### RJ-COSTUME-SIGNATURE-001
Approved manhwa signature-persona design language for later use when story continuity permits: charcoal/black long coat, controlled crimson interior/accent treatment, layered belts, fitted gloves, asymmetrical construction.

A costume design existing in this file does not authorize it to appear earlier than canon allows.

# 8. Jackal Motif

The project jackal motif is abstract rather than literal.

Core construction:
- three primary angular strokes;
- two upper strokes imply ears;
- one lower converging stroke implies muzzle direction;
- no skull;
- no circular superhero badge;
- no snarling animal illustration;
- no copied franchise emblem.

It is a persona mark, not a magical rune and not mandatory on every outfit.

# 9. Mask Summary

Use `MASK-PROPS.md`.

The optional mask is:
- pale;
- matte;
- full-face performance prop;
- subtly faceted;
- abstractly jackal-influenced without external ears or a protruding animal snout;
- marked by one crimson slash/crack;
- non-magical by default;
- not permanently worn.

# 10. Color Hierarchy

Exact production color values remain pending image calibration.

Relative hierarchy is locked:
1. near-black / charcoal — dominant;
2. natural skin — face/hands;
3. deep crimson — restrained identity accent;
4. pale mask — optional high-contrast prop;
5. neutral dark metal/leather — support materials.

Crimson must remain visually meaningful. Do not spread it across half the costume.

# 11. Originality Guard

Do not visually copy or converge on:
- Deadpool;
- The Mask;
- an existing superhero costume;
- an existing anime/manhwa/manga protagonist;
- a living artist's signature style;
- recognizable masks, logos, weapon layouts, chest emblems, eye surrounds, or suit paneling from another franchise.

Red Jackal must still be identifiable if the mask and jackal motif are removed.
