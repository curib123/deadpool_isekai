# Red Jackal — Model Sheet

**Canon Source:** `characters/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md`  
**Status:** DESIGN SPECIFICATION — image references pending

## Identity

- Chosen name: Red Jackal
- Earth name: Evan Calder
- apparent age: approximately 24
- build: lean-athletic, not heavily muscular
- hair: black, usually untidy enough to look intentional
- eyes: deep crimson after arrival in Veyr
- skin: light-to-medium complexion
- default expression tendency: amused even while evaluating danger
- movement: relaxed until action is necessary

## Signature Visual Language

- charcoal and black clothing;
- deep crimson lining or accents;
- long coats;
- layered belts;
- gloves;
- asymmetrical details;
- stylized jackal-head motif when deliberately constructing the persona;
- optional pale mask marked by a single red slash/crack.

## Non-Negotiable

Do not make him visually derivative of Deadpool, The Mask, or a recognizable existing manhwa/manga protagonist. No copied suit geometry, logos, masks, weapon layout, facial design, or signature costume language.
