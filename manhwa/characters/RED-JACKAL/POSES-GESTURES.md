# Red Jackal — Poses and Gestures

**Status:** TEXTUAL MOVEMENT LOCK v1.0 — IMAGE POSE SHEET PENDING

# 1. Movement Thesis

Red Jackal's physical language is built on contrast:

**safe = relaxed economy**  
**danger = practical compression**  
**performance = deliberate theatrical expansion**

He should not look like he is posing heroically at all times.

# 2. Neutral Standing

## P01 — Relaxed Neutral

- weight slightly favoring one leg;
- shoulders low;
- arms loose;
- head level or lightly tilted;
- torso open but not chest-puffed.

## P02 — Waiting / Unbothered

- one hand may rest near belt/coat edge or pocket if costume supports it;
- other hand loose;
- minimal movement;
- stillness is the point.

Do not default to crossed arms in every waiting scene.

# 3. Walking

- stride natural, not runway-long;
- shoulders remain relaxed;
- eyes scan before body fully turns;
- coat movement follows actual stride/wind;
- hands do not remain clenched.

# 4. Alert / Survival

## P03 — Alert

- weight centered;
- knees unlocked;
- feet stagger slightly;
- elbows free;
- chin lowers marginally;
- eyes lead.

## P04 — Improvised Defense

- body protects vulnerable line;
- nearest available object/terrain determines stance;
- posture may be messy;
- do not turn inexperienced early Red Jackal into a polished martial artist.

# 5. Action

Early action should read:
- quick;
- adaptive;
- occasionally awkward;
- survival-first.

Later competence may evolve through canon, but reference art must not pre-authorize mastery.

Avoid default "superhero landing" crouches.

# 6. Theatrical / Aura Farming

## P05 — Deliberate Entrance

- controlled stride;
- coat silhouette allowed to open;
- chin and shoulder line composed;
- hands calm.

## P06 — Manufactured Coolness

- uses environment deliberately;
- may lean/sit where the silhouette looks good;
- pauses long enough to create a visual beat;
- should feel self-aware rather than naturally godlike.

## P07 — Failed Aura Beat

- pose begins composed;
- mundane interruption breaks it;
- face returns to deadpan/annoyed;
- body language should preserve comedy without becoming slapstick unless scene supports it.

# 7. Reader-Facing Gestures

Allowed:
- direct glance;
- one open-hand "you see this?" gesture;
- small shrug;
- finger point used sparingly;
- head turn toward reader while body remains engaged with scene.

Avoid:
- constant finger-guns;
- repeated winking;
- exaggerated vaudeville gestures in every chapter.

# 8. Fear / Unguarded Movement

When genuinely afraid:
- shoulders may rise;
- elbows pull closer;
- steps shorten;
- balance becomes reactive;
- breath and tension show physically.

He can still joke while frightened. The body should reveal truth when the prose says the performance is failing.

# 9. Injury Movement

Track:
- guarded side;
- reduced reach;
- limp or weight shift;
- hand protecting wound;
- breathing/postural compensation.

Regeneration changes these progressively, not instantaneously.

# 10. Reference Pose Sheet

Required first pose sheet:
- P01 relaxed neutral;
- P02 waiting;
- normal walk;
- P03 alert;
- P04 improvised defense;
- P05 deliberate entrance;
- P06 manufactured coolness;
- genuine fear;
- injured movement.

Use neutral perspective for reference images; cinematic foreshortening belongs in story panels, not model calibration.
