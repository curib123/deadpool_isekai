# Red Jackal — Poses and Gestures

## Movement Language

- relaxed posture until action is necessary;
- comfortable stillness when others expect panic;
- deliberate theatrical posing when aura farming;
- efficient emergency movement when survival interrupts performance.

## Required Reference Families

- standing/resting;
- walking;
- running;
- crouching;
- falling/recovering;
- defensive improvisation;
- close combat;
- injured movement;
- reader-facing/meta beat;
- theatrical entrance/exit.

Theatrical poses must not replace physical cause-and-effect during serious action.
