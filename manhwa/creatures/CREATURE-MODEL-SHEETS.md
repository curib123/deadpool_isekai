# Creature Model Sheets

For every recurring creature record:

- canon source;
- silhouette;
- anatomy;
- locomotion;
- scale;
- skin/fur/scales;
- head/mouth;
- limbs;
- sensory features;
- damage states;
- behavior;
- approved views;
- allowed variation;
- forbidden variation.

A creature's visual design must not imply abilities absent from canon.
