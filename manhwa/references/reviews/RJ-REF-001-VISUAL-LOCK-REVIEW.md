### HARD FAIL — Secondary Belt Direction

Text lock requires:

**character-right waist → character-left hip**

In a front view:
- character-right = viewer-left;
- character-left = viewer-right.

So the secondary belt must begin higher on viewer-left and descend toward viewer-right.

The candidate does the opposite. It begins higher on viewer-right / character-left and descends toward viewer-left / character-right hip. The secondary buckle/low point is therefore on the wrong side.

This is a forbidden side-flip under `VISUAL-LOCK.md`.

### Required correction
For RJ-REF-001-R2:
1. start the secondary belt at character-right waist;
2. slope it down toward character-left hip;
3. place the secondary buckle / low point on character-left;
4. keep that direction identical in front, 3/4, and later turnaround views.

Do not alter the already-correct hair asymmetry or coat-hem asymmetry.
