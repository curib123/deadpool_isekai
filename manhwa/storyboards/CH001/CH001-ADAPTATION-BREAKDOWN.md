# CH001 Adaptation Breakdown — Wrong Forest, Wrong World

**Source:** `manuscript/CH001-WRONG-FOREST-WRONG-WORLD.md`  
**Status:** FOUNDATION CREATED — storyboard not yet approved

## Episode Purpose

Introduce Red Jackal after the rain-night collision, establish the unfamiliar forest and altered body, show his first dangerous encounter, first uncontrolled improvised manifestation, abnormal recovery, and movement toward civilization without prematurely explaining the world.

## Locked Major Beats

1. collision memory / breathing;
2. alien forest awakening;
3. body and clothing feel wrong;
4. no phone / no familiar infrastructure;
5. old road and evidence of civilization;
6. fresh large tracks;
7. predator encounter;
8. Red Jackal is injured and genuinely afraid;
9. desperate need for a barrier;
10. crude slab/shield manifests;
11. creature retreats;
12. manifestation dissolves and cannot be repeated;
13. wound visibly repairs faster than normal;
14. Red Jackal admits fear;
15. maintained road/sign indicates settlement;
16. he chooses civilization.

## Compression Rule

Internal reasoning may be shortened only when acting, panel staging, or narration preserves the same uncertainty and character voice. Do not make Red Jackal instantly know he is in an isekai.

## Reveal Locks

Do not explain World System truth, Play Logic terminology/mechanism, Great Design, Fate truth, anomaly origin, or regeneration origin.
