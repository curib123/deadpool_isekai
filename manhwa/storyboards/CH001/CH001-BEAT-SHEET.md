Predator leaves → object vanishes → failed attempt to repeat → wound repairs too fast and painfully → fear admitted without performance.

## Sequence G — Forward
Old road improves → maintained post/sign → settlement direction → he chooses the road toward people.

Final scroll beat should point forward rather than explain the mystery.
