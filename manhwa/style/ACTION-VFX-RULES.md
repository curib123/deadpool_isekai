# Action and VFX Rules

## Action

Show setup, action, contact/result, and consequence clearly enough that the reader understands what happened.

## VFX

Effects must follow systems canon. They may visualize force, magic, Play Logic, impact, regeneration, or environmental phenomena only at the scale and mastery currently established.

## Damage

Do not cosmetically erase wounds, fatigue, destroyed objects, blood, or environmental damage before canon allows recovery or repair.
