# Camera and Cinematography

## Shot Grammar

Use establishing, wide, medium, close, insert, reaction, POV, over-shoulder, low/high angle, and controlled extreme perspective according to story purpose.

## Rules

- establish geography before complex action;
- do not cross spatial logic casually;
- close-ups must earn emphasis;
- low angles do not automatically mean power;
- reader-facing Red Jackal shots should remain intentional;
- maintain eyelines and screen direction through sequences.

Camera choices may dramatize canon, never rewrite it.
