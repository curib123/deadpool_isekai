# Color and Lighting Rules

## Principles

- local environment establishes palette;
- character canonical colors survive lighting shifts;
- light sources must have physical direction and continuity;
- saturation is selective;
- crimson Red Jackal accents should remain controlled rather than flooding every scene.

## Sequence Continuity

Record time, weather, key light direction, practical light sources, ambient color, and major transitions in the shot list.

## Dramatic Lighting

May intensify danger, mystery, comedy, or reveals, but must not create false supernatural information.
