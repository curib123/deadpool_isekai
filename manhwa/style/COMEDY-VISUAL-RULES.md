# Comedy Visual Rules

Comedy may come from timing, Red Jackal's expression, framing, reaction shots, understatement, awkward silence, failed theatrics, or contrast between his performance and reality.

Do not:

- chibi-reset serious injury unless explicitly presented as a non-canonical visual aside;
- make bystanders forget death or trauma for a gag;
- convert every dangerous scene into slapstick;
- repeat a visual joke until it weakens character tension.

The world remains consequential even when Red Jackal is funny.
