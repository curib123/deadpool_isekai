# Line and Shading Rules

- prioritize readable contours at mobile scale;
- vary detail by shot distance;
- keep facial features stable under simplification;
- avoid noisy texture that destroys silhouettes;
- use shadow groups to describe form and mood, not hide inconsistent anatomy;
- preserve material differences between skin, cloth, leather, metal, stone, vegetation, and VFX.

Rendering may evolve only through an explicit art-bible revision, not panel-by-panel drift.
