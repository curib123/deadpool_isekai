# Original Art Direction

## Direction

Original full-color dark-fantasy vertical manhwa with clean readable silhouettes, expressive acting, grounded material detail, selective cinematic framing, controlled atmospheric depth, and strong contrast between serious world consequences and Red Jackal's theatrical behavior.

## Do

- build project-specific shape language;
- derive costumes from Veyr cultures and character function;
- use recurring visual motifs intentionally;
- make locations identifiable by architecture and materials;
- reserve extreme spectacle for earned moments.

## Do Not

- request or imitate a living artist's exact style;
- clone a named manhwa/manga;
- copy recognizable costumes, masks, emblems, compositions, or VFX signatures;
- let every panel become a poster shot.

The project should eventually be recognizable as itself even without the title.
