# Vertical Scroll Pacing

Whitespace is a timing tool.

Use longer gaps for:

- suspense;
- scale reveals;
- emotional hesitation;
- sudden interruption;
- horror;
- comedic beat delay.

Use tighter sequences for:

- rapid dialogue;
- pursuit;
- physical exchanges;
- escalating reactions.

Do not create empty scrolling without narrative purpose. Episode rhythm must be tested on a phone-sized viewport before final approval.
