# Greywake Architecture

## Canon Anchors

- frontier construction;
- timber palisades;
- lantern bridges;
- older half-abandoned stoneworks;
- practical Valic frontier culture.

## Design Rules

Newer construction should visibly prioritize repairability, defense, storage, weather resistance, traffic, and local materials. Older stoneworks must read as materially and historically distinct from the settlement's newer layers.

## Lock Before Production

Define approved examples for:

- gate;
- wall/palisade;
- ordinary exterior;
- civic/guild exterior;
- ordinary interior;
- storage/work structure;
- bridge;
- surviving old stonework.

No single generated building becomes the architectural standard without approval.
