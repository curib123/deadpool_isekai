# Greywake Color and Atmosphere

## Canon Anchors

River fog, wet/muddy roads, timber defenses, lantern bridges, frontier activity, and older stoneworks should inform atmosphere.

## Rules

- weather and time-of-day drive palette;
- lantern warmth may contrast with fog/cool exterior conditions when physically justified;
- mud, wet timber, stone, cloth, metal, and vegetation should remain materially distinct;
- atmosphere must not obscure important geography or acting.

Exact palettes remain pending until visual development is approved.
