## Locked Identity

Greywake is a Valic frontier settlement in the Hollow March and the opening major destination.

Canonical visual anchors:

- timber palisades;
- river fog;
- lantern bridges;
- guild boards;
- muddy monster roads;
- half-abandoned stoneworks older than the settlement;
- practical frontier bureaucracy;
- visible dependence on roads, caravans, guild work, militia/wardens, river trade, and survival infrastructure.

## Design Principle

Greywake should look built by people solving frontier problems, not like a generic fantasy capital. Materials, repair, mud, weathering, old stoneworks, lighting, signage, storage, and defensive infrastructure should communicate function.
