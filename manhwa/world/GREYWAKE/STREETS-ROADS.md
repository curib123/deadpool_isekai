# Greywake Streets and Roads

Canonical anchors include muddy monster roads, caravan movement, old/abandoned infrastructure, maintained routes, and frontier travel hazards.

For every recurring route record:

- road width;
- material;
- drainage;
- wheel/hoof/foot traffic;
- maintenance state;
- signage;
- nearby defenses;
- vegetation;
- weather effects;
- connection points.

Road damage and repairs persist until continuity permits change.
