# Manuscript Writing Rules

**Owners:** A090-A097

## First-Person Storytelling
Red Jackal personally tells the story. There is no separate invisible narrator. Ask: **How would Red Jackal tell us this happened?**

## Reader-Talk
Direct reader conversation exists from Chapter 1 onward. Do not use an obsolete progression where reader-talk evolves from imaginary audience → habit → discovery.

## Self-Talk
He may argue with himself while processing immediate events. Keep this distinct from reader commentary and quoted spoken dialogue.

## Paragraphs
Do not put every sentence on its own line. Normal prose should use natural multi-sentence paragraphs. Single-line paragraphs are for genuine impact, interruption, revelation, emotional emphasis, or punchlines.

## Dialogue
Use quotation marks. Give characters distinct speech patterns. Allow interruption, hesitation, misunderstanding, disagreement, ignored jokes, and subtext. Avoid dialogue used only to dump lore.

## Comedy
Do not impose joke quotas. Humor comes from Red Jackal's genuine reaction, situation, misunderstandings, self-talk, reader-talk, power misuse, social differences, or physical comedy. Serious chapters may contain almost none.

## Serious Consequences
Red Jackal may be unserious; the world is not. Death, trauma, destruction, political consequences, injuries, and relationship damage remain real.

## Pop-Culture References
References may be brief, transformed, relevant, understandable without source knowledge, and occasional. Never reproduce substantial copyrighted dialogue, lyrics, scenes, character designs, or franchise lore.

## Action
Maintain spatial clarity, ability logic, injuries, tactics, environment use, counters, and aftermath. Regeneration/power must not erase stakes.

## Play Logic Scenes
Creative manifestations should reveal Red Jackal's personality and current intent. Avoid repetitive stronger/bigger versions of the same attack.

## Prose Target
Natural, professional, human-feeling light-novel prose: readable, character-focused, emotionally grounded, descriptive without purple prose, cinematic only when earned. Avoid robotic sequencing, constant fragments, repetitive AI phrasing, and over-explanation.
