# 1. Mystery Governance

## 1.1 Truth Layers

Every mystery distinguishes:

- **Writer Truth** — objective production canon.
- **Reader Baseline** — what the reader can reasonably infer at the current reveal stage.
- **Luck Knowledge** — what Luck actually knows or believes.
- **Character/Faction Knowledge** — what another in-world actor can support from evidence.
- **False Beliefs** — plausible but incorrect explanations.
- **Reveal Window** — earliest structural window for explicit confirmation.
- **Payoff** — what changes when the truth becomes usable.

Writer truth is not automatically narration truth.

## 1.2 Spoiler Firewall

Do not reveal writer truth through:
- narration before Luck has evidence;
- dialogue from characters without a knowledge path;
- local antagonists merely because the author knows the answer;
- convenient exposition;
- a System window that suddenly explains an anomaly the System cannot classify.

## 1.3 Foreshadowing State

F-IDs are planning handles.

Use:
- **PLANNED** — approved but not yet planted.
- **PLANTED** — visible in manuscript canon.
- **REINFORCED** — repeated through a different kind of evidence.
- **PAID** — its intended mystery function has resolved.
- **RETIRED** — old concept retained only for ID/history continuity.

## 1.4 Reveal Windows

- **RW-0 — Baseline:** observable anomaly only.
- **RW-1 — Pattern:** repeated evidence proves the anomaly is consistent.
- **RW-2 — Historical Resonance:** older records/ruins show partial analogues.
- **RW-3 — External-Origin Evidence:** cross-world/Soul-Drift interpretation becomes supportable.
- **RW-4 — Exploitation:** factions attempt to use or reproduce the anomaly.
- **RW-5 — Endgame:** final causality and identity-choice payoff.

These windows do not define a fixed volume count.

# 2. Core Writer Truth

## 2.1 Nobody Deliberately Chose Luck

The person who later chooses the name **Luck Everhart** dies during a mysterious Earth explosion.

No god, Veyran ritual, Savael, Exterior Needle, World System, Fate mechanism, or Great Design deliberately selects or summons him.

His arrival results from a rare natural **far Soul Drift** during an unusual cross-reality boundary condition.

Luck does not know this at the beginning.

## 2.2 The Earth Explosion

The explosion is a real pre-Veyr event.

Its exact cause remains protected long-horizon mystery material.

The current canon does **not** require the protagonist's lost Earth name to be fixed or revealed early. **Luck Everhart** is the Veyran identity he chooses for himself in CH001.

## 2.3 Soul Drift

Soul Drift is continuity transfer after death.

Ordinary Veyran reincarnation routes can preserve enough local relation for later registry recognition.

Luck's far drift crosses realities and arrives without:
- native birth;
- local lineage;
- valid world-registry predecessor;
- recognized soul-registry relation;
- native progression root;
- pre-arrival Fate history.

That absence explains why a physical person can exist while the World System cannot construct a normal identity record.

## 2.4 Why Luck Thinks the Way He Does

Luck retains practical cognition, humor, social instincts, fragments of modern knowledge, and a strong habit of theatrical presentation.

His love of:
- entrances;
- pauses;
- ominous lines;
- appearing more informed than he is;
- treating awkward situations like scenes worth staging;

is personality, not a supernatural reader connection.

No external audience powers him.

Veyran time does not stop for his jokes, asides, or performance.

## 2.5 Why His Body Exists

The physical/soul layer can instantiate a viable body around a real incoming soul even when the later World System has no valid identity history to register.

Luck's body is real, humanlike, measurable, vulnerable, and mortal.

## 2.6 Why the System Rejects Him

The System cannot invent a legitimate past merely because Luck is physically present.

Therefore institutional queries may return:
- UNDEFINED;
- NO RECORD;
- UNAVAILABLE;
- FAILED;
- ANOMALY.

Physical measurement can still work because mass, temperature, injury, material presence, and mana interaction do not require a valid identity field.

## 2.7 Fortune Distortion

Luck has exactly one supernatural ability:

**Fortune Distortion.**

It passively biases already-possible outcomes around situations in which Luck is causally involved.

It can influence:
- timing;
- footing;
- aim;
- mechanical failure;
- route choice;
- interruptions;
- discovery;
- who arrives first;
- which existing defect fails;
- social misinterpretation;
- chains of individually possible events.

It cannot:
- create matter;
- rewrite reality directly;
- stop or rewind time;
- restore destroyed tissue;
- resurrect Luck;
- guarantee victory;
- make an impossible physical mechanism possible;
- let Luck consciously select an exact outcome.

Luck never correctly identifies Fortune Distortion as his own supernatural ability, including at the end of the series.

## 2.8 Mortality

Luck does not regenerate supernaturally.

If he is cut, the wound remains until:
- ordinary recovery;
- established Veyran medicine;
- legitimate healing magic.

Fortune Distortion may reduce the severity of a hit or create a lucky escape, but it cannot erase damage after it exists.

This distinction is protected canon because many witnesses may misread repeated survival as immortality, hidden healing, or invulnerability.

## 2.9 Fate and Fortune Distortion

Fate is a native convergence structure.

Fortune Distortion is a local probability anomaly around Luck's causal involvement.

Luck has no native pre-arrival Fate history, but Fate can reroute future convergences around facts created after his arrival.

Fortune Distortion does not make Luck immune to Fate and does not let him command it.

## 2.10 The Eidrathi Continuance

The pre-System Eidrathi Continuance studied:
- soul continuity;
- world boundaries;
- Fate convergence;
- reality stabilization;
- early registry structures.

Their work provides historical evidence that external causality and registry exceptions are theoretically possible.

They did not cause Luck's arrival.

## 2.11 The Exterior Needle

The Exterior Needle is an ancient Eidrathi boundary instrument associated with the First Severance.

It is an artificial analogue for cross-boundary manipulation.

It did **not** import Luck.

Later experts may falsely assume it explains him because it is the nearest known historical comparison.

## 2.12 Savael-of-the-Index

Savael is a surviving Eidrathi-derived archivist intelligence.

Savael:
- did not cause the Earth explosion;
- did not choose Luck;
- did not summon him;
- did not grant Fortune Distortion;
- does not control the World System.

Savael may become interested only after evidence of a genuine external anomaly appears.

Possible motives include:
- study;
- proof;
- exploitation;
- reproducing far-drift access;
- using Luck as leverage in boundary research.

## 2.13 The Great Design

The Great Design is a non-personal deep coherence architecture.

It is not:
- a speaking author;
- a god choosing Luck;
- a summoner;
- a personal strategist.

It reacts to consequences rather than scripting Luck's individual choices.

## 2.14 Quiet Registration

Quiet Registration is the historical convergence of registry practices into the reliable modern World System.

Its history can explain why Luck's failure is not an ordinary damaged status interface.

## 2.15 Endgame Boundary

Luck is not required to become:
- normally registered;
- chosen;
- cosmically assimilated;
- morally purified;
- consciously aware of Fortune Distortion.

A practical legal/social identity may become possible without solving the metaphysical registry problem.

# 3. Mystery Registry

## M-001 — Who Brought Luck Everhart to Veyr?

**Writer Truth:** Nobody deliberately brought him. Natural far Soul Drift followed the Earth explosion.  
**Reader Baseline:** he wakes in Veyr after white-light/explosion memory.  
**Luck Baseline:** no one has claimed responsibility; he has no reliable explanation.  
**False Beliefs:** divine summoning, chosen hero, System accident, Savael importation, Exterior Needle selection.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-2 to RW-4.  
**Payoff:** shifts the question from “who chose him?” to “how was a natural crossing possible?”

## M-002 — Why Is Everything About Luck UNDEFINED?

**Writer Truth:** no valid native registry/soul/Fate/progression root exists from which the System can construct normal identity fields.  
**Reader Baseline:** independent interfaces repeatedly fail.  
**Luck Baseline:** Veyr cannot classify him normally.  
**False Beliefs:** hidden max level, anti-appraisal skill, secret class, divine concealment.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-1 to RW-3.  
**Payoff:** UNDEFINED means no valid answer exists, not a hidden larger number.

## M-003 — Was Luck Reincarnated Normally?

**Writer Truth:** no. He crossed realities through far Soul Drift after death.  
**Luck Baseline:** may casually use reincarnation/isekai language without knowing the mechanism.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-2 to RW-3.  
**Payoff:** explains damaged autobiographical memory and registry absence.

## M-004 — What Is Fortune Distortion?

**Writer Truth:** passive probability bias around Luck's causal involvement; his only supernatural power.  
**Reader Baseline:** individually possible coincidences occur with abnormal density.  
**Luck Baseline:** good timing, bad maintenance, enemy mistakes, useful improvisation, coincidence.  
**Forbidden conclusion:** Luck never correctly accepts it as his personal supernatural power.  
**Reveal Window:** reader pattern may be clear from RW-0; in-world mechanism remains limited/protected.

## M-005 — Why Does Luck Survive So Much Without Being Immortal?

**Writer Truth:** Fortune Distortion often interrupts lethal chains before they resolve, but it does not heal existing damage or guarantee survival.  
**Reader Baseline:** Luck can be hurt and treated normally.  
**Luck Baseline:** he knows survival has often been improbably close.  
**False Beliefs:** immortality, regeneration, divine protection, hidden defensive class.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-1 onward through accumulated evidence.  
**Payoff:** preserves real physical stakes.

## M-006 — Why Does Luck Act Like Life Is a Performance?

**Writer Truth:** stable personality plus surviving cognitive/social habits. No external audience mechanism exists.  
**Reader Baseline:** he enjoys staging entrances and lines.  
**Luck Baseline:** he knows he is performing socially.  
**False Beliefs:** prophetic ritual, supernatural aura, invisible familiar, time magic.  
**Reveal Window:** effectively visible from the opening; no cosmic payoff required.

## M-007 — How Does Fortune Distortion Interact With Fate?

**Writer Truth:** separate systems. Fate reroutes future convergence; Fortune Distortion biases local possible outcomes.  
**Luck Baseline:** no technical knowledge.  
**False Beliefs:** Fate immunity, prophecy control, divine favor, perfect foresight.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-2 to RW-4.

## M-008 — What Is the Great Design?

**Writer Truth:** non-personal coherence architecture beneath ordinary System/Fate behavior.  
**False Beliefs:** supreme god, literal author, Luck's summoner.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-3 to RW-5.

## M-009 — What Happened Before the First Severance?

**Writer Truth:** Eidrathi boundary research caused a historical world-boundary disaster; the Exterior Needle is associated with that work.  
**Critical boundary:** it did not cause Luck's arrival.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-2 to RW-4.

## M-010 — Who Is Savael-of-the-Index?

**Writer Truth:** surviving Eidrathi archivist intelligence that becomes interested after Luck's arrival.  
**Critical boundary:** Savael is exploiter/researcher, not creator/summoner.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-3 to RW-4.

## M-011 — What Was Quiet Registration?

**Writer Truth:** historical standardization that made modern System registration reliable.  
**Use:** explains why old records use different terminology and why Luck's anomaly matters.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-2 onward.

## M-012 — Can Luck Ever Become “Defined”?

**Writer Truth:** normal retroactive registration would require inventing or rewriting history the System does not possess. A lawful social/legal exception may be possible without metaphysical normalization.  
**Luck Baseline:** wants practical freedom more than a cosmic label.  
**Reveal Window:** RW-3 to RW-5.  
**Payoff:** identity can be socially recognized without erasing the fact that he arrived from outside the registry.

# 4. Series-Scale Threat Architecture

## 4.1 No Hidden Summoner

Do not solve the series by revealing that a secret mastermind planned Luck's arrival.

## 4.2 Systemic Pressure

Long-horizon pressure comes from:
- institutions trying to classify an exception;
- factions wanting to exploit one;
- historical boundary technology;
- Fate adapting around an external causal presence;
- legal/social systems needing a person to fit a category.

## 4.3 Escalation Logic

Escalation comes from **knowledge and consequence**, not new powers.

Future arcs may increase:
- who notices Luck;
- how much evidence exists;
- the political value of his anomaly;
- the cost of his reputation;
- attempts to study/control/reproduce the anomaly.

They may not increase his supernatural toolkit.
