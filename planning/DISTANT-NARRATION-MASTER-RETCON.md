# Distant Narration Master Retcon

**Project:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Status:** LOCKED AUTHORIAL RETCON  
**Effective:** 2026-09-26  
**Priority:** Overrides all conflicting close-first-person / first-person-narrator rules in older files.

# 1. Locked POV

All standard Red Jackal chapters use:

**DISTANT THIRD-PERSON LIMITED**

Narration uses:
- Red / he / him / his;
- external scene description;
- observable behavior;
- selective brief Red thought;
- no head-hopping.

Red is the focal character, but not the narrator.

# 2. Internal Thought

Direct internal thought is allowed sparingly.

Use it when:
- the exact wording is funny;
- the thought cannot be shown efficiently through behavior/dialogue;
- a sharp private contradiction matters.

Preferred:

Red stared at the locked door.

*Could break it.*

He tried the handle instead.

Do not turn third-person narration into disguised first-person thought-stream prose.

# 3. Dialogue

Dialogue carries more of Red's personality than before.

Red's:
- humor;
- arrogance;
- cluelessness about his ceiling;
- theatricality;
- meta-awareness;
- accidental-mastermind energy

should usually be visible through what he says and does.

# 4. Fourth-Wall Pause

Fourth-Wall Pause remains canon from CH-001.

A genuine reader address is a deliberate POV exception:
- local Veyran time freezes;
- Red may directly address the external audience in first person because he is literally speaking;
- narration resumes in third person when the pause ends.

# 5. Preservation

This retcon changes narration style, not:
- plot;
- chapter order;
- world;
- power rules;
- mystery truth;
- cast motives;
- battles;
- consequences;
- Greywake outcome;
- Merrowgate plan.

# 6. Conversion Order

Current first-person manuscripts must be converted chapter by chapter.

No chapter is considered fully style-current until:
1. distant-third-person manuscript revision;
2. character voice check;
3. continuity check;
4. Gate 9 revalidation;
5. publication copy synchronization.

# 7. Supersession

Any older file stating:
- close first-person is mandatory;
- Red narrates as "I";
- first-person narration is the locked default;
- narration must remain inside Red's continuous thought stream

is historical and non-authoritative after this retcon.
