## 4. Admission Contract

A provisional slot may become an actual destination arc only when all of these conditions are satisfied:

1. The destination already exists in the World Bible or has been narrowed from an eligible existing region.
2. Destination Selection Gate 5 approves exactly one existing destination.
3. The place has a distinct culture, visual identity, travel position, and local question.
4. The local conflict can resolve locally without exposing protected writer truth.
5. The supporting cast has independent objectives and exit conditions.
6. The mystery window advances only authorized M-IDs/F-IDs.
7. The action environment is distinct and can support a Battle Composition Map when combat naturally occurs.
8. Volume/Arc Gate 6 and Chapter Roadmap Gate 7 pass.
9. The arc creates a lasting consequence for Red, the native story, an institution, a relationship, the route, or the destination.
10. No higher-level Bible or current continuity record is contradicted.

## 5. Merge and Cut Rules

Merge or shorten a slot when two candidate destinations perform the same local and mystery function, or when one strong destination can carry adjacent functions without compressing the reveal ladder.

Cut a slot when:

- it repeats a prior local conflict;
- it exists only to host a reveal or stronger opponent;
- its midpoint and climax repeat an earlier pressure pattern;
- the local resolution is complete before the planned ending;
- its removal does not damage the mystery ladder, native story, or consequence chain.

Every merge or cut must be recorded here before the roadmap is changed. No slot may be retained merely to increase the number of volumes.

## 6. Action and Battle Contract

When a destination conflict naturally supports hostile action, its Gate 6 package must include at least one major/great battle, multiple appropriate minor action encounters, and more than one topology. Across the series, planned action should vary among 1v1, 2v1, 1v4/1vMany, uneven team, split-party, escort, defensive hold, pursuit/interception, rescue under attack, three-sided conflict, environmental action, and multi-front action.

Topology is not a power-ranking promise. Red may be able to end a fight immediately, but the manuscript must show why the actual objective is information, evidence, rescue, route control, timing, restraint, reputation, collateral avoidance, or another consequence-bearing choice.

Exact future matchups remain deferred to Gate 6/7 and detailed Battle/Scene OA remains deferred to Gate 8.
