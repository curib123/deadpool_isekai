# Luck Everhart Cinematic Canon — Current Authority

**Effective:** 2026-10-03  
**Status:** AUTHOR-DIRECTED CURRENT CANON AUTHORITY

This file supersedes conflicting historical protagonist, POV, power, injury, and progression language elsewhere in the repository.

## Protagonist

- Current name: **Luck Everhart**.
- The name is self-chosen in Chapter 1 after the forest encounter; it is not a recovered Earth birth name.
- Luck enjoys deliberate mysterious presentation, impressive pauses, and lines that sound more intentional than his actual plan.
- He remains human, fallible, physically vulnerable, and capable of fear.
- His reputation grows faster than his understanding of why events favor him.

## POV and Prose

- Distant third-person limited centered on Luck Everhart.
- Cinematic scene construction: goal → obstacle → attempt → change.
- Important people, exits, hazards, objects, and distances are staged before they matter.
- Emotion is shown primarily through behavior and purposeful dialogue.
- Action uses shorter sentences when needed, then returns to natural paragraph flow.
- Every scene leaves a meaningful consequence, decision, discovery, obligation, or new problem.

## Only Supernatural Power

Luck has exactly one supernatural ability: **Fortune Distortion**.

It is:
- passive;
- unconscious;
- probability-biased;
- limited to outcomes that remain physically/socially possible.

It may influence timing, aim, footing, mechanical failure, routes, random selection, accidental discoveries, interruptions, arrivals, and chains of ordinary causes.

It may not:
- create matter;
- produce constructs;
- stop or rewind time;
- regenerate Luck;
- resurrect him;
- teleport him without a normal mechanism;
- grant raw superhuman strength;
- guarantee victory;
- be consciously activated or aimed.

## Permanent Ignorance Rule

Luck never correctly identifies Fortune Distortion as his supernatural power, including in the final chapter.

He may call an incident lucky, recognize that coincidences keep happening, or hear other characters theorize about him. He still explains specific events through ordinary causes and never forms a plan that depends on “my luck.”

## Misunderstanding Engine

Use:

**Luck improvises → ordinary variables align improbably → witnesses reconstruct a deliberate plan → Luck notices the misunderstanding → he preserves the useful mystique → the reputation creates a later consequence.**

Do not repeat the same variation every chapter. Misunderstandings can create victories, obligations, suspicion, enemies, embarrassment, political attention, followers, or impossible expectations.

## Injury and Consequence

Luck is mortal.

- wounds persist;
- treatment matters;
- recovery takes time;
- damaged property stays damaged;
- spent money stays spent;
- political and social consequences continue;
- other characters remain competent and causally important.
