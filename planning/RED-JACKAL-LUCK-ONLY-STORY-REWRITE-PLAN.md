# Red Jackal Luck-Only Story Rewrite Plan

**Branch:** `rewrite/luck-only-retcon-2026-09-27`
**Status:** ACTIVE RETCON
**Scope:** Series foundation + Greywake Volume 1 manuscripts (Prologue + CH001–CH027)

# 1. Locked Retcon

Red Jackal has exactly **one supernatural ability: Jackal Luck**.

Remove as powers:
- Play Logic / imagination-based reality manipulation;
- supernatural regeneration or body restoration;
- literal Fourth-Wall Pause / time stop;
- conjured props, cartoon physics, transformations, reality edits, spatial jokes, or conceptual attacks;
- any second hidden combat ability.

Keep as character traits, not powers:
- fourth-wall/meta humor;
- theatrical role-play;
- Deadpool-like irreverence;
- Cid-like effortless aura and accidental intimidation;
- clueless confidence;
- the habit of claiming credit after events go his way.

# 2. Jackal Luck — Final Power Rule

Jackal Luck is passive probability distortion centered on Red's causal presence.

It can bend:
- timing;
- coincidence;
- aim and near-misses;
- enemy mistakes;
- mechanical failures;
- falling debris and terrain;
- who arrives at the right or wrong moment;
- which object happens to be nearby;
- accidental discoveries;
- social interruptions;
- chains of individually possible events.

It cannot:
- create matter from nothing;
- directly rewrite reality;
- stop time;
- resurrect Red;
- instantly heal impossible wounds;
- command a specific outcome on demand;
- make every consequence disappear.

Red can still be injured. Luck primarily keeps lethal events from resolving cleanly against him through absurd but causally possible chains.

# 3. Story Engine

Core loop:

**simple Red motive → reckless/theatrical choice → probability chain bends → Red survives or gains an opening → witnesses assume he planned it → Red accepts the aura → consequences return later**

Red usually does not realize Jackal Luck is a power. He interprets many events as:
- good timing;
- enemy stupidity;
- his own improvisation;
- “obviously part of the plan” after the fact.

The world gradually notices the statistical impossibility before Red does.

# 4. Power-Presentation Rules

1. Never label a lucky event as guaranteed.
2. Every large lucky result must be built from visible causal pieces.
3. Luck may solve Red's immediate problem while worsening another.
4. Supporting characters must remain competent and decisive.
5. Red must not replace the native Hero, Hero Party, or larger world story.
6. No power progression. Luck is present from the start; only reader/world understanding increases.
7. The System may fail to classify Red, but the failure is not itself proof that he is omnipotent.
8. Fourth-wall jokes are prose/voice behavior only. Veyran time continues normally.

# 5. Manuscript Prose Rewrite Rule

The current one-line-paragraph cadence is deprecated.

Target:
- normal narration: mostly 2–5 sentences per paragraph;
- action: 1–3 sentences where speed genuinely benefits;
- dialogue: separate speaker turns remain separate paragraphs;
- single-sentence paragraphs: reserved for impact, punchlines, reveals, reversals, or chapter-ending emphasis;
- avoid chains of 3+ single-sentence narration paragraphs unless intentionally approved for a climax.

The rewrite must preserve:
- simple natural English;
- cinematic readability;
- strong dialogue;
- selective internal thought;
- mobile-friendly paragraph size;
- distant third-person limited Red focus;
- chapter hooks.

# 6. Greywake Rewrite Pass

## Prologue
Preserve Soul Drift and the wrong-person arrival. Remove any implication that multiple anomaly powers are imported with Red.

## CH001 — Wrong Forest, Wrong World
Rewrite the first predator encounter so Red survives through an escalating coincidence chain rather than Play Logic, impossible healing, or time-stop. Establish luck without naming it.

## CH002 — Undefined
Keep the gate/identity failure and Greywake entry. Convert fourth-wall time-stop beats into ordinary meta asides. Injury must be plausible and naturally healing.

## CH003 — The Things They Can Measure
Keep measurement/investigation pressure. Any impossible display becomes measurement noise, timing coincidence, equipment failure, or a statistical anomaly linked to Red.

## CH004 — Off the Books
Preserve employment/social setup. Luck creates suspiciously useful openings without giving Red direct reality control.

## CH005 — A Better Hammer
Rebuild any “impossible tool/solution” beat as Red selecting or misusing an ordinary object at exactly the right time, with environment and enemy error doing the rest.

## CH006 — The Road That Works
Use route choice, weather, traffic, collapsing terrain, and badly timed enemy movement as probability-chain material.

## CH007 — You Should Be Dead
This becomes the clearest early luck chapter. Red appears certain to die; a chain of individually plausible accidents prevents the killing blow. Remove regeneration framing.

## CH008 — Outside the Lanterns
Keep danger and investigation. Make Red's advantage come from timing, noise, visibility, patrol movement, and accidental positioning.

## CH009 — Wrong Tracks
Make Red's incorrect or casual choice accidentally intersect the real trail. Witnesses can misread it as deduction.

## CH010 — The Ledger Doesn't Balance
Luck should expose a discrepancy through mundane coincidence: misplaced records, interrupted deliveries, mismatched seals, overheard timing, or a clerk arriving at the exact moment.

## CH011 — Broken North
Any manifested barrier/slab is removed. Survival/action must use terrain, existing structures, enemy collision, tool failure, or third-party intervention triggered by timing.

## CH012–CH018
Preserve investigation and consequence structure. Do not force a luck spectacle into every chapter. Luck should appear only where causally useful.

## CH019 — Build the Wrong Road
Remove manifestation/reality-edit wording. Red's “wrong” construction choice accidentally becomes the correct tactical solution because of terrain, runoff, migration, or enemy movement.

## CH020–CH025
Preserve escalation and Greywake defense. Large outcomes must remain team victories. Red's luck changes openings, timing, and catastrophic near-misses rather than deleting the battle.

## CH026 — Before They Decide What I Am
Remove regeneration references. The debate around Red should focus on impossible statistical survival and System non-classification.

## CH027 — Salt in the Air
End Volume 1 with Red leaving behind a reputation for impossible timing, not impossible reality control. Carry the luck mystery into Merrowgate.

# 8. Repository-Wide Obsolete Terms

After the manuscript pass, audit and remove or explicitly mark historical/deprecated uses of:
- Play Logic;
- Fourth-Wall Pause as a power;
- regeneration;
- body restoration;
- reality manipulation;
- manifestation;
- conjured props;
- time freeze/time stop caused by Red.

# 9. Acceptance Criteria

Volume 1 passes only when:
- Red has one and only one supernatural power: Jackal Luck;
- no chapter requires Play Logic, regeneration, or time stop to function;
- luck outcomes remain causally legible;
- supporting cast remains useful;
- the Greywake plot still resolves coherently;
- no chapter is dominated by one-line narration formatting;
- single-sentence paragraphs are intentional rather than default;
- chapter endings retain forward pull;
- Red remains clueless, theatrical, self-centered, funny, dangerous, and accidentally legendary.
