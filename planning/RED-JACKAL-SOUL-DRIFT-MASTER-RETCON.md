# Red Jackal Soul-Drift / Fourth-Wall Master Retcon

**Project:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Status:** LOCKED AUTHORIAL RETCON  
**Effective:** 2026-09-20  
**Priority:** Overrides conflicting POV, origin, reincarnation, power-progression, fourth-wall, and Red-personality rules in older files.

# 1. Preservation Lock

This retcon does **not** replace the established story world.

Preserve:
- Veyr and the existing world map;
- all existing nations, regions, cities, roads, ports, seas and destinations;
- Greywake as Volume 1 / Greywake Arc;
- Merrowgate as Volume 2 / Merrowgate Arc;
- existing chapter count, chapter order, chapter titles and pacing structure unless a later explicit pacing retcon says otherwise;
- existing local antagonists, local conflicts, mystery placement, major consequences and destination outcomes where they do not directly contradict this retcon;
- the rule **one major volume = one destination arc**.

The retcon changes **Red Jackal**, his metaphysical origin, how his abilities work, the fourth wall, and the manuscript POV/voice.

# 2. Earth Truth

Before Veyr, Red Jackal was an ordinary young man from Earth.

His writer-truth Earth identity remains **Evan Calder** unless a later authorial retcon renames him.

He was an obsessive, hard-dying **Deadpool fan** across comics, films, games, memes and related pop culture. He loved:
- irreverent humor;
- impossible confidence;
- talking through danger;
- treating violence like performance;
- fourth-wall jokes;
- regeneration as absurd physical comedy;
- refusing to behave with the dignity a situation expects.

This fandom is an **in-world biographical fact**, not permission to copy Deadpool's exact dialogue, costume, plots, catchphrases, or characterization.

Red Jackal must remain an original character.

# 3. Mysterious Explosion

Evan's Earth life ends during a **mysterious explosion**.

Locked facts:
- the explosion is real;
- Evan dies in or because of it;
- it is not currently explained as divine summoning;
- no goddess or System chooses him;
- its exact cause remains a protected long-term mystery.

The explosion is the event that knocks his soul out of its normal post-death route and into Soul Drift.

# 4. Soul Drift

**Soul Drift** is a natural cosmological phenomenon.

It is:
- non-sentient;
- not a god;
- not a spell;
- not the World System;
- not Fate choosing a hero;
- not inherently malicious;
- not unique to Evan in principle, though surviving it intact enough to re-embody is extraordinarily rare.

Soul Drift occurs when a dislocated soul falls outside a stable reincarnation route and is carried through the boundary-current between otherwise separate soul/reality systems.

A drifting soul is not properly inside either world's registry while the drift lasts.

# 5. Memory Erosion

Soul Drift erodes structured autobiographical identity.

By the time Evan reaches Veyr he has lost most of:
- personal history;
- family details;
- ordinary relationships;
- work history;
- specific chronology;
- much of the stable personality he had on Earth.

He retains:
- language and general reasoning;
- broad cultural fragments;
- genre literacy;
- instinctive humor;
- performance habits;
- unusually strong fragments connected to his obsessive Deadpool fandom.

The result is **not literal Deadpool possession**.

The surviving mental groove teaches Red how to think:
- talk through danger;
- mock the frame of the scene;
- perform confidence;
- turn pain into comedy;
- treat enemies as participants in a bit;
- act as though an audience is present;
- refuse to respect the expected tone.

This becomes the seed of Red Jackal's new personality.

He may remember Deadpool references more clearly than he remembers his own Earth family.

That imbalance is intentional and unsettling.

# 6. Arrival in Veyr

Veyr is organized around a strict World System.

Native and normally reincarnated beings possess local causal and registry roots.

Red does not.

He arrives from outside the Closed Loom after Soul Drift without:
- a native soul-registry root;
- a valid Veyran race root;
- a class root;
- a level/progression root;
- a native Fate history;
- a recognized reincarnation entry.

Therefore institutional and System interfaces return states such as:

- **Name: UNDEFINED**
- **Race: UNDEFINED**
- **Class: UNDEFINED**
- **Level: UNDEFINED**
- **Progression: NO RECORD**
- **Registry: FAILED / NO RECORD**
- **Classification: ANOMALY** when an institution creates its own descriptive label.

The System is not hiding a secret level.

There is no number too large to display.

**There is no valid System progression object to measure.**

# 7. The World-System Bug

Red is physically real in Veyr but metaphysically external to the system that expects every person to have a valid local definition.

He is therefore a living exception.

The World System can:
- observe consequences around him;
- observe ordinary matter he touches;
- observe damage to native things;
- fail while trying to define him.

It cannot:
- assign him a real level;
- give him a conventional class;
- make him progress through native tiers;
- measure a true upper limit;
- normalize him through ordinary appraisal;
- explain the origin of his powers.

Red does **not** level up.

He does **not** unlock higher stages.

He does **not** become overpowered later.

He begins and ends the series fundamentally outside the scale.

# 8. Red's Three Core Anomaly Expressions

## 8.1 Regeneration

Red's body reasserts the foreign soul-pattern carried through Soul Drift.

The result resembles absurd comic-book regeneration:
- catastrophic physical injury is not a normal long-term combat limit;
- lost tissue can return;
- lethal damage does not follow ordinary expectations;
- pain can still be real;
- he may deliberately leave damage visible for performance;
- consequences to other people do not regenerate.

This is not a System Skill.

It has no level.

## 8.2 Play Logic / Imagination Authority

Red can make imagined solutions physically real.

This is the story's original reality-breaking ability, with broad cartoon/reality-gag freedom comparable in *type of appeal* to wild imagination comedy, but it must use original manifestations and scene logic.

Writer truth:

**If Red can genuinely imagine an effect as part of his self-authored scene, Play Logic has no known intrinsic raw-power ceiling.**

He can potentially produce:
- objects;
- weapons;
- tools;
- impossible movement;
- spatial gags;
- absurd physical counters;
- environmental changes;
- visual/theatrical effects;
- temporary or persistent physical solutions when the story establishes that choice.

Do not convert this into a spell list.

Do not create mana costs, stages, cooldown tiers, mastery levels, or a final form.

What changes is his **choice, creativity, information, judgment and willingness to reveal**, not his raw capacity.

## 8.3 Fourth-Wall Aside / Dead-Time

When Red deliberately breaks the fourth wall to address the audience, **local world-time stops**.

Locked behavior:
1. Red chooses to make a true fourth-wall aside.
2. Veyr's local physical sequence freezes at that instant.
3. Other characters, projectiles, weather, fire, falling objects and ordinary processes remain paused.
4. Red may speak to the audience, look toward the implied frame, react, complain, explain a joke, or comment on the scene.
5. The aside is experienced only by Red and the audience-facing narrative layer unless later canon explicitly establishes an exceptional witness.
6. When Red ends the aside, physical time resumes from the same instant.

The default purpose is **conversation/performance**, not tactical optimization.

To preserve readable scenes:
- he does not need to use it every page;
- serious moments may contain no aside at all;
- he should not spend hours solving ordinary problems inside Dead-Time just because he technically could;
- if he abuses it physically, that is a deliberate story event, not the routine baseline.

There is no progression path for Dead-Time.

It works from the beginning.

# 9. Enemy-Play Lock

Red loves playing with enemies.

He may:
- pretend to misunderstand;
- let attacks hit;
- fake a weakness;
- invent rules for himself;
- use a ridiculous counter when a simple one would work;
- let an enemy finish a speech;
- praise an attack that never threatened him;
- stage entrances and exits;
- deliberately appear weaker;
- act impressed;
- stop time for a private fourth-wall comment;
- prolong the confrontation because he is entertained.

This must feel like a **personality choice**, not a hidden training mechanic.

The direct-combat question is rarely:

**Can Red win?**

It is:

**How long will Red keep playing, what will happen around him while he does, and what will make him stop?**

# 10. Serious Switch

When Red stops playing:
- the jokes can vanish;
- the fourth-wall asides can stop;
- he stops offering openings;
- he uses the shortest solution;
- the enemy realizes the earlier fight was theater.

No transformation.

No level-up.

No awakening.

Only restraint ending.

# 11. Narrative POV

The locked default manuscript POV is:

**close first-person limited — Red Jackal narrates as “I.”**

The narration should feel physically close and movie-like.

Use:
- what I see;
- what I hear;
- what I feel;
- what I think;
- what I misunderstand;
- what I choose not to say.

Do not use omniscient information Red does not possess.

First-person does **not** mean constant internal essays.

Keep internal thought short and selective.

# 12. Movie-Like English

Use simple, natural cinematic English.

Prefer:
**visible action / sound / expression → my reaction → dialogue or choice → consequence**

Dialogue should feel human:
- interruption;
- pauses;
- teasing;
- unfinished answers;
- callbacks;
- subtext;
- people ignoring Red's jokes;
- different voices for different characters.

Comedy comes from character and situation.

Serious danger and emotion may become quiet.

# 13. Originality Guard

High-level inspiration is allowed.

Do **not** copy:
- Deadpool's exact voice, lines, costume, plot beats or supporting cast;
- Cid/Shadow's exact dialogue, organizations, techniques, names, scenes or misunderstandings;
- The Mask's exact set pieces, dialogue or visual identity.

The target appeal is original:

**an amnesiac outsider whose remaining Earth mental groove is comic-book irreverence, who treats reality as a stage because reality literally cannot define him.**

# 14. Existing Story Preservation

Greywake and Merrowgate remain structurally intact.

Existing pacing remains intact.

For old scenes where Red appears limited:
- reinterpret limitation as restraint;
- incomplete information;
- role-play;
- concern for collateral;
- following a specialist because force is not knowledge;
- deliberately choosing an ordinary solution;
- not yet realizing how naturally he can express an absurd idea.

Never reinterpret them as real raw-power ceilings.

# 15. Production Consequence

All plans must use this retcon immediately.

Existing manuscripts written in close-third are now **stylistically stale** and require a new first-person / Soul-Drift / fourth-wall consistency revision before new chapter production continues.

CH-028 remains paused until the current manuscript set is synchronized.

# 16. Core Sentence

**Red Jackal is not a man climbing a power system; he is an unregistered soul-drift survivor that the system cannot define, already absurdly powerful from the beginning, playing inside a world that assumes everything must have rules.**
