# Rook Vane — Luck-Only Full Story Reset

**Status:** AUTHORITATIVE RESET — 2026-09-27
**Supersedes:** Red Jackal / Play Logic / regeneration / supernatural fourth-wall versions
**Scope:** Series identity, protagonist, power system, Volume 1 Greywake, future arcs

# 1. New Main Character

**Name:** Rook Vane

Rook Vane replaces Red Jackal as the protagonist identity.

Rook is not a reality-warper, immortal, regenerator, time-stopper, chosen hero, or secret max-level fighter.

He has exactly **one supernatural ability**:

# 2. Fortune Distortion

Working in-world term: **Jackal Luck is retired.**
Canonical ability name: **Fortune Distortion**.

Fortune Distortion is passive probability distortion around Rook.

Rook cannot activate it on command.

He cannot choose the exact result.

He cannot create impossible outcomes.

Instead, chains of individually possible events become absurdly favorable around him.

Examples:
- an enemy's weapon breaks at the worst moment;
- a loose stone causes a stronger opponent to slip;
- a monster attacks the wrong target;
- two enemies collide;
- a falling beam blocks a killing blow;
- the right person arrives at the exact wrong/right second;
- Rook guesses and accidentally chooses the correct route;
- a document falls from a stack and exposes the one record everyone needed;
- a terrifying opponent mistakes Rook's silence for confidence and retreats;
- a rumor causes enemies to overestimate him and sabotage themselves.

# 3. Core Character Engine

Rook's comedy/aura engine is:

**Rook is nervous or improvising → he says/does something simple → coincidence makes it look brilliant → witnesses interpret hidden genius or terrifying confidence → Rook realizes the misunderstanding → he keeps a straight face and lets them believe it → the reputation creates a larger future problem.**

Important:
- Rook is not cowardly all the time.
- He can be clever, observant, shameless and opportunistic.
- He is physically ordinary-to-capable, not superhuman.
- He survives because probability refuses to let lethal sequences resolve cleanly.
- He can still be injured, exhausted, poisoned, arrested, embarrassed, trapped, or defeated in an objective.
- His luck does not automatically save other people.
- His reputation can become more dangerous than his real combat ability.

# 4. Personality

Public face:
- calm;
- dry;
- confident-looking;
- difficult to read;
- sometimes deliberately theatrical once he notices people are afraid of him.

Private reality:
- frequently improvising;
- often unsure what is happening;
- notices danger clearly;
- internally more expressive than he looks;
- willing to bluff when the misunderstanding is useful;
- enjoys taking credit after luck has already saved him;
- curious enough to enter trouble;
- self-centered enough to avoid becoming a conventional hero.

Rook is original. Broad inspiration may include accidental-legend and misunderstood-aura comedy, but no copied scenes, dialogue, powers, costume, or plot beats from existing works.

# 6. New Combat Law

Rook may face opponents much stronger and more skilled than he is.

Combat works through:
1. real opponent pressure;
2. Rook trying to survive or bluff;
3. small probability deviations;
4. an improbable chain forms;
5. Rook or an ally notices the opening;
6. the opening is converted into escape, victory, rescue, evidence, delay, or another objective;
7. witnesses misunderstand how much Rook planned.

Rook can win without throwing the strongest attack.

He may win because:
- the enemy defeats itself;
- terrain collapses;
- reinforcements arrive;
- an attack ricochets;
- a trap triggers early;
- a stronger ally misreads Rook's gesture as a signal;
- the enemy retreats from a threat that does not actually exist.

# 7. Aura / Misinterpretation Law

Rook gradually becomes famous as a hidden monster or master strategist.

The truth is simpler:
- he is lucky;
- he is good at keeping a straight face;
- he learns to exploit misunderstandings;
- people connect unrelated coincidences into a legend.

Reputation ladder:

1. Unknown outsider
2. Unclassifiable stranger
3. Suspicious survivor
4. “He planned that.”
5. “He knew that would happen.”
6. “Do not attack him first.”
7. “Nobody knows what happens if he gets serious.”
8. Late-series myth: people assume entire disasters were part of Rook's plan

Rook himself never receives a second supernatural power.
