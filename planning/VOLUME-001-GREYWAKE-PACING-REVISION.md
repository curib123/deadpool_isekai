# 1. Revision Principle

The Greywake Arc does not need a new plot.

Its current structure already has:
- a strong opening;
- useful early work/action variety;
- a clear conspiracy;
- a real midpoint;
- Red's causal mistake;
- a major reversal;
- a combined-cast climax;
- lasting consequences;
- a strong departure.

The pacing problem is distribution.

Current pressure is uneven:
- CH-009–CH-013 place five investigation-heavy chapters consecutively;
- several clue chapters are longer than the physical climax;
- CH-021 spends substantial time in preparation;
- CH-023, the primary physical climax, is only about 2,036 words;
- CH-024 can feel like a separate resolution after CH-023 instead of the second half of one continuous climax.

Revision objective:

**investigation must keep moving physically, consequences must arrive sooner, and CH-021–CH-024 must read as one uninterrupted escalating crisis.**

# 3. New Pacing Law

From CH-008 onward:

1. No more than **one low-action / processing-heavy chapter in succession**.
2. Every investigation chapter must contain at least one of:
   - physical risk;
   - interpersonal conflict;
   - irreversible evidence;
   - immediate deadline;
   - material consequence;
   - choice that worsens the next chapter.
3. Repeated clue explanation is cut. Once the reader understands a fact, later scenes build on it rather than re-proving it.
4. Every chapter ending from CH-009 through CH-024 must create forward pressure, not merely another question.
5. CH-020 is the **climax ignition** even though Movement VI still formally begins at CH-021.
6. CH-021–CH-024 are treated as **one continuous climax sequence** with no emotional or temporal reset between chapters.
7. Spectacle comes from scale, geography, simultaneous objectives, Red's theatrical restraint, and the contrast between play and serious release.
8. Red may dominate the physical battlefield, while supporting characters remain essential for information, evacuation, logistics, evidence, and local decisions.

# 8. Battle Scale Rule

Arc 1 may feel larger by finally revealing more of how overpowered Red already is.

Battle spectacle should come from:

- visible mass movement;
- failing infrastructure;
- crowd/civilian pressure;
- limited routes;
- multiple simultaneous problems;
- weather/terrain where already canon-compatible;
- coordinated militia/workers;
- predators exploiting chaos;
- physical pain/exertion that does not reduce Red's underlying Play Logic capacity;
- Red being unable to know/attend every front at once;
- choices about what must be saved first;
- the danger of him treating a serious crisis like a game for too long.

The climax should feel like Greywake is surviving a disaster, not waiting for Red to cast the winning move.
