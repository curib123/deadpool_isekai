# Severity Review

## CRITICAL

**None after correction.**

## MAJOR — RESOLVED

### 1. CH-001 Knowledge Timing Conflict

The prior Volume 1 knowledge-state file said Red Jackal began the volume already knowing that:
- his body regenerates;
- imagination can create physical effects.

That contradicted the locked Chapter 1 purpose, which requires CH-001 to contain his first discovery of both phenomena.

**Resolution:** the knowledge-state file now separates:
- CH-001 Opening Knowledge;
- Knowledge Gained During CH-001;
- Begins CH-002 Knowing.

CH-001 now correctly owns both first discoveries.

### 2. Cross-World Reading Was Not Canonized

The first scene-architecture draft allowed the possibility that Red Jackal could read a road sign naming Greywake, but existing canon did not establish automatic translation or Veyran literacy.

**Resolution:** CH-001 now uses **No Silent Translation**.

Red Jackal may understand:
- road engineering;
- arrows;
- directional iconography;
- signs that a settlement lies ahead.

He may **not** read Veyran writing in CH-001.

The road leads to Greywake as production truth, but Red Jackal does not yet know the settlement name.

## MINOR — RESOLVED

An older Chapter Roadmap normalization pass had introduced the typo “Rovan Rovan Hesk.”

**Resolution:** restored canonical name **Rovan Hesk**.
