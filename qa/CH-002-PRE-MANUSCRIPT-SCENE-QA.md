# Severity Review

## CRITICAL

**None.**

## MAJOR — RESOLVED

### 1. Spoken-Language Continuity Gap

CH-001 canonically established that Red Jackal cannot read Veyran writing, but pre-CH-002 canon did not explain how first human conversation would work.

Silently giving him automatic spoken translation would have contradicted the established language boundary and created an unexplained ability.

**Resolution:**

The Systems Bible now defines **Wayfarer Tongue Tokens** as ordinary short-range institutional translation magic used at gates and trade posts.

For CH-002:
- first contact begins without mutual comprehension;
- gate staff activate a Wayfarer Tongue Token;
- ordinary spoken meaning becomes understandable;
- proper names can pass phonetically;
- writing remains unreadable;
- System text remains unreadable unless a local person explains it aloud;
- the token is not connected to Red Jackal's anomaly or any protected mystery.

### 2. Independent F-001 Interfaces Needed Canon Support

F-001 requires multiple legitimate appraisal methods to fail independently.

The Systems Bible previously defined World System fields but did not define routine institutional interfaces.

**Resolution:**

Two standard tools are now canonized:

1. **Gate Registry Seal**
2. **Certified Identity Slate**

They are separate authorized interfaces with different intake purposes and failure presentations.

This supports F-001 without using the physical-measurement contrast reserved for F-002.

## MINOR — RESOLVED

### Optional Power Retry

An early scene draft allowed Red Jackal to possibly attempt another Play Logic manifestation.

That was too loose for final Scene Architecture and risked stealing CH-005's intentional experimentation role.

**Resolution:** CH-002 now contains **no successful or failed Play Logic test**.

### Temporary Admission Was Too Flexible

The first architecture draft listed possible entry restrictions rather than a locked set.

**Resolution:** CH-002 now locks:
- a physical Unverified Entrant Token;
- presentation on request;
- no normal registered guild contracts;
- no identity-bound gate/travel services;
- no standard registered-traveler guarantees/insurance;
- mandatory controlled follow-up at the next intake cycle.
