# Purpose

This pass rewrites character expression without resetting plot, battles, mystery state, geography, or chapter order.

The goal is that every recurring character is identifiable by:
- what they notice;
- what they value;
- how they speak under pressure;
- what they refuse to do;
- what they think Red is useful or dangerous for.

The pass also enforces Red Jackal's final reader-facing engine:

**clueless chaos + meta-comedy + effortless aura + accidental legend + self-centered motive.**

Red may learn facts, but he does not become a conventional responsible hero.

# Locked Voice Fingerprints

- **Red Jackal:** playful, self-centered, meta-aware, casually arrogant, uninterested in measuring his full ceiling, willing to let others overinterpret him.
- **Maelis Orra:** evidence-first, precise, dry, separates observation from conclusion.
- **Tavian Rook:** terrain/movement/risk first; short operational language; no theatrical praise.
- **Captain Brynn Alder:** command-first; converts argument into assignments, routes and responsibility.
- **Sela Arven:** body-first blunt medical language; no romanticizing pain or regeneration.
- **Kellan Dorr:** physical/practical worker language; roads, timber, water, outer-community cost.
- **Jessa Vale:** cautious procedural voice; employment/family risk remains visible even after she chooses to act.
- **Rovan Hesk:** calm logistical pressure; frames choices through roads, winter, capacity, supply and survival cost.

# Red Jackal Stability Decision

Red may:
- remember a consequence;
- learn a useful tactical fact;
- like somebody;
- protect somebody;
- stay in a fight;
- choose not to use overwhelming power;
- accidentally produce a heroic outcome.

None of those beats automatically mean:
- moral growth;
- duty;
- humility;
- conventional heroism;
- power progression.

His default remains:

**The world has problems. Red has interests. Those overlap only when Red decides they do.**

# Supporting-Cast Independence Decision

Supporting characters must continue to solve parts of the story Red cannot replace with raw force:

- Maelis — proof and institutional record;
- Tavian — ecological/field interpretation;
- Brynn — command and civilian movement;
- Sela — medical judgment;
- Kellan — construction and outer-community knowledge;
- Jessa — operational record meaning and personal-cost testimony;
- Hesk — antagonist logistics and political survival argument.

They do not exist merely to:
- admire Red;
- explain Red;
- become followers;
- validate his jokes;
- lose competence so Red can look smarter.
