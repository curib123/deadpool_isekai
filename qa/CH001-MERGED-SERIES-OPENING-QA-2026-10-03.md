# CH001 Merged Series Opening QA

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Branch:** `story/merge-prologue-into-chapter-1-2026-10-03`  
**Scope:** former standalone prologue + CH001 + CH002 handoff + publication/canon documentation

# Decision

**PASS — CH001 is the single official beginning of the series.**

# 1. Structural Merge

The former prologue material has been merged into:

`manuscript/CH001-WRONG-FOREST-WRONG-WORLD.md`

There is no separate current prologue.

CH001 now contains one continuous opening arc:

**Earth explosion → missing memory/name → arrival in Veyr → first unexplained probability event → predator encounter → causal survival chain → theatrical bluff → ordinary injury → self-chosen Luck Everhart identity → civilization ahead**

# 2. Opening Hook

PASS.

The first line immediately establishes the catastrophic pre-Veyr event:

**“The last thing he remembered clearly was white light.”**

The second line establishes Luck's dry voice before his name or reputation exists.

The chapter then moves quickly from mystery into a physical survival problem rather than remaining in abstract exposition.

# 3. Character Establishment

PASS.

Before meeting any Veyran character, the opening establishes that Luck:
- uses humor under pressure;
- rehearses dramatic lines;
- fixes his appearance even without an audience;
- prefers presentation over admitting confusion;
- is frightened despite outward performance;
- does not understand Fortune Distortion.

His theatrical identity therefore predates fame and misunderstanding.

# 4. Fortune Distortion

PASS.

All lucky outcomes remain physically possible and use staged variables:
- rain-weakened branch;
- loose soil around the survey post;
- rotten post;
- roots;
- drainage geometry;
- vine snag;
- dead limb failure;
- predator attention shift after a distant howl.

Luck never activates or identifies a luck power.

# 5. Physical Stakes

PASS.

The chapter preserves:
- forearm cut;
- sore ankle;
- exhaustion;
- shaking hands;
- self-applied bandage.

There is:
- no supernatural regeneration;
- no time-stop;
- no manifestation;
- no second power;
- no guaranteed victory.

# 6. Name Origin

PASS.

Luck Everhart remains a self-chosen Veyran identity.

The chapter explicitly establishes:
- the old Earth name cannot be recovered;
- **Luck** is chosen because luck is the most practical explanation he has for surviving;
- **Everhart** is chosen because it sounds respectable and theatrical;
- the name is not destiny, prophecy, a recovered birth name, or power awareness.

# 7. Chapter Ending

PASS.

CH001 ends with Luck:
- possessing a usable public identity;
- having a direction;
- moving toward civilization;
- still lacking answers about Veyr or himself.

The final beat creates a clean next-click into CH002.

# 8. CH002 Handoff

PASS.

CH002 begins at civilization:
- Greywake wall/gate;
- language barrier;
- Luck gives the name he just chose;
- Wayfarer Tongue Token;
- identity-system pressure.

No arrival, predator, or name-origin material is repeated.

A leftover unstaged sentence (“The horse finished its step”) was removed during this QA pass.
