# Why the prose previously felt dry

The main problems were:
- too many isolated one-line narration paragraphs;
- stacks of fragments that read like screenplay beats;
- mechanical POV-conversion wording;
- dialogue separated from action/reaction too often;
- too little natural paragraph grouping;
- a few leftover first-person lines after the distant-third-person retcon;
- old regeneration-style wording that made Play Logic feel split into multiple powers.

# Current prose standard

The current chapter style is:

**normal flowing story prose + distant third-person limited + lively dialogue + selective Red self-thought**

Default paragraph behavior:
- 2–5 connected sentences when the same beat belongs together;
- short one-line paragraphs only for genuine impact, comedy, shock, or reversal;
- dialogue woven into movement, expression, silence, and physical reaction;
- internal thoughts used selectively rather than continuously;
- mobile-friendly web-novel readability without screenplay formatting.

# Power presentation

Current Red power identity remains:

- **Play Logic**
- **Jackal Luck**
- **Fourth-Wall Pause**

There is no separate Regeneration power.

Red's abnormal body repair is shown as the same impossible Play Logic behavior acting on his own body.

Jackal Luck is planted as subtle probability/coincidence distortion rather than announced as a skill.
