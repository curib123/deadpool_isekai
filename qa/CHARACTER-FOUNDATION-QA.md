# Red Jackal Specific QA

## Identity
**PASS.**

He is defined by:
- agency;
- performance;
- curiosity;
- vanity;
- irreverence;
- attachment he dislikes admitting;
- emotional avoidance;
- fear of externally assigned roles.

This produces an original internal engine instead of relying on external character imitation.

## Coolness vs Ridiculousness
**PASS.**

The Bible allows four valid outcomes:
1. performance succeeds;
2. people are impressed;
3. nobody cares;
4. the performance backfires.

This prevents aura farming from becoming automatic author favoritism.

## Comedy
**PASS.**

Humor is situational and optional. Serious stretches remain valid.

## Emotional Capacity
**PASS.**

Red Jackal can care, fear, fail, grieve, and accept consequences without becoming a conventional pure-hearted hero.

## Power/Personality Separation
**PASS.**

His personality explains why he chooses unusual applications of Play Logic, but the Character Bible does not overwrite the Systems Bible's power limits.
