## Canon Conflict Review

### Series Constitution compatibility
PASS.

- World-first structural law preserved.
- No event/theme arc names were created.
- No destination has been promoted to an arc.
- Red Jackal remains an unregistered anomaly.
- Comedy/consequence rules were not overwritten.

### World/System separation
PASS.

- Physical reality is not the World System.
- Magic predates and functions independently of System permission.
- Reincarnation governs soul continuity.
- Fate governs bounded convergence/probability.
- Great Design governs deeper long-horizon coherence.
- Play Logic is an anomaly expression, not a normal spell school or hidden class.

### Geography/logistics consistency
PASS.

- Crownspine influences Avarran climate and routes.
- Kharad settlement follows water and irrigation.
- Namarra settlement follows living root geography.
- Outer Pale settlement follows heat basins and supply corridors.
- Sea routes connect established economic regions.
- Teleportation is anchor-limited and expensive rather than free travel.
