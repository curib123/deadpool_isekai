# QA Gates

**Status:** CURRENT — 2026-10-03  
**Structural authority:** `series/STORY-STRUCTURE-BIBLE.md`

# Gate Order

## Gate 1 — Foundation
World/System/Character/Mystery Bibles do not contradict each other.

## Gate 2 — Destination
The destination exists in world canon and has a distinct local identity/conflict.

## Gate 3 — Volume Architecture
Local antagonist, local question, supporting cast, consequence path, climax, and departure are coherent.

## Gate 4 — Chapter Roadmap
Every chapter has an objective, conflict, change, and ending pull.

## Gate 5 — Scene Architecture
Every scene has goal, obstacle, staging, attempt, turn, and exit condition.

## Gate 6 — Manuscript
POV, prose, dialogue, action, Fortune Distortion, and character voice obey current rules.

## Gate 7 — Chapter QA
Check structure, causality, continuity, injury, resources, misunderstanding, mystery boundary, and ending.

## Gate 8 — Canon/Continuity
Update knowledge/consequence state and detect contradictions with prior chapters.

## Gate 9 — Publication Copy
Reader-facing copy is semantically equivalent to approved manuscript canon.

# Hard Fail Conditions
- removed power returns;
- first-person current narration returns;
- Luck consciously controls supernatural luck;
- a supporting character is made incompetent to elevate Luck;
- an injury/resource consequence vanishes;
- a scene uses unstaged convenience;
- a mystery reveals protected truth early;
- publication copy differs materially from canon.

# Revision Rule
Fix the highest owning layer first. Do not patch a structural contradiction only at line level.
