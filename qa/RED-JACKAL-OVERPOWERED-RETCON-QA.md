# Red Jackal Overpowered Retcon QA

**Project:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Review Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Status:** PASS — FOUNDATION RETCON ACCEPTED / DOWNSTREAM REVALIDATION REQUIRED

# Character Originality

**PASS**

Red Jackal remains an original character.

The retcon uses a broad overpowered-theatrical-role-player archetype but explicitly prohibits direct copying of an existing franchise character.

# Power Model

**PASS**

Authoritative rules now establish:
- already overpowered from the beginning;
- no Stage progression;
- no level/class raw-power growth;
- no mandatory awakening;
- no known intrinsic Play Logic ceiling;
- future spectacle is revelation, not upgrade.

# Combat Model

**PASS**

Direct combat tension is no longer based on whether Red can become strong enough.

Enemy scenes may use:
- voluntary restraint;
- role-play;
- deliberate underestimation;
- false weaknesses;
- staged difficulty;
- sudden serious release.

# Narrative Tension

**PASS**

Meaningful pressure remains through:
- incomplete information;
- simultaneous objectives;
- politics;
- evidence;
- relationships;
- collateral;
- timing;
- bad judgment;
- Red allowing a game to continue too long.

# Supporting Cast

**PASS**

Supporting characters remain narratively necessary through competencies Red does not automatically possess:
- knowledge;
- local access;
- evidence;
- engineering;
- logistics;
- command;
- law;
- social relationships.

# Regeneration

**PASS**

Ordinary lethal physical damage no longer defines Red's long-term combat ceiling.

Pain may remain, but pain is not equivalent to insufficient power.

# Arc Compatibility

## Volume 1
**REQUIRES DOWNSTREAM REVISION**

The local Greywake plot remains compatible, but old Stage I, exhaustion-limit and “cannot win by force” language requires reinterpretation or manuscript revision.

## Volume 2
**STRUCTURALLY COMPATIBLE / POWER LANGUAGE REVISED**

Merrowgate works well with the new model because its threat is political/commercial/evidentiary rather than primarily raw combat.
