# 3. Red Jackal Final Personality

PASS.

Current Arc 1 Red is consistently:
- self-centered;
- theatrical;
- meta-comedic;
- casually arrogant;
- uninterested in measuring his true ceiling;
- capable of absurd reality-breaking behavior;
- willing to use ordinary solutions when they are more interesting;
- capable of liking/protecting particular people without accepting universal duty;
- resistant to ownership, sainthood, institutional assignment and chosen-hero framing.

His Arc 1 change is informational/tactical only.

He does **not** become:
- morally purified;
- conventionally responsible;
- humble;
- law-abiding;
- a proper hero;
- stronger through progression.
