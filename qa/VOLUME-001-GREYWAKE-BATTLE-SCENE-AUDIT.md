# 2. Topology Variety Audit

Current Greywake contains:
- 1v1;
- team vs small pack;
- mixed team vs pack with worker-protection objective;
- evidence-site defense;
- evacuation/rescue under ecological attack;
- construction under hostile pressure;
- multi-front route defense;
- Red vs major predator inside a larger coalition battle;
- human interception/confrontation during active battlefield conditions.

**PASS — topology is not mechanically repetitive.**

The arc does not need an artificial 1v4 inserted solely to satisfy variety.

Future 1v4 / 2v1 / split-team / three-sided matchups should be used when naturally supported by the situation.

# 4. Major / Great Battle Requirement

Greywake has an intended multi-chapter great battle:

**CH-022 → CH-023 → CH-024**

The architecture is correct:
- first wave;
- territorial-predator / infrastructure hold;
- Hesk's final lure intervention;
- human confrontation;
- migration commitment.

However, the current saved manuscripts are structurally underweight for the studio's new great-battle standard:
- CH-022: ~1.35k words;
- CH-023: ~1.31k words;
- CH-024: ~1.37k words.

The older pacing authority explicitly targeted a larger continuous climax and specifically identified CH-023 as the primary physical battle chapter.

The current prose contains the correct events but does not yet give enough page-space to:
- sustain three simultaneous fronts;
- make battlefield geography fully legible;
- give supporting characters enough independent battle turns;
- allow opponent/hostile-force adaptation;
- make the territorial predator encounter feel like a true battle rather than a compressed obstacle;
- create a satisfying physical-climax → human-climax progression.

**PASS — CH-022→CH-024 battle amplification completed and revalidated.**

# 5. Red Jackal Battle Engine

Current manuscripts correctly establish:
- Red can physically dominate ordinary predators;
- he can make much larger Play Logic interventions;
- he does not possess a Stage ceiling;
- route/civilian/ecological information is the true limiting problem;
- serious mode is restraint dropping, not power-up.

**PASS.**

The amplification pass must not reintroduce:
- Stage I/II;
- stamina/resource depletion;
- fake precision loss;
- a stronger monster whose only purpose is to challenge Red physically.

# 6. Supporting-Cast Battle Agency

Current required battle ownership is strong:
- Tavian: ecology, route reads, timing, geometry;
- Brynn: militia command, evacuation, line control;
- Kellan: infrastructure and outer-route stakes;
- foremen/workers: actual road survival;
- Maelis: evidence integrity;
- Jessa: lure/service procedure;
- militia: physical line, rescue, barriers;
- Red: impossible intervention and raw physical dominance where selected.

**PASS**, but CH-022→024 expansion must give these characters additional visible independent beats.

# 7. Opponent / Hostile-Force Quality

Monster encounters generally follow ecology rather than villain choreography.

The territorial predator in CH-023 currently:
- wants space/route access;
- adapts by testing openings;
- is physically dominated by Red;
- exits when an available route opens.

This is logically sound.

However, in the amplified battle the predator needs at least one clearer adaptation beat and the herd/pack pressure needs to change the battlefield around it.

Hesk remains a human institutional antagonist rather than a boss-monster.

**PASS WITH AMPLIFICATION NOTE.**

# 8. Scene QA Audit

Battle scenes generally contain:
- orientation;
- objective;
- first contact;
- complication;
- choice;
- consequence;
- handoff.

Weakest area:
- CH-022→024 turns occur too quickly for their climax role.

The sequence is causally correct but compressed.

**PASS — climax scene depth and matchup clarity corrected.**
