# Architecture Quality Review

## Destination Identity
**PASS.**

Greywake materially shapes the conflict. The story depends on:
- the frontier economy;
- caravan-road survival;
- outer-homestead inequality;
- monster migration;
- distant Valedorn authority;
- local guild power.

Moving the exact architecture to a capital, island, desert city, or forest federation would require major rewriting.

## Local Story Independence
**PASS.**

Greywake Arc works without revealing the central series cosmology.

Rovan Hesk is a local antagonist, not a disguised endgame servant.

The migration crisis has local ecological and political causes.

## Protagonist Integrity
**PASS.**

Red Jackal enters for selfish/practical reasons, performs, jokes, resists authority, makes an actual mistake, and eventually takes responsibility without becoming a traditional hero.

## Power Integrity
**PASS.**

Stage I Play Logic remains improvisational and unstable. Regeneration gives survival capacity rather than automatic victory.

## Mystery Integrity
**PASS.**

The architecture uses early anomaly questions as hooks while leaving the writer-truth mystery structure intact.
