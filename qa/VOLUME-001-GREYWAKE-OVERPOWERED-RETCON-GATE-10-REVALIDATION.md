# 5. Character / Power Model

**PASS.**

Volume 1 now consistently presents a **revelation arc rather than a power-progression arc**.

Writer truth:
- Red is already overwhelmingly powerful;
- Play Logic has no known intrinsic raw-power ceiling;
- no Stage I → II → III → IV progression exists;
- no level/class/stat growth is required;
- ordinary pain or exhaustion does not shut off raw capability;
- increasingly large feats are reveals of existing power.

The manuscripts do not require the reader to believe Red gained a new tier during the Greywake climax.

# 6. Early-Volume Interpretation

CH-006 establishes voluntary non-use when ordinary specialist procedure is already better for the situation.

Red can survive and physically dominate an encounter while still making a decision that gets an ordinary worker hurt.

# 8. Red's Mistake / Major Reversal

**PASS.**

The new prose explicitly establishes:
- Red could destroy the lure housing by overwhelming force;
- he deliberately chooses a precision tool because he does not understand the device/network;
- despite receiving specialist warnings, curiosity/impatience wins;

- Kellan is seriously injured;

The retcon therefore increases, rather than removes, Red's responsibility.

# 11. CH-023 Physical Climax

**PASS.**

CH-023 now functions as the unmistakable major physical battle chapter.

Key reveal:
- Red catches and redirects the largest territorial predator;
- when one road shelf failure spreads beyond the initially requested brace, Red expands the same support across the failing inner edge;
- the road stops falling;
- he leaves the support operating while he moves elsewhere;
- permanent timber/stone repairs are installed under its protection;
- he releases the impossible support only as the real structure takes load;
- he remains capable of additional impossible action afterward.

This is explicitly framed as **removing restraint / revealing capability**, not gaining power.

Supporting competencies remain necessary:
- Tavian decides migration geometry and timing;
- Brynn commands personnel and evacuation;
- road crews own permanent structural repair;
- militia handle multiple local threats;
- Red cannot replace their knowledge merely by using more force.

# 12. CH-024 Human Climax

**PASS.**

Red explicitly confirms he **could stop the animals by force**, while explaining why that does not make him qualified to choose the correct ecological outcome.

Core line:

**“Power is not the same thing as being right.”**

Hesk is defeated through combined independent functions:
- Red prevents unilateral physical action;
- Brynn exercises emergency authority;
- Maelis preserves evidence;
- Jessa understands and neutralizes the housing correctly;
- Tavian determines when/how the herd can safely recommit;
- Greywake's road/work crews have already made Broken North viable.

This fully satisfies the new overpowered-protagonist narrative model.
