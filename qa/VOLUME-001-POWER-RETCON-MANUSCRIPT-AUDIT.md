# 1. Audit Standard

A chapter requires prose revision only when the manuscript itself materially asserts an old power rule that conflicts with the new canon.

A revision is mandatory when prose establishes one or more of:

- Red genuinely lacks enough raw power;
- repeated Play Logic use depletes a finite combat-power budget;
- pain/injury prevents him from accessing power he otherwise possesses;
- a manifestation fails because Red has reached a real Stage-I-style ceiling;
- Red must conserve power because he may run out;
- Red cannot stop an opponent or physical threat because he is not strong enough yet;
- a fight treats Red as genuinely matched when the new character model requires voluntary role-play/restraint;
- the chapter's plot depends on Red being physically incapable rather than choosing not to act.

A chapter does **not** require revision merely because:

- Red feels pain;
- Red is physically injured;
- Red accepts normal medical care;
- Red uses a small manifestation;
- Red chooses ordinary tools instead of power;
- a manifestation is temporary by choice;
- Red lacks local knowledge;
- Red cannot read Veyran;
- Red cannot protect people in places he does not know about or is not present to see;
- supporting characters contribute expertise Red does not possess;
- Red incorrectly interprets his own ability early in the story.

# 2. Final Classification

## A — SUBSTANTIVE PROSE REVISION REQUIRED

These chapters contain scene logic that materially depends on the old finite-power model.

### CH-007 — You Should Be Dead

**Decision:** REVISE — SUBSTANTIVE

Current manuscript problems:
- Red enters the predator fight believing injury/survival gives him only a limited advantage;
- pain disrupts Play Logic directly;
- the barricade arrives weakly because he is injured;
- the construct cracks while Red physically struggles to maintain it;
- the scene presents Red as genuinely endangered by ordinary predators.

What must remain:
- Red makes a selfish/reckless choice;
- he steps out of Tavian's safer line;
- a worker is injured because Red's choice opens space;
- Red learns that his personal survivability does not protect everyone else;
- Tavian's field competence remains valid;
- F-003 / abnormal restoration observation remains intact.

New interpretation:
Red can dominate the predators physically, but he treats the encounter like a test/game, overvalues his own invulnerability, and fails to account for how the pack reacts around other people.

The worker's injury must come from **Red's judgment failure**, not insufficient power.

---

### CH-019 — Build the Wrong Road

**Decision:** REVISE — SUBSTANTIVE

Current manuscript repeatedly establishes:
- pressure behind the eyes;
- manifestation cracking from duration;
- second manifestation coming slower;
- hands trembling from repeated power use;
- a third manifestation appearing badly;
- worsening shake after each use;
- explicit statement that Red will not/cannot make a fourth thing;
- “energy” not returning even as injuries close.

This is the clearest pre-climax finite-resource chapter.

What must remain:
- collective construction;
- real timber/stone work matters;
- Red accepts a support role;
- Tavian/Brynn/work crews remain necessary;
- the road becomes physically usable through ordinary labor;
- live animal movement interrupts construction.

New interpretation:
Red deliberately follows the coalition's engineering plan and limits himself to small temporary aids because he is **playing the assigned support role** and because permanent road design requires local expertise/ownership.

He may become physically dirty/tired from ordinary work, but Play Logic itself may not deplete a raw-power reserve.

---

### CH-020 — The Last Lure

**Decision:** REVISE — SUBSTANTIVE

Hard conflicts:
- manifestation cracks immediately due headache/exhaustion;
- Red's hands shake more after using it;
- he explicitly does not try again;
- Hesk escapes because Red is too exhausted/slow to catch him;
- Red concludes Hesk is too far away to catch in his current state.

This chapter's ending currently depends directly on Red being physically unable to stop Hesk.

New required logic:
Red **chooses not to immediately take Hesk**.

Preferred retcon-compatible reason:
- evidence, witnesses, stock custody, and Jessa are the immediate priority;
- Red recognizes Hesk is running toward his remaining field option;
- part of Red is curious enough to let the game continue and see where Hesk goes;
- this choice creates real later risk and strengthens Red's anti-villain responsibility problem.

Hesk's escape becomes **Red's choice / misjudgment**, not lack of power.

---

### CH-021 — Before the Herd

**Decision:** REVISE — SUBSTANTIVE

Hard conflicts:
- lingering tremor is treated as manifestation depletion;
- ordinary work is praised because it avoids “pressure spike” and “loss of focus”;
- Sela medically orders Red not to use Play Logic before the surge;
- the chapter treats power conservation as necessary because of finite stamina;
- “keep your power unused” is an explicit finite-resource battle setup.

What must remain:
- evacuation;
- Kellan's route expertise while injured;
- ordinary workers solving ordinary problems;
- zero actual Play Logic manifestations if desired;
- Red accepting a support role.

New interpretation:
Red avoids Play Logic because the coalition wants **predictability**, controlled routes, and ordinary fixes that remain after he leaves—not because he needs to save mana/stamina.

Sela may object to Red's recklessness and pain tolerance, but not manage a finite Play Logic battery.

---

### CH-022 — First Wave

**Decision:** REVISE — SUBSTANTIVE

Hard conflicts:
- pressure/headache tied to manifestation use;
- second manifestation hurts sooner;
- hands shake progressively;
- third manifestation comes out wrong before right;
- Red explicitly treats the whole herd as beyond what he can shape;
- Tavian ends his use because Red is reaching a real capacity limit;
- injuries and exhaustion visibly degrade output.

What must remain:
- three major local intervention beats may remain for pacing;
- Tavian identifies the correct route geometry;
- Brynn coordinates workers/militia;
- Red stays with the line instead of abandoning people to chase Hesk;
- local failures continue into CH-023.

New interpretation:
The limited-looking interventions are **deliberate, exact, local choices** because Red is honoring Tavian's migration plan and avoiding collateral/panic.

They are not his maximum capability.

---

### CH-023 — Greywake Holds

**Decision:** REVISE — MAJOR / CLIMAX REBUILD

This is the largest retcon target.

Hard conflicts:
- “No power. Not yet” is written as conservation;
- regeneration keeps him standing but does not return spent capacity;
- shaking hands telegraph depletion;
- Red says he can probably hold only one shelf;
- first shape becomes “too much” and blurs;
- construct deforms and cracks because Red cannot sustain it;
- vision darkens from holding the manifestation;
- the climax positions one temporary brace as his maximum meaningful contribution.

This directly contradicts the new protagonist.

New climax requirement:
- Red is physically capable of vastly more from the beginning;
- the chapter must make his restraint intentional;
- the battle can finally reveal a much larger gap between what witnesses thought his limits were and what he actually can do;
- no power-up language;
- no Stage unlock;
- no “new form.”

Supporting cast remains essential because:
- Tavian knows migration behavior;
- Brynn owns command/evacuation;
- workers know the road;
- Red cannot automatically know the correct route outcome.

This chapter should become the first major **“he was holding back”** physical reveal.

---

### CH-024 — No One Gets Chosen

**Decision:** REVISE — MAJOR / CLIMAX SECOND HALF

This contains the most explicit contradiction in Volume 1.

Hard conflicts include:
- Hesk sees Red shaking and barely standing;
- Hesk says Red cannot stop the migration;
- Red answers **“No.”**
- Red believes he has “one useful thing left”;
- the final manifestation forms slowly because he is depleted;
- the construct cracks and collapses from strain;
- Red explicitly says:
  **“I could not have made anything useful if I wanted to.”**

That final line is incompatible with the new canon and cannot be reinterpreted.

New interpretation:
- Hesk mistakes Red's earlier restraint/injuries for weakness;
- Red can let Hesk believe that for a moment;
- Red should demonstrate that physical force was never the real problem;
- the route still requires Tavian/Jessa/Maelis/Brynn because evidence, lure procedure, migration behavior, and legal authority are different problems from raw strength;
- Hesk loses because both the local system and Red turn against his imposed-sacrifice logic.

This chapter should deliver the **human climax plus the clearest serious-switch moment** of the arc.

### CH-016 — Backflow

**Decision:** REVISE — TARGETED / HIGH IMPORTANCE

Problems:
- pain causes concentration to slip;
- barrier is too short and has to be forced wider;
- impact makes it flicker;
- collapse is framed as maximum duration;
- “Seconds. That was all it had bought” reads as a raw-capacity ceiling.

Preserve:
- the backflow spreads across multiple routes;
- Red cannot be everywhere at once;
- rescue choices happen under incomplete information;
- Kellan is severely injured;
- Red learns overwhelming personal survivability does not prevent others from paying for his mistake.

Retcon:
The local barrier should be intentionally narrow/temporary because Red is trying to **redirect**, not stop/crush the moving animals.

Kellan's injury survives because it occurs in a larger multi-route crisis Red did not fully understand or see in time.
