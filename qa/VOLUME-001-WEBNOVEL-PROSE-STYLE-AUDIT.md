# Volume 1 — Greywake Web-Novel Prose Style Audit

**Project:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Scope:** CH-001 through CH-026  
**Audit Date:** 2026-09-26  
**Standard:** `qa/WEBNOVEL-PROSE-STYLE-STANDARD.md`  
**Status:** PASS — CLOSE FIRST-PERSON WEB-NOVEL STYLE

# 1. Style Decision

Arc 1 uses the locked prose model:

**close narration + active dialogue + short self-thought + scene movement**

The prose is not required to hit identical percentages every chapter.

Scene function controls the mix.

# 2. Paragraph / Mobile Readability

Across the audited chapters:
- average paragraphs are generally short;
- no audited chapter relies on repeated 100+ word paragraph blocks;
- action chapters remain visually breathable;
- dialogue is broken by movement/reaction rather than presented as screenplay walls;
- short one-line paragraphs are used for impact rather than every sentence.

**PASS.**

# 3. Dialogue Distribution

Observed dialogue density varies naturally.

Examples:
- CH-001 is low-dialogue because Red wakes alone in the forest;
- CH-004 / CH-010 / CH-012 / CH-013 use heavier dialogue because negotiation and evidence conflict drive the scene;
- CH-014 is deliberately dialogue-heavy because it is a council/Hesk confrontation;
- CH-018 is deliberately dialogue-heavy because it is coalition planning;
- CH-022 / CH-023 are lower-dialogue because continuous physical movement and battlefield geography dominate;
- CH-025 returns to stronger dialogue because civic consequence/accounting matters.

No chapter should be revised merely to force a numerical dialogue target.

# 4. Self-Thought / Internal Commentary

Current Red narration already supports direct internal reaction through short lines such as:
- immediate judgments;
- private jokes;
- selfish motive checks;
- genre comparisons;
- brief serious observations;
- things he deliberately does not say aloud.

Because the POV is first person, these thoughts normally remain plain narration.

Italicized thoughts are optional and should be rare.

The prose should not repeatedly use:
- I thought;
- I wondered;
- I realized;
- I told myself.

**PASS.**

# 5. Close-Narration Distance

Current Arc 1 generally filters setting through Red rather than using neutral catalog description.

Preferred model remains:

**object / movement / sensory fact → Red interpretation**

not:

**neutral paragraph → later Red opinion**

**PASS.**

# 6. Chapter-Type Flexibility

## Solo / Survival
CH-001 may use very little spoken dialogue.

Compensate with:
- immediate perception;
- physical action;
- self-thought;
- fourth-wall address;
- environmental response.

## Work / Action
CH-005, CH-006, CH-007, CH-015, CH-016, CH-019, CH-022, CH-023 may use lower dialogue when movement requires clean geography.

## Investigation / Social
CH-009→CH-014 and CH-017→CH-020 may use more dialogue because evidence, disagreement, authority and procedure are active conflict.

## Aftermath
CH-025→CH-026 may slow slightly while preserving conversation, private reaction and forward movement.

# 7. Character Dialogue Separation

Current voice locks remain mandatory:
- Maelis — precise/evidence-first;
- Tavian — short field logic;
- Brynn — command/assignment language;
- Sela — blunt medical facts;
- Kellan — practical worker/infrastructure language;
- Jessa — cautious procedure + personal employment stakes;
- Hesk — controlled survival/logistics arithmetic;
- Red — theatrical, selfish, meta-comedic, casually arrogant.

**PASS.**

# 8. Common Web-Novel Reading Rhythm

The preferred scene rhythm going forward is:

1. Red notices something.
2. Red privately interprets it.
3. Someone speaks or acts.
4. Red answers / interferes.
5. Another character reacts according to their own motive.
6. The physical/social situation changes.
7. Red gets a final private beat or makes the next move.

Do not force every scene to use this exact order.

It is a pacing engine, not a template.

# 9. Final Decision

**PASS — ARC 1 ALREADY FITS THE NEW CLOSE-NARRATION WEB-NOVEL STANDARD.**

No wholesale POV rewrite is required.

Future revisions should focus only on:
- making distant passages closer;
- adding dialogue where exposition is doing a character's job;
- adding brief private Red reactions where scenes become emotionally flat;
- removing repetitive internal catchphrases;
- preserving mobile-friendly paragraph rhythm.

Gate 9 must now include the Web-Novel Prose Style Standard.
