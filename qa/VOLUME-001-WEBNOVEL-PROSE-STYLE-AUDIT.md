**Status:** **REWRITE REQUIRED — DISTANT THIRD-PERSON RETCON ACTIVE**

# Decision

The previous PASS applied to the now-superseded close first-person style.

The series has been retconned to:

**distant third-person limited + active dialogue + selective Red thought + Fourth-Wall direct-address exception**

Current CH-001→CH-026 manuscripts are still predominantly close first-person and therefore do **not** satisfy the new POV standard yet.

This is a prose/POV synchronization task.

It does not invalidate:
- plot;
- chapter order;
- Greywake events;
- Hesk conflict;
- mystery placements;
- battles;
- supporting-cast roles;
- consequences;
- Red's power model;
- Red's personality.

# Conversion Rules

During rewrite:
- I / me / my narration → Red / he / him / his;
- remove constant access to Red's thoughts;
- move personality into dialogue, behavior, timing, and selective brief thoughts;
- keep external scene description more neutral;
- preserve simple cinematic English;
- preserve mobile-friendly paragraphs;
- preserve all current dialogue unless POV conversion requires a small adjustment;
- preserve genuine Fourth-Wall direct addresses as deliberate first-person speech.

# Gate Decision

CH-001→CH-026 must be revalidated chapter by chapter under the distant-narration standard before the Volume 1 publication set can again be called fully style-compliant.

**Current result: POV RETCON PENDING MANUSCRIPT SYNCHRONIZATION.**
