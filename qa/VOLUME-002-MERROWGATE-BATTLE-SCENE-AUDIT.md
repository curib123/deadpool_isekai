## 1. Scope and Planning Interpretation

Merrowgate is an urban harbor arc. Its action comes from industrial hazards, weather and tide, public/private command conflict, rescue, evidence movement, and infrastructure under load. The arc does not need a fight in every chapter.

The numerical topology changes only when the situation changes. A number such as 2v4 or 1v4 is meaningful here because the objective is inspection protection, route preservation, evidence custody, or public control—not because four ordinary guards can physically threaten Red.

Red remains overwhelmingly powerful. He may end an ordinary physical confrontation immediately, but the planned encounters give him reasons to choose restraint, protect people and evidence, obey specialist timing, preserve public authority, or avoid an intervention that would destroy the larger objective.

## 3. Encounter Cards and Scene OA

### V2-A01 — CH-033 — Short Infrastructure Emergency

**Chapter / Scene ID:** CH-033 / warehouse incident  
**Classification:** HAZARD ACTION  
**Battle / Action ID:** V2-A01  
**Scale:** MINOR ACTION  
**Matchup identity:** Red + warehouse workers vs damaged storage platform, unstable cargo, and competing access priorities  
**Protected targets / non-combatants:** injured workers, nearby operators, active cargo, Nessa's contracts  
**Initial numerical topology:** Red + workers on one response line versus one spreading warehouse/infrastructure hazard; Nessa, Dalen, Iria, and private guards operate on separate priority lines  
**Topology changes:** one blocked cargo route becomes a secondary access problem when the damaged platform shifts; the response divides between people, cargo, and authority  
**Terrain / action geography:** warehouse edge, damaged platform, cargo quay, emergency access lane  
**Primary objective:** clear the immediate hazard and protect people  
**Secondary objective:** prevent cargo loss from becoming a larger commercial liability  
**Why immediate overwhelming force is not the actual objective:** a large intervention could collapse the platform, injure workers, destroy cargo, and contaminate the investigation  
**Red's role:** one bounded brace/obstruction utility only after workers identify the safe direction; physical strength is support, not the whole plan  
**Decisive supporting roles:** workers clear people; Nessa and Dalen prioritize cargo and contracts; Iria keeps private security from owning the incident site  
**Opponent / hazard behavior:** the damaged structure shifts under load and makes the obvious route unsafe  
**Turning point:** the first cleared lane becomes unusable, forcing the response to protect people before cargo  
**Resolution:** workers and Red stabilize enough access for evacuation and controlled cargo removal  
**Cost / irreversible consequence:** damaged cargo, lost time, and an early Venn-linked service opportunity; no combat-topology count  
**Handoff:** Dalen notices that disruption creates profit opportunities, leading into the wider commercial pattern  

**Scene beat spine:** orientation at the damaged platform → first shift → workers' response → secondary route failure → Red chooses a bounded support use → access is stabilized → people/cargo are separated → commercial cost remains → the pattern question advances.

### V2-B01 — CH-031 — Load-Bearing Reputation

**Chapter / Scene ID:** CH-031 / Kesran dry dock repair  
**Classification:** BATTLE/ACTION  
**Battle / Action ID:** V2-B01  
**Scale:** MINOR  
**Matchup identity:** Red + Samir + shipwright crew vs shifting suspended load and wet structural footing  
**Protected targets / non-combatants:** shipwrights, repair crew, suspended equipment, adjacent berth workers  
**Initial numerical topology:** uneven team—Red, Samir, and the crew versus one structural hazard with multiple failure points  
**Topology changes:** stable repair becomes emergency rescue/weight transfer when the load shifts; Samir's safe route becomes the only viable route  
**Terrain / action geography:** crane platform, wet deck, rigging lines, repair berth, water edge  
**Primary objective:** complete the repair without making the yard less safe  
**Secondary objective:** protect workers and preserve Samir's independent yard from further cost  
**Why immediate overwhelming force is not the actual objective:** brute force or a visually large manifestation would change load distribution and endanger the crew  
**Red's role:** follow Samir's load logic; any small manifestation supports a brace, hook, or access move and cannot replace engineering judgment  
**Decisive supporting roles:** Samir owns the load path and repair call; the crew owns lines, footing, and timing  
**Opponent / hazard behavior:** wet footing, shifting weight, and limited crane geometry punish a shortcut rather than a lack of strength  
**Turning point:** the expected repair position becomes unsafe and Samir redirects the crew before Red acts  
**Resolution:** the load is transferred, the repair is completed, and the crew remains alive  
**Cost / irreversible consequence:** repair delay, higher operating cost, and Samir's warning that the yard cannot absorb unlimited security/assurance pressure  
**Handoff:** the port's real structural vulnerability is established before the first deliberate sabotage pattern becomes visible  

**Scene beat spine:** Samir sets the safe load path → first shift → Red proposes a flashy shortcut → Samir rejects it → footing fails → crew changes line position → Red provides bounded support → the repair holds → cost remains visible → Samir's yard becomes a later pressure point.

### V2-B02 — CH-036 — Somebody Else's Accident

**Chapter / Scene ID:** CH-036 / independent dry-dock rescue  
**Classification:** BATTLE/ACTION  
**Battle / Action ID:** V2-B02  
**Scale:** MAJOR RESCUE  
**Matchup identity:** Red + Samir + workers vs failing dock equipment, rising water, and secondary structural collapse  
**Protected targets / non-combatants:** trapped workers, shipwrights, nearby crews, adjacent yard occupants  
**Initial numerical topology:** rescue team versus one failing dry-dock system with two trapped-worker access points  
**Topology changes:** one rescue point becomes two when water and a secondary support failure block the first route; the team splits without losing command  
**Terrain / action geography:** dry-dock floor, waterline, damaged supports, adjacent cargo lane, emergency access  
**Primary objective:** extract trapped workers and prevent a second collapse  
**Secondary objective:** preserve enough of the damaged system for Samir to identify why it failed  
**Why immediate overwhelming force is not the actual objective:** destroying the system may free one person while burying the other and erase the mechanical evidence  
**Red's role:** enter the dangerous space and provide a bounded extraction support at the specialist's call; regeneration does not heal others or make the structure safe  
**Decisive supporting roles:** Samir reads the structure; workers guide the rescue from inside; Iria controls access and keeps the response from becoming a private-security spectacle  
**Opponent / hazard behavior:** water, load, and damaged supports create a moving failure pattern rather than a thinking enemy  
**Turning point:** the first extraction route closes and Samir orders a split rescue instead of a stronger push through the same opening  
**Resolution:** trapped workers are extracted and the secondary collapse is contained long enough for an ordinary investigation  
**Cost / irreversible consequence:** injuries, infrastructure damage, Red's painful depletion, and a credible suspicion that the failure was not random  
**Handoff:** Dalen reports that Venn-backed representatives reach the damaged operator immediately, feeding the pattern investigation  

**Scene beat spine:** rescue orientation → first access attempt → equipment response → water/secondary collapse → Samir splits the team → Red supports one extraction → workers complete the second route → the dock survives enough to inspect → injury and suspicion remain → commercial pattern advances.

### V2-B03 — CH-042 — Dangerous by Contract

**Chapter / Scene ID:** CH-042 / cargo-transfer failure  
**Classification:** BATTLE/ACTION  
**Battle / Action ID:** V2-B03  
**Scale:** MAJOR REVERSAL  
**Matchup identity:** Red + Iria + Samir + dockworkers vs altered cargo-transfer brakes, moving load, structural cascade, and contested response access  
**Protected targets / non-combatants:** dockworkers, civilians, adjacent platforms, rescue crews, evidence of the failure  
**Initial numerical topology:** four-person response coalition and dockworkers against one failing cargo-transfer system; private guards control an adjacent perimeter and create a second command front  
**Topology changes:** the initial lift failure becomes a multi-front rescue; the public rescue perimeter becomes contested access when private security tries to control who enters and what is recorded  
**Terrain / action geography:** cargo-transfer lift, chain-bridge approach, adjacent platforms, moving cargo, emergency Harbor Watch perimeter  
**Primary objective:** save as many people as possible and stop the failure from cascading  
**Secondary objective:** preserve enough mechanical evidence to identify deliberate alteration  
**Why immediate overwhelming force is not the actual objective:** an uncontrolled intervention could drop the load, destroy evidence, or create a larger structural collapse; the first casualty cannot be undone  
**Red's role:** provide one bounded rescue/defensive intervention after the initial failure; he cannot restore the infrastructure or erase the death  
**Decisive supporting roles:** Samir identifies altered mechanics; Iria commands rescue and perimeter; workers move people and cargo away from secondary danger  
**Opponent / hazard behavior:** damaged brakes, gravity, load movement, water, and private command interference compound one another without giving the scene a single boss target  
**Turning point:** the rescue perimeter becomes a public/private control conflict while the damaged lift remains active  
**Resolution:** the immediate cascade is stopped, the area is cleared, and the surviving mechanical evidence remains available  
**Cost / irreversible consequence:** one dockworker dies, several people are injured, infrastructure is damaged, and Red becomes a credible public suspect in the instability Cassian claims requires central control  
**Handoff:** public evidence of Red's off-manifest role spreads before the rescue site is fully cleared, forcing CH-043's choice to remain or leave  

**Scene beat spine:** crowded lift orientation → initial brake failure → rescue response → load/cascade complication → Iria and Samir split command → Red supports one extraction → private perimeter contest changes priorities → the cascade stops → death/injury/evidence cost remains → public suspicion hands into the legal/emotional reversal.

### V2-B04 — CH-045 — The Weak Point

**Chapter / Scene ID:** CH-045 / covert infrastructure inspection  
**Classification:** BATTLE/ACTION  
**Battle / Action ID:** V2-B04  
**Scale:** MINOR HOSTILE INTERCEPTION  
**Matchup identity:** Red + Samir + trusted workers vs four private-security contractors interrupting an infrastructure inspection  
**Protected targets / non-combatants:** trusted workers, inspection evidence, concealed countermeasures, uninvolved dock crews  
**Initial numerical topology:** 2v4 at the first contact—Red and Samir face four contractors while the workers remain behind a structural access point  
**Topology changes:** the interception becomes a Red 1v4 delay when Samir and the workers withdraw with the inspection knowledge; one route is sacrificed to preserve the counter-plan  
**Terrain / action geography:** crane network, chain-bridge service works, narrow maintenance route, concealed inspection point, alternate worker exit  
**Primary objective:** protect the inspection and preserve the counter-plan without alerting compromised personnel  
**Secondary objective:** avoid turning Red's presence into public proof that Cassian's emergency authority is necessary  
**Why immediate overwhelming force is not the actual objective:** flattening four guards exposes Red's capability, creates a legal/security narrative, and may destroy the quiet route needed to inspect the next failure point  
**Red's role:** delay, reposition, or restrain only as needed; the temporary 1v4 is a timing problem, not a power test  
**Decisive supporting roles:** Samir preserves the inspection; workers create a safe retreat; Nessa controls access timing and the usable exit  
**Opponent / hazard behavior:** contractors do not duel honorably; they block routes, call for support, and try to seize the inspection site  
**Turning point:** Samir leaves with the critical structural observation, allowing Red to abandon the winning exchange and preserve the wider plan  
**Resolution:** the inspection survives, the team withdraws, and the contractors gain partial confirmation that a counter-plan exists  
**Cost / irreversible consequence:** secrecy is partially compromised, the team loses time, and Cassian's security network accelerates its deployment  
**Handoff:** Nessa reports unusual movement of Venn-contracted personnel and warehouse keys as weather worsens in CH-046  

**Scene beat spine:** concealed inspection orientation → four contractors cut off the route → Samir and workers begin withdrawal → Red keeps the front from closing → contractors adapt and call support → Red chooses delay over destruction → inspection exits → route is sacrificed → secrecy/timing cost remains → pre-climax deployment begins.

### V2-B05 — CH-047 — Harbor Closed

**Chapter / Scene ID:** CH-047 / first climax chapter  
**Classification:** BATTLE/ACTION  
**Battle / Action ID:** V2-B05  
**Scale:** MAJOR / GREAT-BATTLE BUILD  
**Matchup identity:** Harbor Watch, workers, and Red vs simultaneous infrastructure/weather failures and private-security seizure attempts  
**Protected targets / non-combatants:** workers, civilians, public responders, evacuation/cargo lanes, active berths, evidence routes  
**Initial numerical topology:** distributed coalition response versus three connected failure fronts, while private security forms a competing command force at selected closures  
**Topology changes:** the defensive hold divides into separate fronts; a private-security seizure changes one rescue route into a public-authority confrontation; reinforcement and weather alter which front can still be saved  
**Terrain / action geography:** main cargo basin, active berths, chain-bridge approaches, Harbor Watch command routes, moving cargo and ships  
**Primary objective:** prevent mass casualties and keep enough routes open for public responders  
**Secondary objective:** stop private security from owning closures and evidence sites  
**Why immediate overwhelming force is not the actual objective:** Red cannot safely solve simultaneous fronts at once; a large intervention at one point can redirect cargo, panic crowds, expose evidence, or leave another route without trained control  
**Red's role:** follow specialist timing and perform exactly two sequential support manifestations at assigned points—one access/bridge utility and one bounded brace/anchor/obstruction utility  
**Decisive supporting roles:** Iria owns public command; Samir owns structural response; Dalen owns cargo/evacuation movement; workers execute the physical plan  
**Opponent / hazard behavior:** failures spread through load, weather, tide, and movement; private security uses the confusion to claim emergency authority and seize response space  
**Turning point:** the harbor avoids immediate collapse but the coalition must choose between a visible physical save and the evidence route that can prevent permanent private control  
**Resolution:** public responders retain enough access to continue; the harbor is stabilized for the next phase but the evidence route remains exposed  
**Cost / irreversible consequence:** Red accumulates injury and fatigue, infrastructure is damaged, priorities are exposed, and the political emergency remains live  
**Handoff:** Nessa locates the route to decisive records/agents while the physical crisis continues into CH-048  

**Scene beat spine:** distributed crisis orientation → first failures → coalition response → routes split → private seizure front appears → specialists assign Red two bounded interventions → one front stabilizes while another worsens → the harbor remains open but not safe → cost and evidence urgency persist → CH-048 begins without a reset.

### V2-B06 — CH-048 — Keep It Moving

**Chapter / Scene ID:** CH-048 / final climax convergence  
**Classification:** BATTLE/ACTION  
**Battle / Action ID:** V2-B06  
**Scale:** CLIMAX  
**Matchup identity:** split coalition fronts vs continuing harbor failures, private security, and evidence seizure  
**Protected targets / non-combatants:** harbor workers, evacuation lanes, records/evidence, public authority, independent cargo operators  
**Initial numerical topology:** split-party coalition—Red and structural responders on one crisis front, Iria and Harbor Watch against private seizure, Nessa/Dalen on evidence and evacuation routes  
**Topology changes:** physical and evidentiary fronts exchange priority; private security attempts to seize a route and records; coalition fronts converge only after public control and evidence survive separately  
**Terrain / action geography:** evidence route, cargo basin, service bridge, emergency berth, moving ships/cargo, weather-affected lanes  
**Primary objective:** keep the harbor functional long enough to secure evidence and prevent Cassian's emergency authority from becoming uncontested  
**Secondary objective:** prevent any single faction from owning the port's physical and documentary response  
**Why immediate overwhelming force is not the actual objective:** Red cannot be physically present at every front; a total-force solution would erase the specialists' evidence/control work and let the political narrative survive even if the immediate hazard ended  
**Red's role:** choose one intervention at a time and leave specialists their decisive scenes; the roadmap permits exactly three sequential support manifestations—catch/deflection, temporary anchor/brace, and short-lived barrier/access utility

**Opponent / hazard behavior:** infrastructure, weather, moving cargo, private security, and evidence loss create mutually reinforcing failure; Cassian's representatives adapt instead of waiting for a duel  
**Turning point:** public responders retain physical control while the records proving coordinated sabotage are secured, removing the factual basis for Cassian's emergency consolidation  
**Resolution:** the harbor stays open enough for public response, evidence survives, and private control loses its practical claim; Cassian is not defeated by a punch  
**Cost / irreversible consequence:** one worker is dead, people are injured, infrastructure and trade are damaged, Red ends severely exhausted, and Merrowgate must negotiate real security reform  
**Handoff:** CH-049 owns the legal, commercial, and human aftermath without erasing Red's off-manifest responsibility  

**Scene beat spine:** CH-047 handoff orientation → simultaneous physical and evidence fronts → private-security adaptation → coalition roles separate → Red chooses where not to intervene → physical/evidence turning points occur independently → public control and proof converge → harbor survives at cost → CH-049 aftermath begins.

## 5. Great Battle QA

The great battle is **CH-047 → CH-048**.

- **More than one meaningful objective/front:** physical stabilization, evacuation/cargo movement, public command, private-security interference, and evidence security.
- **Material arc change:** the harbor survives without granting Cassian uncontested emergency control; the political basis of the Unified Harbor Security Charter changes.
- **At least two independent non-Red decisive actors:** Iria owns public command; Samir owns structural response; Nessa, Dalen, and workers also own decisive fronts.
- **Topology change:** distributed defensive response → split fronts → private-security seizure/interception → coalition convergence.
- **Irreversible consequences:** the worker death and injuries from CH-042 remain; CH-047/048 add infrastructure, trade, fatigue, and political costs that are not reset.
- **Environment matters:** tide, weather, cranes, chain bridges, cargo, berths, water, workers, and restricted routes determine what actions are safe.
- **Opposing force objective:** Cassian's network wants a real emergency to become proof that only centralized private authority can run the harbor.
- **Primary objective is not defeat:** the coalition must keep people, routes, evidence, and public control alive; eliminating every guard or confronting Cassian physically is insufficient.
- **Red-only solution prohibited:** Red's manifestations are bounded supports, and the harbor survives only when each specialist completes an independent function.

## 7. Red Battle Engine QA

PASS at planning level:

- no ordinary opponent is treated as Red's raw-power equal;
- no new level, stage, awakening, or progression is required;
- no finite manifestation bar is used as a hidden combat ceiling;
- pain, injury, fatigue, and regeneration consequences remain real without reducing underlying capability;
- serious action is expressed as restraint dropping or selective involvement, not a power-up;
- Red's most important battle choices are objective choices: rescue, evidence, public control, timing, and what not to destroy;
- Fourth-Wall Pause and Play Logic are not used as free tactical cheating in these assignments;
- Red may look dominant in a local exchange and still fail the larger objective if he chooses the wrong front.

## 8. Antagonist and Hostile-Force QA

PASS at planning level:

- Cassian's network is dangerous through systems, timing, money, information, private security, and public narrative;
- private-security contractors pursue access, evidence, route control, and authority rather than waiting for honorable duels;
- industrial hazards behave through load, water, weather, structural failure, and movement rather than villain choreography;
- no named antagonist becomes physically superhuman merely to give Red a fight;
- the hostile plan can act when Red is absent and remains causally independent of him;
- the arc's real conflict survives even if Red instantly wins a local physical exchange.
