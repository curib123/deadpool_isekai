# Opening Structure

CH001 now follows one uninterrupted retention chain:

**death/explosion → missing identity → strange arrival → first improbable event → predator danger → causal luck chain → theatrical bluff → ordinary injury → self-naming → civilization ahead**

This is stronger than the prior split opening because:
- there is no duplicated arrival;
- the reader does not restart momentum after a prologue;
- the protagonist's personality, power pattern, danger, and name origin all pay off in the same first chapter;
- CH002 can begin directly with civilization rather than re-establishing the premise.

# Current Promise

The opening accurately promises the current story:
- Luck Everhart is not the chosen hero;
- he is physically mortal;
- Fortune Distortion is passive;
- he never consciously controls it;
- witnesses will later overinterpret physically plausible lucky outcomes;
- consequences persist.

# Decision

**PASS — CH001 IS THE SINGLE OFFICIAL COMMERCIAL OPENING**

Future edits to CH001 or CH002 should rerun this opening-readiness check.
