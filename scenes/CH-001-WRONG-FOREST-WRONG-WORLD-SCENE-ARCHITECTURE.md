# CH-001 Scene Architecture — Wrong Forest, Wrong World

**Volume:** 1 — Greywake Arc  
**Chapter:** CH-001 — Wrong Forest, Wrong World  
**Phase:** Detailed Scene Architecture  
**Status:** GATE 8 PASS — SCENE ARCHITECTURE LOCKED / CH-001 MANUSCRIPT AUTHORIZED  
**Final Scene Count:** 5  
**POV:** Red Jackal first person  
**Scope:** Scene architecture only. No final dialogue and no manuscript prose.

# 1. Chapter Purpose Lock

CH-001 must accomplish only the approved opening functions:

1. Red Jackal awakens after the Earth collision/transition.
2. He establishes that his environment and body are unfamiliar.
3. He recognizes that he is no longer in an ordinary Earth situation.
4. He encounters one small frontier predator threat.
5. He experiences a minor but real injury.
6. Under immediate pressure, he produces his first crude Stage I Play Logic manifestation.
7. He survives through messy improvisation rather than polished combat mastery.
8. He observes abnormal healing after the danger.
9. He finds maintained road information pointing toward Greywake.
10. He chooses civilization over remaining lost in the Hollow March.

CH-001 does **not** introduce:
- the World System interface;
- Greywake residents;
- Maelis Orra;
- Tavian Rook;
- Rovan Hesk;
- Greywake-local cast;
- F-001, F-002, F-003, or F-004;
- series cosmology explanations.

# 2. Chapter Emotional Curve

**Disorientation → defensive humor → suspicion → fear → desperate improvisation → disturbed relief → controlled forward motion**

The chapter should not begin with Red Jackal already enjoying the isekai.

He uses performance to regain psychological control because he does not actually know whether he is injured, dead, hallucinating, kidnapped, or somewhere impossible.

# 3. Scene Architecture

## SC-001 — Still Alive, Apparently

**Approximate Time:** Minute 0–7 after regaining consciousness.  
**Location:** Hollow March forest floor beneath dense, unfamiliar woodland canopy. No visible settlement or maintained road yet.  
**Characters / Creatures Present:** Red Jackal only.

### Scene Purpose
Establish Red Jackal's voice, Earth memory boundary, bodily disorientation, reader relationship, and baseline fear-management behavior without explaining the world.

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal
Determine whether he is alive, injured, trapped, or hallucinating.

### Conflict
His senses report a physically real environment that does not match the aftermath of the traffic collision he remembers.

His body also feels subtly wrong:
- balance differs;
- musculature feels unfamiliar;
- clothing/body state does not cleanly match his last Earth memory;
- crimson-eye detail may be noticed only if a reflective surface naturally appears later, not forced here.

### Knowledge Entering Scene
Red Jackal knows:
- Earth existed;
- his last clear memory involves rain, impact, and a violent transition;
- he is conscious now.

He does not know:
- Veyr;
- Hollow March;
- Greywake;
- the System;
- regeneration;
- Play Logic;
- reincarnation mechanics;
- whether he is even definitely in another world.

### Discovery
The environment is physically consistent enough that “dream” becomes less convincing.

He confirms basic body function:
- breathing;
- movement;
- pain response;
- no obvious catastrophic injury.

### Reader-Talk Opportunity
Immediate direct address may appear naturally as he tries to frame the absurdity for the audience.

Purpose:
- establish reader-talk from the very first scene;
- let him perform confidence he does not fully feel;
- make the audience his conversational pressure valve.

Do not make the reader relationship itself mysterious.

### Self-Talk Opportunity
Short practical internal questioning:
- body check;
- memory reconstruction;
- survival priorities.

Self-talk should be directed inward and functional, distinct from the outward reader-facing commentary.

### Emotional State
Confused, guarded, frightened beneath sarcasm.

### Aura-Farming Opportunity
Low.

He may instinctively try to compose himself physically—standing properly, checking posture, acting as though waking in an impossible forest is beneath him.

The environment provides no audience response, making the attempt mildly ridiculous.

### Comedy Opportunity
Natural source:
He expects some recognizable isekai explanation—voice, goddess, menu, tutorial, glowing message—and receives absolutely nothing.

No extended reference chain.

### Stage I Play Logic Opportunity
**None.**

He must not discover the ability before danger creates a concrete need.

### Regeneration / Injury Beat
None.

### Worldbuilding Introduced Naturally
Only sensory Hollow March basics:
- dense forest;
- damp/cool frontier air;
- unfamiliar vegetation forms;
- distant animal sounds;
- no modern road noise, aircraft, engines, phone signal, or urban infrastructure.

Avoid naming local species he cannot know.

### Continuity Dependencies
- Earth collision is last clear Earth memory.
- Red Jackal has not yet encountered any Veyran person.
- Chapter 2 owns formal System identification.

### Knowledge Restrictions
Do not use:
- Veyr by name;
- Hollow March by name in Red Jackal's thoughts unless supplied through visible signage later;
- soul/reincarnation technical terms as facts;
- Savael;
- Exterior Needle;
- Eidrathi;
- Great Design;
- Fate mechanics.

Red Jackal may use generic Earth genre vocabulary as speculation or joke, never as confirmed truth.

### What Changes
He moves from passive awakening to active survival assessment.

### Exit Condition
He accepts that remaining still will not answer anything and chooses to move toward higher/clearer ground or signs of human passage.

### Transition
Movement through the forest naturally becomes environmental investigation in SC-002.

## SC-002 — This Is Not My Neighborhood

**Approximate Time:** Minute 7–25.  
**Location:** Deeper Hollow March forest, shallow rise, traces of an old overgrown road or drainage cut, abandoned roadside stonework.  
**Characters / Creatures Present:** Red Jackal; distant unseen wildlife only.

### Scene Purpose
Give Red Jackal enough physical evidence to stop treating the situation as an ordinary Earth accident while introducing the Hollow March as inhabited-but-dangerous frontier rather than untouched wilderness.

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal
Find water, a road, smoke, structures, or any sign of civilization before wandering deeper.

### Conflict
The environment contains human-made traces but they are old, damaged, and unfamiliar.

He has no map, compass, phone service, supplies, or local language context beyond what he may later see on maintained signage.

### Knowledge Entering Scene
He knows:
- he survived/woke after the collision;
- the forest is unfamiliar and physically real;
- he needs civilization.

He still does not know this is Veyr or that supernatural systems exist.

### Discovery
He finds evidence that people use or once used this region:
- worn stone or timber road remnants;
- an old marker;
- cut/managed vegetation patterns;
- abandoned drainage/road engineering.

This tells him civilization exists somewhere nearby, but not how near.

He also notices signs that a larger animal has passed through recently.

### Reader-Talk Opportunity
He can explain his very professional survival strategy to readers while obviously improvising.

Purpose:
show unreliable presentation without making him factually incoherent.

### Self-Talk Opportunity
Practical route choice:
- follow old human infrastructure;
- avoid thick undergrowth;
- listen for running water or travel noise.

### Emotional State
More focused, less panicked, increasingly convinced something impossible happened.

### Aura-Farming Opportunity
Moderate but private.

He may imagine the scene as a dramatic “mysterious wanderer enters another world” moment, only to immediately deal with mud, roots, insects, or an awkward slope.

### Comedy Opportunity
Contrast between the cinematic version in his head and unpleasant frontier terrain.

### Stage I Play Logic Opportunity
None.

The ability still needs urgent, concrete pressure.

### Regeneration / Injury Beat
Minor ordinary scrapes from terrain are allowed but should not visibly regenerate enough to establish the power.

### Worldbuilding Introduced Naturally
- roads in this region can decay quickly under forest pressure;
- there was enough organized travel here to justify old road engineering;
- the area is not safe enough for dense habitation;
- distant animal calls should feel unfamiliar but biologically plausible.

### Continuity Dependencies
CH-001 roadmap requires old road remnants and distant signs of settlement.

### Knowledge Restrictions
Do not identify:
- Greywake yet;
- road-guild systems;
- monster migration;
- march-lures;
- ancient ruins as Eidrathi;
- any named creature Red Jackal has no source for.

### What Changes
Red Jackal gains a direction and realizes civilization is plausible.

He also enters an area used by wildlife and becomes vulnerable to predation.

### Exit Condition
He finds fresher tracks/scat/disturbed brush or hears movement indicating something is following or approaching.

### Transition
Environmental curiosity turns into survival tension in SC-003.

## SC-003 — Something Is Hunting Me

**Approximate Time:** Minute 25–33.  
**Location:** Overgrown road cut / broken roadside ditch with limited sightlines.  
**Characters / Creatures Present:** Red Jackal; one small opportunistic Hollow March predator, species intentionally unnamed because Red Jackal cannot identify it yet.

### Scene Purpose
Force the chapter from exploration into immediate physical danger, establish Red Jackal's lack of combat preparation, and create the injury that later reveals regeneration.

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal
Avoid being bitten/maimed and create enough space to escape.

### Conflict
The predator is:
- physically plausible;
- dangerous to an unarmed unfamiliar traveler;
- not a boss monster;
- not magical enough to create lore questions;
- aggressive because Red Jackal is isolated and looks vulnerable.

Red Jackal has no proper weapon and no reliable understanding of his new body's capabilities.

### Knowledge Entering Scene
He knows:
- something has been tracking him;
- the old route is his best lead toward civilization;
- he has no tested supernatural ability.

He does not know he can manifest anything.

### Discovery
His body is faster/stronger or at least differently balanced than expected, but not enough to make him a trained fighter.

During the struggle, the predator inflicts a minor but meaningful wound:
- forearm;
- shoulder;
- side;
or another non-disabling location.

Exact final injury placement may be chosen during manuscript drafting if continuity remains equivalent.

### Reader-Talk Opportunity
Reader-talk becomes shorter under real threat.

He may continue performing confidence, but the shrinking space for commentary signals fear naturally.

### Self-Talk Opportunity
Fast internal survival calculation:
- distance;
- terrain;
- what can be used as a weapon;
- whether to run or stand.

### Emotional State
Adrenaline, fear, disbelief, increasingly genuine panic.

### Aura-Farming Opportunity
A quick attempt at looking fearless can immediately fail when the animal does not care.

This provides the Chapter 1 aura-farming backfire.

### Comedy Opportunity
Very brief:
The predator is completely immune to intimidation theater.

Do not undercut the injury.

### Stage I Play Logic Opportunity
**Trigger setup only.**

Red Jackal reaches for or desperately imagines a simple physical solution because nothing usable is within reach.

The manifestation itself belongs to SC-004.

### Regeneration / Injury Beat
The predator creates the first real injury.

The wound hurts normally and visibly enough that Red Jackal believes it is a genuine problem.

No immediate painless closure.

### Worldbuilding Introduced Naturally
- Hollow March wildlife can threaten lone travelers close to old roads;
- frontier danger exists before the main plot's manipulated migration becomes relevant;
- wilderness threats do not wait for introductions or quests.

### Continuity Dependencies
The predator encounter must remain small-scale and messy, as locked by the Chapter Roadmap.

### Knowledge Restrictions
Do not imply:
- migration manipulation;
- Fate;
- System levels;
- monster classifications;
- Red Jackal has hidden combat mastery.

### What Changes
Red Jackal moves from uncertain exploration to active survival crisis and becomes physically injured.

### Exit Condition
He is cornered or loses the easy escape route, creating an immediate need for a tool/weapon/barrier that he does not possess.

### Transition
The concrete imagined need triggers SC-004.

## SC-004 — That Was Not There Before

**Approximate Time:** Minute 33–40.  
**Location:** Same roadside cut and nearby broken road structure.  
**Characters / Creatures Present:** Red Jackal; same opportunistic predator.

### Scene Purpose
Deliver the first crude Play Logic manifestation, resolve the immediate predator threat without making Red Jackal look polished or overpowered, and reveal abnormal regeneration only after the action.

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal
Create one usable opening to survive.

### Conflict
He has seconds, pain, fear, and no understanding of what he is doing.

He cannot deliberately select a sophisticated power.

### Knowledge Entering Scene
He knows:
- he is injured;
- the predator can hurt him;
- he lacks a normal weapon/tool;
- he needs something simple immediately.

He does not know imagination can manifest objects.

### Discovery — Play Logic
Under pressure, Red Jackal intensely imagines a basic physical answer.

Approved architecture:
**a crude, short-lived blocking/striking object or slab-like tool manifests close enough to use immediately.**

Requirements:
- rough rather than elegant;
- visibly unstable or inconsistent;
- simple physical function;
- lasts seconds, not minutes;
- may be heavier/lighter/less balanced than expected;
- cannot transform the environment;
- cannot kill the predator effortlessly through raw supernatural scale.

The manifestation allows:
- a block;
- separation;
- one improvised strike;
- or creation of an escape opening.

The exact final visual design remains deferred to manuscript drafting.

### Predator Resolution
Preferred outcome:
Red Jackal drives the predator away, disables it briefly, or kills it only if immediate self-defense makes escape impossible.

The scene must not frame him as an experienced monster hunter.

### Reader-Talk Opportunity
After the manifestation appears, he may briefly address readers with disbelief disguised as confidence.

The point is not “I knew I could do this.”

The point is “I am going to pretend this makes sense because panic is less attractive.”

### Self-Talk Opportunity
Rapid internal checking:
- Did I make that?
- Is it real?
- Can I do it again?
- Why is it already failing?

No technical theory.

### Emotional State
Panic → astonishment → opportunistic focus → shaky relief.

### Aura-Farming Opportunity
After survival, he tries to reframe the messy encounter as a stylish first victory.

This is the chapter's “pretend the accident was intentional” beat.

### Comedy Opportunity
The manifested object may be aesthetically disappointing, awkward, or fail immediately after serving its purpose.

Comedy comes after functional survival, not during the injury peak.

### Stage I Play Logic Opportunity
**FIRST MANIFESTATION — LOCKED.**

It must satisfy:
- crude;
- short-lived;
- unstable;
- physically useful;
- immediate-pressure driven;
- not polished;
- not a named skill;
- not explained.

He should not reproduce it successfully a second time in this chapter.

### Regeneration / Injury Beat
Once immediate danger drops, he checks the wound.

The wound is:
- still painful;
- visibly closing or already smaller than it should be;
- not instantly pristine;
- accompanied by fatigue, heat, throbbing, or other bodily cost.

His reaction should be disturbed rather than triumphant.

He cannot conclude he is immortal.

### Worldbuilding Introduced Naturally
No new formal lore.

The scene establishes only that this world can contain phenomena he cannot explain and that his own body is part of that problem.

### Continuity Dependencies
- CH-005 later becomes first intentional experimentation; therefore CH-001 manifestation must remain largely accidental/desperate.
- CH-007 later becomes first major regeneration demonstration; CH-001 healing must remain minor.

### Knowledge Restrictions
Do not reveal:
- the term Play Logic in Red Jackal's own knowledge unless used only in production metadata;
- self-model mechanism;
- mana;
- System skill;
- hidden class;
- reader-powered ability;
- Fate intervention.

### What Changes
Red Jackal now has two observed impossible facts:
1. an imagined physical solution briefly became real;
2. his injury is healing abnormally.

He understands neither.

### Exit Condition
The predator threat is over, the manifestation is gone, and Red Jackal is physically able—but unsettled and tired—to continue moving.

### Transition
He chooses not to remain at the fight site and follows the old route toward clearer signs of civilization.

## SC-005 — Greywake

**Approximate Time:** Minute 40–70, including cautious travel after the encounter.  
**Location:** Old route joining a better-maintained frontier road; roadside post/sign within the Greywake approach network but outside settlement sight.  
**Characters / Creatures Present:** Red Jackal only; distant ordinary wildlife.

### Scene Purpose
Cool the chapter after action, let Red Jackal process only what he has actually observed, provide the first named destination, and create a clean forward objective for CH-002.

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal
Find civilization before another predator finds him.

### Conflict
He is injured, tired, hungry, uninformed, and now knows that even his own body cannot be treated as normal.

He must choose whether to trust the maintained road rather than continue hiding in wilderness.

### Knowledge Entering Scene
He knows from direct experience:
- the forest is not an ordinary Earth location;
- a strange predator attacked him;
- he manifested one impossible temporary object under pressure;
- his wound is healing too quickly;
- old human infrastructure exists.

He still does not know:
- the world's name;
- the System;
- why any anomaly happened;
- whether other people are safe;
- the settlement's name, politics, culture, or status.

### Discovery
He reaches clearly maintained travel infrastructure:
- repaired road surface or drainage;
- recent wheel/boot/hoof use;
- maintained post/marker;
- recognizable directional iconography indicating an inhabited settlement lies ahead.

Production truth: the road leads toward **Greywake**.

Red Jackal does **not** read or understand the settlement's written name in CH-001.

The actual name **Greywake** is learned through later human contact in CH-002, after language handling is established there.

### Reader-Talk Opportunity
Red Jackal can frame “civilization” as either rescue or the next category of problem.

This restores some conversational rhythm after the serious survival beat.

### Self-Talk Opportunity
Practical choice:
- road versus forest;
- approach openly versus cautiously;
- check wound;
- preserve energy.

### Emotional State
Relieved, suspicious, exhausted, newly curious.

### Aura-Farming Opportunity
A controlled end-of-chapter recovery of persona:
he composes himself before walking toward civilization even though nobody can see him yet.

### Comedy Opportunity
Mild:
He has survived another-world wilderness only to realize the next challenge may simply be talking to people while covered in dirt and blood.

### Stage I Play Logic Opportunity
None.

Do not repeat the manifestation; preserving uncertainty matters.

### Regeneration / Injury Beat
The wound continues closing but is not fully forgotten.

Pain/fatigue follows him toward the road.

### Worldbuilding Introduced Naturally
- this region has maintained routes;
- trade/travel is active enough to justify signage;
- an inhabited settlement exists ahead on the maintained route;
- civilization is close enough to reach by following the road.

Do not introduce Greywake politics or culture yet.

### Continuity Dependencies
CH-002 begins at Greywake gate/intake.

Red Jackal must end CH-001:
- without local currency;
- without formal identity;
- without System knowledge;
- without knowing the settlement name Greywake;
- without meeting any named character;
- with the wound mostly/impossibly improved but not treated;
- with only one experienced Play Logic event.

### Knowledge Restrictions
No:
- World System terminology;
- Valedorn;
- Ledger;
- guild structure;
- monster migration;
- Greywake plot conflict;
- protected mysteries.

### What Changes
Red Jackal gains a concrete objective: reach Greywake and deal with people.

### Exit Condition
He commits to the maintained road toward Greywake.

### Transition
Chapter ends before he meets anyone.

CH-002 opens at Greywake's gate/intake process.
