# CH-002 Scene Architecture — Undefined

**Volume:** 1 — Greywake Arc  
**Chapter:** CH-002 — Undefined  
**Phase:** Detailed Scene Architecture  
**Status:** PROPOSED FOR PRE-MANUSCRIPT SCENE QA  
**Final Scene Count:** 5  
**POV:** Red Jackal first person  
**Scope:** Scene architecture only. No final dialogue and no manuscript prose.

# 1. Chapter Purpose Lock

CH-002 moves Red Jackal from:

**an unknown traveler following a maintained road toward civilization**

to:

**an admitted but suspicious temporary presence inside Greywake whose identity cannot be processed normally.**

The chapter must establish:
1. Red Jackal's first human contact in Veyr;
2. Greywake as the settlement name;
3. a canon-safe spoken communication method;
4. routine frontier intake as normal world behavior;
5. Red Jackal's first direct encounter with World System registration;
6. two independent institutional identity checks that fail consistently;
7. F-001 as a planned clue location only;
8. Captain Brynn Alder as cautious, competent, and non-hysterical;
9. Maelis Orra as an observer with a legitimate professional reason to become interested;
10. limited admission rather than either instant trust or arbitrary imprisonment.

# 2. Language / Communication Lock

Existing canon before CH-002 establishes:
- Red Jackal cannot read Veyran writing;
- no automatic spoken-language comprehension was previously canonized.

The Systems Bible now defines the standard institutional tool:

**Wayfarer Tongue Token**

For CH-002:
- initial contact begins without mutual spoken comprehension;
- Greywake gate staff recognize the language problem as ordinary traveler business;
- a charged Wayfarer Tongue Token is activated;
- the token translates ordinary spoken conversation at short range;
- proper names pass phonetically;
- writing remains unreadable to Red Jackal;
- System text remains unreadable unless a local person explains it aloud.

The token is mundane institutional magic, not a Red Jackal power and not a series mystery clue.

# 3. System Interface Lock

CH-002 uses two independent standard interfaces:

1. **Gate Registry Seal** — fixed gate screening ward.
2. **Certified Identity Slate** — separate desk-mounted intake tool.

They are independent normal tools.

Their consistent failure is the planned basis of **F-001 — Independent Appraisals Fail Consistently**.

CH-002 must not use physical measurement instruments. That material belongs to F-002 / CH-003.

# 4. Red Jackal Declared Name Boundary

Red Jackal may verbally identify himself as:

**Red Jackal**

This is a self-declared alias/persona.

Greywake may manually record:

**Claimed Name / Alias: Red Jackal**

This does **not** change the System field:

**Name: UNDEFINED**

His Earth identity is not required for CH-002 intake and is not automatically disclosed.

## SC-002-01 — The Gate Has Questions

**Approximate Time:** Shortly after CH-001, late morning / early daylight.  
**Location:** Greywake outer approach, timber palisade, gate lane, river-fog edge.  
**Characters Present:** Red Jackal, two Greywake gate guards, gate intake attendant.

### Scene Purpose
Introduce Greywake visually, establish first human contact, solve spoken communication without contradicting CH-001, and move Red Jackal from wilderness uncertainty into institutional scrutiny.

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal
Get through the gate and gain access to food, shelter, information, and safety.

### Opposing Goal
Gate personnel need to determine whether an unknown, dirty, lightly bloodstained traveler can be admitted safely.

### Conflict
Red Jackal approaches expecting conversation but discovers he cannot understand the gate staff's speech and still cannot read local writing.

The guards do not treat this as supernatural. They identify it as a language problem and use a Wayfarer Tongue Token.

Once communication begins, Red Jackal cannot answer basic intake questions cleanly:
- where he came from;
- which road party he belongs to;
- whether he has papers;
- which local authority can verify him.

### Knowledge Entering Scene — Red Jackal
He knows only what CH-001 canon established:
- Earth collision memory;
- non-Earth environment;
- one crude manifestation;
- abnormal painful healing;
- maintained road leads to civilization;
- local writing is unreadable.

He does not know:
- Greywake's name;
- Veyr's name;
- the World System;
- local law;
- local currency;
- registry procedure.

### Supporting Character Knowledge
**Gate staff know:**
- ordinary gate protocol;
- traveler language barriers happen;
- Wayfarer tokens are standard tools;
- unknown travelers without documents require extra screening.

They know nothing about Red Jackal's origin.

### What Red Jackal Discovers
- the settlement is called **Greywake** through spoken translation;
- institutional translation magic exists;
- locals expect travelers to have identity/paper/registry context;
- being near civilization does not automatically mean unrestricted entry.

### Reader-Talk Opportunity
Red Jackal can frame first contact as the moment civilization finally recognizes his importance, immediately undercut by the fact that nobody understands a word he says until a clerk fetches a translation token.

### Self-Talk Opportunity
Practical assessment:
- don't antagonize armed gate staff;
- learn what questions matter;
- do not volunteer more impossible information than necessary.

### Emotional State
Relieved at seeing people, then cautious and mildly irritated at becoming a problem immediately.

### Aura-Farming Opportunity
Low-to-moderate.

He may compose himself before approaching despite dirt/blood and attempt a controlled mysterious presence.

The language barrier ruins the first version of the entrance.

### Comedy Opportunity
Natural contrast:
- Red Jackal prepares for dramatic human contact;
- first result is mutual incomprehension;
- gate staff treat the problem like routine paperwork.

### Dialogue Purpose
- establish translation tool;
- establish settlement name Greywake;
- ask normal intake questions;
- reveal Red Jackal lacks papers and a credible local origin.

No exposition speech.

### System Interaction
None yet beyond gate staff announcing that formal registry screening is required.

### Play Logic Restrictions
No successful manifestation.

Red Jackal does not attempt to prove his strange ability to armed strangers.

### Regeneration Continuity
His CH-001 forearm injury is visibly much improved but:
- still sore;
- still marked;
- not perfectly healed;
- not examined magically.

Gate staff may notice dried blood or damage but do not conduct a healing investigation.

### Worldbuilding Introduced Naturally
- Greywake palisade;
- river fog;
- controlled gate traffic;
- frontier guards;
- carts/travelers;
- standard spoken-translation magic;
- ordinary requirement for traveler screening.

### Continuity Dependencies
Direct continuation from CH-001 maintained road.

### Knowledge Restrictions
Do not explain:

- why he arrived;
- why his wound heals;
- Play Logic;
- the World System beyond what staff naturally introduce.

### Mystery / Foreshadowing Role
None yet.

F-001 does not begin until an actual registry interface is used.
