# 3. F-004 Lock

**F-004 — Play Logic Follows Meaning More Than Spell Grammar**

Planned CH-005 setup:

A manifestation succeeds without normal spell construction and changes when Red Jackal changes the mental framing of what he wants.

The clue must contain both sides:

## Component A — Casting-Structure Mismatch

The manifestation occurs without the normal formal structure expected in Valedorn magic:
- no prepared sigil;
- no enchanted casting tool;
- no learned spoken formula;
- no visible deliberate mana-shaping method;
- no System Skill activation.

A road guard or experienced Guild worker familiar with ordinary Valedorn practical magic may notice that mismatch.

They may interpret it as:
- strange magic;
- improvised anomaly;
- something they do not understand.

They may **not** correctly identify Play Logic's origin or call it a hidden class as fact.

## Component B — Meaning / Framing Difference

First intentional attempt:

**vague frame:** “I need a hammer.”

Result:
- weak, wrong-sized, badly balanced, unsuitable, or otherwise technically “a hammer” but not what the work actually needs;
- short-lived;
- demonstrates that simple wishing is unreliable.

Second intentional attempt:

**clear functional frame:** something equivalent to:

**“I need a hammer that can drive this brace-pin through the swollen bridge timber in one clean strike.”**

Result:
- manifestation changes materially;
- becomes an oversized/excessive but functionally appropriate hammer;
- accomplishes the immediate task;
- may also serve as an improvised defensive object during predator pressure;
- remains unstable and temporary.

The clue is:

**clear concept and intended meaning produce a stronger usable result than vague desire.**
