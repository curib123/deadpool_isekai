**seeing the Main Caravan Road mainly as paid work and proof that Greywake's infrastructure functions**

3. introduce Tavian Rook through independent Warden work already in progress;

10. let Red Jackal's attempt at dramatic positioning be interrupted by a correct safety instruction;
11. end with Tavian's quiet conclusion that the animals are moving wrong;
12. introduce no new F-ID and no Fate/M-007 activation.

## SC-006-03 — The Road That Works

**Location:** Main Caravan Road guarded rest post and adjacent neglected side-route spur.  
**Characters Present:** Red Jackal, Tavian Rook, road guards, caravan travelers, rest-post staff.

### Scene Purpose

Deliver the chapter's geographic/social contrast: one road works because resources are concentrated on it, while nearby lesser routes visibly receive less protection.

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal

### Opposing Goal

Road guards prioritize:
- approved route safety;
- caravan schedule;
- keeping travelers on the maintained corridor.

Tavian prioritizes understanding movement beyond the maintained corridor because side-route ecology can affect the main road.

### Conflict

Tavian wants to inspect the side spur and track crossings near it.

Guild guards consider the main road currently open and protected and see little reason to delay traffic over signs outside the approved corridor.

Nobody needs to be corrupt.

### Knowledge Entering Scene — Red Jackal

- side-route neglect reduces observation quality.

**Road guards know:**
- the main route is maintained, patrolled, and open;

- they are responsible for the caravan, not a broad migration survey.

The Main Caravan Road rest post has:
- guards;

- water;
- maintained surface;
- current markers;
- traffic information.

- ruts;
- brush encroachment;

Tavian identifies older plus fresher crossings from incompatible movement patterns.

## SC-006-04 — Not There

### Red Jackal's Immediate Goal

### Opposing Goal

Tavian wants the threat redirected away from travelers, wagons, and trapped movement lanes before anyone tries to win a dramatic fight.

### Conflict

A prey animal or smaller creature bursts toward/across the road with an ordinary opportunistic predator following.

The road geometry creates a danger:
- wagons restrict movement;
- frightened travelers cluster badly;
- the predator can be accidentally funneled toward people.

Red Jackal initially chooses a dramatic interception position that would make the situation worse by redirecting the animal toward the caravan.

Tavian immediately corrects his position.

### Tavian's Rescue-First Response

Tavian:
- reads the animal's likely line;
- orders travelers behind wagon cover;
- tells guards where to angle rather than chase blindly;
- keeps an escape lane open for the prey/predator to leave the road;
- positions Red Jackal where his speed/aggression is useful without endangering civilians.

This must work because Tavian understands movement, not because the story makes Red Jackal foolish.

### Red Jackal's Contribution

Once repositioned, Red Jackal:
- physically blocks, strikes, feints, or pressures the pursuing predator away from the caravan;
- follows Tavian's safer line despite annoyance;
- demonstrates he can adapt when someone else's expertise is better.

### Supporting Character Knowledge
