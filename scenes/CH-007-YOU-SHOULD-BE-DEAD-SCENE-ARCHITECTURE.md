# 1. Chapter Purpose Lock

CH-007 must move Red Jackal from:

**understanding intellectually that Tavian's safety judgment is useful**

to:

**experiencing the concrete cost of treating his own survivability as permission to take reckless positions around people who cannot recover like he does.**

The chapter must also:

1. escalate CH-006's abnormal migration concern into an unexpected displaced-predator attack;
2. make the attack occur somewhere experienced locals reasonably considered low-risk;
3. make Red Jackal choose a dangerous position partly because he assumes he can absorb the consequences;
4. make that same choice increase danger to another person;
5. give Tavian a rescue-first response that preserves his CH-006 character authority;
6. force Red Jackal to prioritize extraction over looking impressive;
7. permit one short-lived Stage I Play Logic rescue/defense aid without introducing a new power rule;
8. inflict a severe injury on Red Jackal that would be life-threatening to an ordinary person;
9. make regeneration painful, exhausting, and tactically costly rather than empowering;
10. introduce Sela Arven through active triage at Greywake's treatment house;
11. show the material cost and prioritization pressure of frontier medicine;
12. plant F-003 observationally: conventional healing magic encounters a body already correcting itself in a nonstandard way;
13. keep the true regeneration mechanism protected;
14. end with Sela rejecting the idea that surviving a reckless decision makes the decision safe.

# 5. Recklessness / Consequence Lock

The core character causality must be explicit.

Red Jackal makes a tactically understandable but personally reckless choice:

- he deliberately takes or holds a high-risk position because he believes his body can survive the likely hit;
- he focuses on absorbing/containing the strongest predator;
- this opens, narrows, or shifts a safety lane in a way that exposes an ordinary road worker to a second attacker.

The consequence must **not** be:

- random bad luck unrelated to Red Jackal;
- Tavian failing to warn him;
- the worker behaving absurdly;
- Red Jackal intentionally sacrificing someone.

The mistake is:

**he evaluates the risk using his own survivability instead of the survivability of everyone affected by his positioning.**

Tavian recognizes the problem during the action.

Sela names the moral/practical problem afterward.
