# 1. Chapter Purpose Lock

CH-009 begins the investigative movement of Volume 1.

The chapter must move Red Jackal from:

**knowing that official outer-route warnings are stale/incomplete and that animals are moving strangely**

to:

**understanding, through Tavian's field expertise, that repeated predator/prey movement is contradicting expected migration behavior in a directional pattern.**

The chapter must also:

1. reintroduce Tavian through his independent Warden investigation rather than coincidence;
2. connect CH-006/007 abnormal movement with CH-008's warning mismatch;
3. teach Hollow March tracking/ecology through action rather than lecture;
4. establish that careful observation can reveal things Red Jackal cannot improvise his way into knowing;
5. give Tavian clear narrative authority as the specialist;
6. preserve Red Jackal's dry/deadpan annoyance at having to follow a method;
7. make Red Jackal perform one deliberate, unnecessary curiosity test after Tavian explicitly prefers observation first;
8. make that test contaminate the observation and create a localized complication;
9. include one displaced-pack encounter that follows causally from the disturbance rather than appearing as random combat;
10. allow no more than one small Stage I utility manifestation;
11. keep regeneration out of focus and avoid a new injury/recovery clue;
12. move Tavian's perception of Red Jackal toward **R3 — Dangerous Wildcard** for evidence-based reasons;
13. end with repeated field evidence showing movement away from the profitable Main Caravan Road and toward old northern paths;
14. reveal no march-lure, no Hesk connection, no casualty-record fraud, and no protected mystery truth.

# 5. Hollow March Tracking / Ecology Lock

CH-009 should teach field-reading through Tavian's choices.

Useful evidence types:

- track direction;
- relative freshness;
- stride/gait;
- track depth;
- repeated use of the same crossing;
- prey and predator tracks overlapping in abnormal sequence;
- disturbed brush;
- droppings/feeding sign only if necessary;
- whether an animal was walking, fleeing, stalking, or being driven;
- whether tracks represent one panic event or repeated movement across days.

The key abnormality is not merely:

**“predators are present.”**

The abnormality is:

**multiple animals with different ecological roles are repeatedly crossing expected route boundaries in the same broad direction.**

Tavian may explain ordinary possibilities first:
- weather;
- food pressure;
- territorial displacement;
- fire;
- flooding;
- breeding season;
- stronger predator pressure.

The current field pattern does not cleanly fit any single explanation.

Do not invent a new cosmic ecology mechanic.
