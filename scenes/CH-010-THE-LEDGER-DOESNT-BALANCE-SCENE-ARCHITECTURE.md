# 5. Documentary Logic Lock

CH-010 must make the records understandable without relying on invented accounting jargon.

Approved record layers:

- **route assignment / work record** — who or what was assigned to a route;
- **certification record** — identity-bound registered worker/escort status;
- **liability / injury record** — formal protection/claim path;
- **casualty record** — recognized death/loss attached to a countable identity/route category;
- **irregular labor / discretionary expense record** — payment or supplies issued without standard identity-bound worker certification;
- **unresolved / missing / unconfirmed route-loss record** — incidents that may remain outside certified casualty totals.

The key mechanism in CH-010 is **counting/category separation**, not a magical or technical accounting trick.

Important:

A document can be individually valid while the overall reporting picture remains misleading.

Examples allowed:
- a real irregular labor payment exists without a standard registered-worker record;
- a missing person can remain “unconfirmed” rather than enter the certified casualty total;
- a route incident can be logged while the person involved is absent from normal worker statistics;
- certification stamps/status can continue appearing in administrative sequences after a person's practical disappearance if reassignment/closure records were never reconciled.

Do not invent exact laws unless already canonical.
