# 3. Midpoint Proof Standard

The midpoint cannot rest on one confession.

CH-013 must establish deliberate diversion through **three independent evidence classes**:

## A. Physical / Ecological Evidence
Owned primarily by Tavian + CH-011/012 field observations.

Must show:
- lure orientation is functional, not decorative;
- observed migration drift is consistent with repeated redirection;
- the scale/persistence cannot be comfortably explained by one ordinary isolated lure.

## B. Documentary / Operational Evidence
Owned primarily by Maelis + Jessa.

Must show:
- recurring diversion-equipment supply/maintenance/service entries;
- route-management or closure/service instructions tied to more than one location/corridor;
- approval/authorization structure above a junior clerk;
- repeat timing aligned with periods when main-road safety improved and outer-route weak-category losses rose.

The exact document names/codes may be created during manuscript drafting only if they remain ordinary Road Guild records and do not invent a new system.

## C. Decision / Knowledge Evidence
Owned by Hesk's authority + Jessa's processing history + the repeated pattern.

Must show:
- the diversion was not an accidental byproduct unknown to leadership;
- Hesk's office/authority approved or maintained the diversion policy;
- the policy aimed to protect critical road access;
- decision-makers knew pressure moved elsewhere;
- repeated outer-route consequences did not stop the policy.

Only when A + B + C align may the midpoint conclusion become canon.
