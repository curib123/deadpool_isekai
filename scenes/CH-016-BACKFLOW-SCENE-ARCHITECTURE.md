## Bridge Loss Standard

The bridge is not destroyed because it was secretly sabotaged.

It fails because several pressures combine:

- emergency civilian/cart traffic;
- already-limited/repaired infrastructure;
- panicked animal movement;
- sudden load/impact at the approach/deck;
- insufficient time to evacuate in normal load order.

The loss must feel causally earned.

By chapter end:

- the bridge/deck or critical support fails enough that the crossing becomes unusable for carts and organized evacuation;
- the route is considered lost until reconstruction;
- people may still escape by foot through another way, but the bridge is no longer an operational crossing.
