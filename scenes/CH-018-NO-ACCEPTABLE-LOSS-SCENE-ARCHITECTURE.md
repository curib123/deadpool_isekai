# 3. Planning Problem Lock

The planning table must treat three options as insufficient on their own:

## Option A — Abandon / Pull Back From Outer Homesteads

Reject as primary solution because:

- outer communities are part of Greywake;
- they provide food, timber, livestock, labor and repair capacity;
- evacuation capacity is already damaged by the lost seasonal-cut bridge;
- this repeats the acceptable-loss logic under a different name.

Limited temporary evacuation may still be necessary for immediate danger.

## Option B — Restore Hesk's Previous Lure Pattern

Reject as primary solution because:

- the previous pattern knowingly displaced danger toward outer communities;
- current network geometry has changed after CH-015/016;
- blind restoration/strengthening could redistribute pressure again;
- Hesk's old data/reporting cannot be treated as complete neutral truth.

Some known lures may still be used under controlled, observed timing as tools.

## Option C — Do Nothing / Wait For Full Mapping

Reject because:

- backflow is already moving;
- Greywake-facing approaches are under pressure;
- the settlement does not have time for perfect knowledge.

The third option must therefore be:

**use incomplete but current shared information to deliberately create a safer migration corridor that does not designate an inhabited community as the pressure sink.**

## Existing Canon Advantages

Broken North is:

- partly abandoned;
- partly collapsed;
- less inhabited than active outer farm routes;
- connected to older route infrastructure;
- formerly connected to three smaller routes;
- already intersected by the abnormal migration drift investigated in CH-009/011;
- already associated with one known march-lure installation;
- bordered by old retaining works, a watch point and drainage cut;
- geographically capable of receiving movement away from inhabited approaches if made usable.

## Existing Canon Problems

Broken North is not ready because:

- road edges/washouts are unstable;
- roots/forest have reclaimed sections;
- old retaining structures are damaged;
- at least one major gap/washed section exists;
- monster activity is elevated;
- it cannot automatically carry a surge;
- old connectors need verification/clearing;
- CH-011 required a temporary brace just for four people to cross one damaged section.

Therefore CH-018 may select the corridor.

CH-019 owns making it physically viable.
