# 3. Retained Lure Reserve — Writer/Chapter Lock

CH-020 may establish a **Roadmaster emergency reserve** retained outside the shared CH-017/018 operational inventory.

The reserve may contain ordinary already-established lure components/material categories such as:

- sealed scent reservoirs;
- concentrated mana-guided diversion compound;
- spare mana-channel pins;
- replacement vent/service components.

Do not introduce a new magical technology.

## What the Reserve Can Do

The reserve is sufficient, when combined with existing installed lure housings, to restore the old main-road-first / outer-exposure pressure pattern for one emergency cycle or equivalent short emergency sequence.

It is **not** enough to:

- control the entire migration perfectly;
- create a new network from nothing;
- guarantee Hesk's success;
- override ecology like mind control.

## Why It Was Hidden

Hesk retained the reserve because:

- he does not trust shared/committee control under frontier time pressure;
- he believes a Roadmaster must retain a final emergency option;
- he expects the coalition's Broken North plan may fail;
- he intends to restore protection to the main road/core if that happens, even if outer routes again absorb the pressure.

This is consistent with his worldview.

## Discovery Method

The reserve must be discovered through records Jessa can actually understand.

Preferred chain:

1. routine issue logs show high-strength diversion material left the ordinary store ledger;
2. shared emergency inventory does not contain the corresponding amount;
3. there is no valid consumption/service entry explaining all of it;
4. there is no normal return entry;
5. a Roadmaster-controlled contingency/storehouse record or physical stock tag reconciles the discrepancy.

Jessa may identify the discrepancy because she knows:

- issue forms;
- return forms;
- crew codes;
- stock categories;
- route schedules;
- normal paperwork sequence.

She does not infer ecological impact alone.

Maelis verifies documentary meaning and chain of custody.

## SC-020-05 — Do Not Burn the Proof

**Location:** storehouse records aisle / loading yard / Guild core service passage.  
**Characters:** Red, Maelis, Jessa, Hesk, loyalists.

### Purpose

Deliver the short human confrontation and Red's restraint.

### Hesk Action

Hesk/loyalists attempt to:

- remove key stock;
- seize route logs;
- destroy or invalidate evidence;
- close/seal access while escaping through a service route.

### Red Choice

Red has an obvious simple destructive option:

- smash/burn/destroy all lure stock/records.

- the coalition needs the controlled lure material and route information to save Greywake;
- destroying evidence would also erase Jessa's choice and Maelis's case.

### Stage I Manifestation

- small;
- seconds;
- unstable from CH-019 exhaustion;
- no combat damage;
- no second manifestation.

### Human Action

- short grapple/pursuit;
- loyalists divided between Hesk and emergency order;
- Maelis/Jessa secure logs/stock tags;
- Red blocks/coerces space without becoming a killing machine.

### Hesk Escape

Hesk uses the confusion/service route to escape Greywake Core custody.

### What Changes

The coalition wins the evidence/stock fight but loses full operational certainty.

### Exit Condition

Most/all retained stock and proof are secured; Hesk is gone.
