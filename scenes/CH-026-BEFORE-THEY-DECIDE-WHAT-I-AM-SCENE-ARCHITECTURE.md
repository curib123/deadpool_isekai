# 1. Chapter Purpose Lock

CH-026 is the final chapter of the Greywake Arc and Volume 1.

It must close Greywake without selecting the next destination.

CH-026 must:

1. show Greywake visibly reconstructing rather than resetting;
2. preserve damaged roads, reduced trade and ongoing repair;
3. show Red's residual injuries recovering but not magically erased;
4. show the new governance order functioning at least at the level of work/records/representation;
5. give each major supporting character an independent next obligation;
6. prevent Maelis/Tavian/Brynn/Sela/Kellan/Jessa from automatically joining Red;
7. confirm Red is no longer required to remain under emergency anomaly/public-safety restrictions;
8. establish that he has enough ordinary supplies/resources/knowledge to travel independently;
9. preserve his inability to read Veyran;
10. preserve ordinary spoken-language translation through already-established Wayfarer Tongue Token/institutional support where needed;
11. make Greywake's expectations around Red increasingly visible:
   - hero;
   - anomaly;
   - weapon;
   - patient;
   - subject;
   - employee;
   - civic problem/asset;
12. make Red recognize that staying would turn chosen help into an assigned role;
13. preserve genuine care—he does not leave because Greywake meant nothing;
14. preserve anti-villain identity—he refuses ownership, sainthood and permanent duty;
15. give Maelis/Tavian/Brynn/Sela/Kellan/Jessa bounded farewells consistent with their relationships;
16. keep public reputation fragmented rather than authoritative;
17. use no combat;
18. use no required Play Logic;
19. introduce no new F-ID;
20. advance no M-007 clue;
21. reveal no protected series mystery;
22. name no next destination;
23. imply no guaranteed permanent party;
24. end with Red physically departing Greywake into an unnamed wider world.

# 8. Reputation / Assigned-Role Pressure Lock

Red may encounter or hear expectations such as:

- militia seeing him as a useful crisis asset;
- Ledger interest in his anomaly;
- healer treating him as a recurring patient;
- workers wanting his impossible tools;
- residents fearing him;
- officials seeing him as a problem that should be monitored.

The accumulation matters.

Red's realization:

**if he stays, chosen relationships and chosen help will begin hardening into roles other people expect him to keep performing.**

This is the departure pressure.
