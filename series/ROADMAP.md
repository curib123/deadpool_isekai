## 5. Series Locks

One major volume equals one destination arc. Volume 3's destination is not fixed. Supporting characters keep independent goals and expertise. Local arcs work without cosmology reveals, and consequences cross arc boundaries.

Luck never becomes the chosen Hero or correctly discovers Fortune Distortion. There is no supernatural progression tree. His ordinary skills and relationships may grow without replacing his theatrical personality.

## 7. Future Destination Rule

Select a new destination only when current consequences and mystery windows permit it, the location has an independent identity/problem, Luck has a practical reason to travel, and its misunderstanding/consequence pattern is not a repeat of Greywake or Merrowgate.

**The series is Luck Everhart's accidental-legend story, not a legacy Red Jackal power-fantasy plan.**
