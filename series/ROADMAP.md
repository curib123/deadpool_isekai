# 3. Series Locks

- one major volume = one destination arc;
- no fixed Volume 3 destination yet;
- supporting characters retain independent goals and expertise;
- local arcs must work even without cosmology reveals;
- consequences carry across arcs;
- Luck never becomes the chosen Hero;
- Luck never correctly discovers Fortune Distortion;
- no supernatural progression tree exists.

# 4. Current Production Order

1. treat CH001 as the single official beginning;
2. preserve audited CH001–CH027 continuity;
3. keep publication copies synchronized with manuscripts;
4. resume CH028 scene architecture from the current Merrowgate roadmap;
5. run structural/continuity QA before every new manuscript chapter.

# 5. Future Arc Rule

A future destination may be selected only after:
- current arc consequences are known;
- mystery windows permit it;
- destination has its own local identity/problem;
- Luck has a practical reason to go there;
- the arc can create a new kind of misunderstanding/consequence rather than repeating Greywake or Merrowgate.

# Final Lock

**The current series is Luck Everhart's accidental-legend story, not a legacy Red Jackal power-fantasy plan.**
