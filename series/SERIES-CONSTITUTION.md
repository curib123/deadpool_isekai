# Series Constitution

**Title:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Author:** minuszeroo  
**Genres:** Shounen • Action • Adventure • Dark Fantasy • Comedy • Meta-Fiction  
**Status:** LOCKED foundation

## Core Premise
Red Jackal is a foreign anomaly inside a world governed by a World System, Fate, and a deeper Great Design. The world can physically interact with him, but its governing structures cannot properly register or define him.

## Red Jackal
He is an anti-villain: self-centered, theatrical, unpredictable, dangerous, playful, and interested in aura farming when the situation amuses him. He may treat deadly events like entertainment while the world and consequences remain serious.

He directly addresses readers from Chapter 1 and tells the entire story in first person. This relationship with readers does not need a staged progression.

His identity must remain original. Inspirations may inform broad traits but must not become copied characterization, dialogue, lore, or design.

## Structural Law
**World first. Destination second. Story third.**

**One major volume = one destination arc.**

Arc names are place names: **[Destination] Arc**.

Old generic arc plans such as event/theme-based arcs are **DEPRECATED** and must not be reused as the canonical roadmap.

## Local + Global Rule
Every destination has its own culture, environment, local problem, antagonist/opposing force, exploration, climax, and meaningful local resolution while contributing controlled clues or consequences to the larger mystery.

## Consequence Rule
Comedy does not erase death, trauma, destroyed relationships, politics, injuries, or irreversible choices.

## Foundation Gate
Do not lock a long-term arc roadmap until the World Bible and core systems pass QA.
