# 1. Cosmological Stack

Veyr exists inside the **Closed Loom**, the practical boundary within which ordinary reality, souls, magic, Fate, and the Great Design interact.

The major layers are distinct:

1. **Physical Reality** — matter, energy, geography, bodies, ordinary cause and effect.
2. **Magic Field** — ambient and living mana that can be shaped by trained beings.
3. **Soul Layer** — identity continuity, death transition, reincarnation routing.
4. **World System** — later regulatory/registry layer that records and standardizes many living processes.
5. **Fate** — patterned probability and convergence around significant events and beings.
6. **Great Design** — deeper long-horizon stabilizing architecture beneath System and Fate.

No single layer is omnipotent.

# 2. World System

## 2.1 Purpose

The **World System** is a regulatory and descriptive layer superimposed on living reality. It standardizes identity records, progression records, class structures, skill expression, titles, blessings, curses, and measurable combat/health states.

It does not create the universe, souls, magic, or Fate.

## 2.2 Registration Lifecycle

For normal beings:
1. a soul enters or develops within Veyr;
2. the soul receives a valid local registry relation;
3. living embodiment produces a measurable System identity;
4. race and baseline traits are recorded;
5. growth creates level/progression data;
6. classes and skills may emerge through aptitude, training, social practice, thresholds, or formal rites.

Registration can be delayed or locally degraded, but total nonexistence in both world and soul registries is extraordinarily abnormal.

## 2.3 Normal Fields

Common fields include:
- Name
- Race
- Level
- Class
- HP
- MP
- Attributes
- Skills
- Titles
- Blessings
- Curses
- Progression
- Status Conditions

Not every culture displays or interprets these fields identically.

## 2.4 Levels and Experience

Levels are an abstraction of accumulated embodied growth, not a direct kill counter.

Growth sources include:
- training
- dangerous experience
- meaningful combat
- repeated skill use
- survival under stress
- study where relevant to class
- completion of class-specific milestones

Killing can contribute when it produces meaningful exertion or survival growth, but trivial slaughter is inefficient and cannot replace real development.

## 2.5 Classes

Classes are stable System-recognized patterns that emerge from behavior, aptitude, training, social role, and magical development.

Examples of normal class families include martial, scout, artisan, scholar, healer, caster, priestly, command, craft, and hybrid paths.

Class advancement requires actual development. Titles alone do not grant mastery.

## 2.6 Attributes

Attributes reflect measurable capacities such as strength, endurance, agility, perception, cognition, will, and magical capacity. Different institutions use different translated labels.

The System does not reduce a person to attributes; it records patterns it can recognize.

## 2.7 Skills

Skills are stabilized patterns of competence. Some are active, some passive, some magical, and some social or craft-oriented.

A person may know how to do something before the System formalizes it as a Skill.

## 2.8 Titles

Titles are System-recognized contextual records tied to achievements, social recognition, vows, or unusual conditions. Titles may influence how other System functions interpret the bearer, but do not automatically grant overwhelming power.

## 2.9 Blessings and Curses

Blessings and curses are persistent external or internal magical/metaphysical conditions that the System can often label but did not necessarily create.

## 2.10 Death

When a registered being dies, the body ceases producing normal living-state data. The soul layer separates according to reincarnation rules. The System archives the life record but does not own the soul.

## 2.11 What the System Cannot Do

The System cannot:
- create reality from nothing;
- fully rewrite the laws of magic;
- control every soul;
- determine all Fate;
- guarantee prophecy;
- map every remote physical location;
- automatically understand Night Seam phenomena;
- normalize Red Jackal;
- convert Red Jackal into a conventional registered being.

# 3. Magic

## 3.1 Source

Magic uses **mana**, a field-like resource present in living beings, environments, minerals, weather, and some nonphysical structures.

Mana predates the modern World System.

## 3.2 Use

Magic requires some combination of:
- internal capacity;
- sensitivity;
- learned structure;
- intent;
- control;
- environmental access;
- ritual tools.

Different cultures teach different techniques while obeying the same underlying reality.

## 3.3 Spell Construction

A spell is an intentional pattern that shapes mana toward an effect.

Most formal magic uses:
- conceptual intent;
- a shaping method such as words, gestures, sigils, rhythm, geometry, breath, memory, or ritual;
- sufficient mana;
- control;
- an effect compatible with local conditions.

## 3.4 Costs

Magic can cause:
- mana exhaustion;
- physical fatigue;
- nerve pain;
- cognitive overload;
- tissue damage;
- temporary sensory distortion;
- magical backlash;
- environmental depletion in extreme cases.

## 3.5 Failure

Spells can fail through:
- insufficient mana;
- broken concentration;
- incorrect patterning;
- hostile interference;
- incompatible environment;
- resistance;
- unstable artifacts;
- poor synchronization between multiple casters.

## 3.6 Regional Traditions

### Valedorn Sigilcraft
Written geometry, runic arrays, enchanted tools, guild-standard safety. Strong in practical civic and combat magic.

### Serekh Contract Magic
Spoken and written conditions, heat management, water detection, oath wards, caravan protections. Precision matters more than spectacle.

### Tazuuni Hydraulic and Stone Magic
Mana routed through architecture, irrigation gates, ceramics, stone, and controlled heat. Strong in public infrastructure and siege engineering.

### Rootbound Living Magic
Growth shaping, healing, memory-binding, plant communication, beast empathy, and long-duration ecological effects.

### Pale Wardcraft
Heat retention, storm prediction, preservation, light storage, shelter barriers, cold resistance.

## 3.7 Divine, Demonic, and Ancient Magic

**Divine magic** relies on mediated power, covenant, worship, or sacred structures. It is not proof that gods control the Great Design.

**Demonic magic** is a broad cultural label for traditions associated with demonic peoples, corruption zones, or taboo energy patterns. It is not automatically evil.

**Ancient magic** refers to pre-Quiet-Registration practices whose frameworks do not always map cleanly onto modern classes or spell taxonomies.


## 3.7 Wayfarer Tongue Tokens

**Type:** Common enchanted communication tool used at major gates, caravan posts, ports, and customs desks.

A Wayfarer Tongue Token creates a short-range conversational translation field between willing speakers actively addressing one another.

### What It Can Do
- convey ordinary spoken meaning between speakers who do not share a language;
- pass proper names phonetically;
- handle simple questions, answers, warnings, trade, directions, and intake procedures;
- support multiple turns of conversation while the token remains powered and nearby.

### What It Cannot Do
- translate writing, signs, books, contracts, runes, or System text;
- grant literacy or permanent language knowledge;
- read thoughts or hidden intent;
- reveal whether a speaker is lying;
- translate coded ritual language with perfect precision;
- guarantee exact handling of poetry, idioms, legal nuance, technical jargon, or obscure dead languages;
- function at long range.

### Reliability
Common conversation is usually clear. Unusual metaphors, culture-specific expressions, specialist terms, and deliberately ambiguous statements can translate awkwardly and may require clarification.

### Power and Availability
Tokens are ordinary institutional magic in trade-heavy regions, not rare artifacts. Gatehouses generally keep charged tokens for foreign travelers.

They require periodic mana recharge and can fail under damage, interference, or exhausted charge.

### Red Jackal Boundary
A Wayfarer Tongue Token can translate Red Jackal's ordinary spoken Earth language because it works from expressed speech and conversational intent rather than a preloaded language list.

This is **not** a Red Jackal ability.

It does not:
- register him with the World System;
- translate Veyran writing for him;
- explain his arrival;
- interact with Play Logic;
- imply System compatibility;
- connect to Fate, the Great Design, Savael, the Exterior Needle, or any protected mystery.

CH-001 remains unchanged: without such a tool or another established translator, Red Jackal cannot read Veyran writing and does not automatically understand local speech.


# 4. Reincarnation and Souls

## 4.1 Soul Identity

A soul is the persistent identity-bearing structure that can survive biological death. Personality, memory, and embodied experience are not perfectly identical to the soul; they are layers attached to it.

## 4.2 Normal Death Process

1. biological death occurs;
2. living System state closes;
3. soul separates from body;
4. soul enters a transitional state;
5. memory and identity are filtered according to natural/metaphysical conditions;
6. a compatible reincarnation path may eventually form;
7. the new embodiment receives a valid local soul and world registry relation.

## 4.3 Reincarnation Frequency

Most souls do not reincarnate immediately. Timing varies.

Full conscious memory retention is rare.

## 4.4 Memory

Normally:
- emotional tendencies may survive faintly;
- explicit memories erode;
- traumatic fragments sometimes persist;
- magical intervention can preserve or damage memory;
- complete prior-life retention is abnormal and often unstable.

## 4.5 Gods and Reincarnation

Some divine beings, temples, or psychopomp entities can influence transition, protection, or routing, but none possesses universal ownership of all souls.

## 4.6 Soul Registry

The soul registry is not a simple physical database. It is the System's recognized relation to a soul that belongs inside Veyr's normal metaphysical continuity.

## 4.7 Cross-World Souls

Cross-world transfer is theoretically possible but exceptionally rare. A foreign soul normally requires:
- a compatible entry path;
- metaphysical adaptation;
- registry acceptance;
- local embodiment;
- Fate integration.

Red Jackal violates this expected sequence.

## 4.8 Forbidden Experiments

Historical attempts to preserve identity, duplicate souls, bind the dead, or force reincarnation have produced fragmented personalities, possession, artificial ghosts, and corrupted registry states.

None of the known cases equals Red Jackal's complete absence from both world and soul registries.

# 5. Fate

## 5.1 Definition

Fate is the real tendency of significant possibilities to converge around patterns, people, places, promises, prophecies, and historical pressure.

Fate is not a script controlling every footstep.

## 5.2 Fate Can Influence

- probability;
- coincidence density;
- encounter timing;
- survival odds around major convergences;
- prophetic visions;
- recurring historical roles;
- bloodline tendencies;
- major turning points.

## 5.3 Fate Cannot Guarantee

- exact dialogue;
- every decision;
- every battle result;
- total obedience;
- perfect prophecy;
- immunity from outside variables;
- control of an unregistered anomaly.

## 5.4 Prophecy

Prophecy reads likely convergence, not an immutable future. Strong prophecies are accurate because they describe heavily reinforced paths, not because alternatives are impossible.

## 5.5 Fate Resistance

Powerful will, competing prophecies, magic, divine intervention, anomaly conditions, and chaotic systems can reduce predictability.

## 5.6 Red Jackal and Fate

Red Jackal has no valid native destiny record because he did not enter through normal integration.

Consequences:
- Fate can react to his physical actions;
- people around him remain subject to Fate;
- convergences can still pull circumstances around him;
- Fate can resist disruption indirectly;
- he is difficult to predict;
- he is not automatically immune to prophecy, curses, causality, or convergence effects.

# 6. Great Design

## 6.1 Writer Truth

The **Great Design** is the deepest known organizing structure inside the Closed Loom. It is not a person, god, narrator, or ordinary machine.

Its function is to preserve long-horizon coherence: civilizations may rise and fall, people may rebel, wars may change outcomes, but reality resists contradictions that would destabilize the continuity of the world itself.

## 6.2 Scope

The Great Design influences:
- long-term historical stability;
- compatibility between souls, reality, and Fate;
- limits on catastrophic contradiction;
- convergence around world-scale continuity;
- recovery after severe metaphysical disruption.

## 6.3 Limits

The Great Design:
- does not micromanage individuals;
- cannot perfectly predict free decisions;
- cannot directly force every outcome;
- does not fully control foreign variables;
- can be delayed, misled, or locally contradicted;
- requires existing mechanisms such as Fate, world laws, divine agents, or correction phenomena to exert influence.

## 6.4 Relationship to Fate

Fate handles patterned convergence.

The Great Design concerns whether the broader world remains coherent across long timescales.

Fate is one mechanism through which the Design may exert pressure, but Fate is not identical to it.

## 6.5 Relationship to the System

The World System is a regulatory layer that helps classify and stabilize beings inside known reality.

The Great Design is deeper and older.

The System can detect inconsistencies without understanding the Design's full purpose.

## 6.6 Relationship to Gods

Gods may perceive fragments, laws, consequences, or patterns associated with the Great Design. Their religions may interpret those perceptions as divine order.

Gods are not automatically its creators or masters.

## 6.7 Correction Behavior

When severe contradictions accumulate, reality may produce:
- probability pressure;
- converging actors;
- magical instability;
- environmental correction;
- repeated opportunities for stabilization;
- Fate reinforcement.

Correction is not an instant cosmic delete button.

## 6.8 Reveal Boundary

Most mortals do not know the Great Design exists.

Experts may suspect hidden order.

Writer truth remains private until later story architecture deliberately releases it.

# 9. Play Logic

## 9.1 Definition

**Play Logic** is the working term for Red Jackal's imagination-based anomaly.

He imagines a meaningful form, object, transformation, rule-bending interaction, or exaggerated physical concept, and reality may temporarily accept an abnormal expression of that idea.

It is not a menu of conventional skills.

## 9.2 Expression Categories

- temporary objects;
- improvised weapons;
- oversized or absurd tools;
- physical transformations;
- exaggerated momentum or impact;
- visual-symbolic constructs;
- environmental alterations;
- impossible utility objects;
- temporary spatial tricks;
- metaphor-like manifestations;
- rule-bending effects at high mastery.

## 9.3 Core Principle

The effect is strongest when Red Jackal has:
- a clear concept;
- a strong imaginative image;
- sufficient focus;
- emotional or situational commitment;
- an effect he can mentally frame coherently.

Random wishing is weaker than vivid intentional imagination.

## 9.4 Interpretation Risk

Play Logic may follow the *idea* rather than the precise desired engineering.

Possible failures:
- wrong scale;
- wrong material behavior;
- comedic literal interpretation;
- unstable duration;
- unwanted side effect;
- collateral damage;
- effect ending early;
- reality resisting the concept.

## 9.5 Mastery Stages

### Stage I — Improvised Manifestation
Small or short-lived objects and effects. High unpredictability. Best for immediate physical situations.

### Stage II — Controlled Play
More reliable objects, transformations, short environmental manipulations, combination effects, improved duration.

### Stage III — Rule Bending
Can impose temporary abnormal interactions on a local area or target, but strong world laws, Fate pressure, conceptual defenses, and concentration become major factors.

### Stage IV — Narrative-Scale Imagination
Potentially enormous conceptual reach. This is theoretical late mastery, not starting capability. Effects face extreme resistance, cost, instability, and Great Design pressure when they threaten world-scale coherence.

## 9.6 Constraints

### Focus
Distraction, pain, mental interference, fear, and sensory overload can destabilize effects.

### Scale
Larger manifestations are harder to sustain and easier for reality to resist.

### Complexity
A simple hammer is easier than a self-consistent artificial ecosystem.

### Duration
Permanent changes are dramatically harder than temporary ones.

### Knowledge
He can imagine impossible-looking things without understanding engineering, but ignorance increases interpretation risk.

### Resistance
Targets with strong magic, divine protection, conceptual anchors, Fate reinforcement, or reality-stabilizing artifacts can resist.

### Collateral
Play Logic can produce unintended physical consequences. Comedy does not protect bystanders.

### Recovery
Heavy use can leave him mentally exhausted, physically depleted, emotionally unstable, or temporarily unable to form precise manifestations.
