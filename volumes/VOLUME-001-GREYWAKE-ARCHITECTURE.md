# Volume 1 — Greywake Arc

**Destination:** DEST-001 — Greywake. **Chapters:** CH001–CH026.
**Revision:** 2026-10-02 — Fortune Distortion only; distant third-person limited.

Rook wants paid work and freedom to leave. Greywake needs roads that move food and people safely. Hesk has protected the main trade corridor by diverting animal pressure toward outer communities and keeping their losses outside the convenient headline totals. The system works well enough for its beneficiaries to defend it.

The arc moves from personal survival to paid work, from conflicting field observations to evidence, then from exposure to a dangerous premature intervention. Rook disables a lure knowingly, despite incomplete information. Luck makes the extraction possible; it does not make the decision sound. Backflow destroys the seasonal bridge and injures Kellan.

Hesk uses the disaster to argue for centralized control. The alternative is a real, risky evacuation and migration corridor through Broken North, with experts, crews, materials, witnesses, and reversible field timing. Rook helps because the mess has his fingerprints on it and he refuses to become Hesk's excuse or property. He does not acquire a second power or turn into a chosen savior.

The final battle has three overlapping objectives: keep people out of the flow, keep the road carrying load, and stop Hesk's unauthorized eastern pull. A local accident can buy seconds on one front while another worsens. Tavian reads animals, Brynn commands militia, Kellan provides engineering knowledge while immobilized, Jessa controls service information, Maelis preserves evidence, and workers make repairs. Every specialist can disagree with Rook and can act without his permission.

Resolution removes Hesk's unilateral authority and gives outer communities a recorded voice in emergency route decisions. It leaves damage, recovery, uncertain employment and divided public opinion. Rook leaves with supplies, limited money, a wooden entrant tag, unfinished healing and a reputation much tidier than the truth.
