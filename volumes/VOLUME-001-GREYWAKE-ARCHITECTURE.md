# Volume 1 Architecture — Greywake Arc

**Project:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Volume:** 1  
**Destination:** Greywake  
**Range:** CH001–CH026  
**Status:** CURRENT CANON ARCHITECTURE — 2026-10-03  
**POV:** Distant third-person limited — Luck Everhart  
**Power:** Fortune Distortion only  
**Structural source:** `series/STORY-STRUCTURE-BIBLE.md`

# 1. Volume Purpose

Greywake introduces Luck Everhart, Veyr's frontier reality, the World System's inability to classify him, and the accidental-legend engine.

The local story must work without protected cosmology.

Central local question:

**Who gets counted when survival becomes logistics?**

Luck's personal pressure:

**What happens when an institution decides his usefulness gives it the right to define his role?**

# 2. Opening State

Luck arrives:
- after a fragmentary Earth explosion memory;
- with damaged autobiography;
- without usable local identity;
- without money, shelter, or local knowledge;
- physically mortal;
- with passive Fortune Distortion already present;
- unaware that the luck pattern is supernatural.

He chooses the name **Luck Everhart** himself after the first forest survival sequence.

# 3. Greywake Identity

Greywake is a frontier settlement that survives because people continually maintain:
- roads;
- bridges;
- caravans;
- supply depots;
- outer farms;
- timber routes;
- militia coverage;
- medicine and repair capacity.

Its moral pressure comes from finite manpower and unequal political visibility.

The main road is genuinely vital.

The outer communities are genuinely vital.

The conflict works because Hesk is wrong in method and sacrifice, not because the main road is unimportant.

# 4. Local Conflict

The Chartered Road Guild has used illegal or unsafe long-duration march-lures to push animal migration away from:
- the main caravan road;
- Greywake's walls;
- profitable infrastructure.

Pressure is redirected toward:
- outer homesteads;
- minor roads;
- politically weak camps;
- less visible travelers.

The policy has operated long enough to alter animal movement.

The lure network is now unstable.

Stopping it blindly can be dangerous because animals have already adapted to the manipulated corridors.

# 5. Main Antagonist — Rovan Hesk

Hesk wants Greywake to remain supplied and politically worth supporting.

He believes:
**a frontier survives by deciding what cannot be allowed to die.**

He protects:
- the main road;
- trade;
- core supply;
- Greywake's political viability.

He sacrifices:
- outer communities;
- unregistered labor;
- accurate loss records;
- consent.

His policy predates Luck.

If Luck never arrived, the migration crisis, audit pressure, Tavian's field evidence, and outer-community losses would still exist.

# 6. Why Luck Gets Involved

Luck does not decide to save Greywake.

He needs:
- food;
- lodging;
- money;
- information;
- freedom of movement.

Hesk offers off-book work because Luck cannot be normally registered.

Luck accepts because the arrangement benefits him.

This creates the thematic mirror:
the same administrative gap that gives Luck freedom also makes him easy to treat as expendable.

# 7. Supporting Cast Functions

## Maelis Orra
Owns:
- records;
- audit logic;
- statistical/evidentiary discipline.

She can identify suspicious probability clustering without proving Fortune Distortion or conscious control.

## Tavian Rook
Owns:
- tracking;
- migration ecology;
- field safety;
- terrain interpretation.

He is correct often enough that Luck cannot dismiss him as ordinary authority.

## Captain Brynn Alder
Owns:
- militia command;
- civilian protection;
- emergency authority.

She refuses to treat Luck's legend as command rank.

## Sela Arven
Owns:
- medical reality;
- injury consequences.

She prevents the story from romanticizing damage.

## Kellan Dorr
Owns:
- outer-community perspective;
- practical engineering;
- bridge/road knowledge.

He keeps the cost of Hesk's policy human.

## Jessa Vale
Owns:
- route paperwork;
- service logs;
- the ordinary-complicity angle.

Her employment risk makes disclosure costly.

# 8. Volume Movements

## Movement I — The Undefined Stranger (CH001–CH003)
Purpose:
- establish Luck;
- establish chosen name;
- establish identity failure;
- establish physical measurability;
- establish Maelis and Brynn;
- keep cosmology bounded.

## Movement II — Frontier Work (CH004–CH008)
Purpose:
- give Luck money and practical stakes;
- show main-road competence;
- show injury vulnerability;
- introduce Tavian and Kellan;
- contrast protected and neglected routes;
- begin Luck's local reputation.

## Movement III — The Road Is Lying (CH009–CH013)
Purpose:
- combine field evidence and records;
- reveal hidden infrastructure;
- make the migration manipulation deliberate rather than vague suspicion;
- shift Luck from employee to inconvenient witness.

## Movement IV — Necessary Sacrifices (CH014–CH017)
Purpose:
- present Hesk's strongest argument;
- prevent a simplistic "destroy the bad machine" solution;
- let Luck's impatient action contribute to backflow;
- force the story to preserve consequences.

## Movement V — No Acceptable Loss (CH018–CH020)
Purpose:
- build a third option;
- combine engineering, evidence, militia, and ecological knowledge;
- prevent Hesk from quietly restoring the sacrificial policy.

## Movement VI — Greywake Holds (CH021–CH026)
Purpose:
- evacuation;
- corridor preparation;
- migration surge;
- collective climax;
- political/institutional consequence;
- departure.

# 9. Fortune Distortion Use

Greywake may use probability chains for:
- survival;
- accidental access;
- material failure;
- timing;
- discovery;
- openings during action;
- reputation-building misunderstandings.

It may not:
- create tools;
- build structures;
- heal Luck;
- solve migration science;
- generate evidence;
- replace Tavian, Maelis, Kellan, Brynn, Sela, or workers.

# 10. Locked Luck Mistake

The arc requires Luck to contribute to a worsening event.

His intervention against the lure network before the full pressure geometry is understood becomes part of the backflow causal chain.

This is not punishment for caring.

It demonstrates:
- incomplete information matters;
- good intent is not enough;
- being lucky is not the same as being right.

# 11. Climax Requirements

The climax must require:
- Tavian's migration knowledge;
- Kellan/work crews' route engineering;
- Brynn's militia coordination;
- evacuation;
- Maelis/Jessa evidence;
- Greywake labor;
- Luck acting inside openings rather than replacing everyone.

The migration is an ecological/logistical crisis, not one boss monster.

# 12. Resolution Requirements

By CH026:
- Greywake survives;
- Hesk's hidden policy is exposed or stripped of legitimacy;
- outer losses are counted;
- route repair remains necessary;
- no utopian reset occurs;
- Luck receives no System class/level/title;
- Luck does not learn Fortune Distortion;
- injuries/resources remain continuous;
- Luck chooses to leave before the settlement assigns him a permanent role.

# 13. Continuity Handoff

Carry into Volume 2:
- chosen name;
- NO RECORD status;
- literacy limitation;
- reduced money after travel;
- lingering recovery;
- contradictory reputation;
- knowledge that off-book freedom has costs;
- knowledge that his own decisions can worsen a system he does not understand;
- permanent ignorance of his supernatural luck.
