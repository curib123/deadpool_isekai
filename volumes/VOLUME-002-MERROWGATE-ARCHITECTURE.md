# Volume 2 — Merrowgate Arc

**Destination:** DEST-002 — Merrowgate. **Chapters:** CH027–CH050.
**Revision:** 2026-10-02 — current luck-only plan. Only CH027 has manuscript prose.

Merrowgate is a port where cargo, work, assurance and legal responsibility have a price. Rook wants money and passage. His undefined identity blocks bonded work, while his Greywake reputation attracts irregular offers. He accepts a profitable off-manifest delivery without knowing the cargo contains altered brake assemblies.

Cassian Venn benefits from engineered failures through debt, security contracts and a proposed emergency charter. He can offer genuinely useful short-term assistance while using the resulting dependency to consolidate control. He did not summon Rook and does not know his cosmic origin.

The middle turns Rook from useful worker into implicated participant. A receipt with his claimed name can make him liable even when the System cannot define him. His rescues preserve lives without settling that contradiction. Nessa must decide how much of her own business and access to risk; Iria needs proof before transferring coercive authority; Dalen traces who profits; Samir understands what the machinery can actually do.

The climax protects a working harbor and a documentary chain at the same time. Luck may jam a mechanism or spoil an attack long enough for somebody to act. It cannot conjure a support structure, clear every site, heal an injury, or make a copy appear. Cassian adapts under pressure rather than confessing because Rook looks impressive.

The charter is defeated through specific evidence and coordinated public response, leaving repair costs and divided interests. Rook departs voluntarily with earned options and unresolved System identity. His power never progresses. F-010 remains planned for CH038 at a bounded vocabulary level; broader history is protected.
