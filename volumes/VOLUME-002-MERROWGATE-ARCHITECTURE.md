# Volume 2 Architecture — Merrowgate Arc

**Project:** This Isekai Has a Bug. Unfortunately, It's Me.  
**Volume:** 2  
**Destination:** Merrowgate  
**Range:** CH027–CH050  
**Status:** CURRENT ACTIVE ARCHITECTURE — 2026-10-03  
**Current manuscript:** CH027  
**POV:** Distant third-person limited — Luck Everhart  
**Power:** Fortune Distortion only  
**Structural source:** `series/STORY-STRUCTURE-BIBLE.md`

# 1. Volume Purpose

Merrowgate expands the story from frontier survival/logistics into commerce, liability, reputation, and private power.

Central question:

**Who owns a free port when control can be purchased through fear?**

Luck's personal pressure:

**If nobody can officially define him, who gets to profit from the story people tell about him?**

# 2. Opening State

Luck reaches Merrowgate after ordinary road/river travel.

He carries:
- a self-chosen name;
- no valid System identity;
- reduced funds;
- residual Greywake recovery;
- contradictory rumors;
- no companion imported automatically from Volume 1;
- no awareness of Fortune Distortion.

# 3. Merrowgate Identity

Merrowgate is a commercially independent trade port defined by:
- layered docks;
- chain bridges;
- warehouses;
- cranes;
- dry docks;
- migrant labor;
- mixed cultures;
- brokers;
- assurance/insurance;
- debt;
- Harbor Watch;
- private security;
- merchant influence.

People care about:
- contracts;
- solvency;
- reliability;
- cargo;
- guarantees;
- reputation;
- who absorbs risk.

# 4. Local Conflict

A pattern of port instability is being created or amplified through:
- selective theft;
- forged inspection results;
- sabotaged equipment;
- damaged moorings;
- false risk notices;
- targeted failures;
- manipulated emergency response.

The incidents are then converted into:
- distressed debt;
- security contracts;
- warehouse control;
- assurance leverage;
- berth closures;
- political support for centralized private authority.

The proposed **Unified Harbor Security Charter** would place too much emergency control in one commercial network.

# 5. Main Antagonist — Cassian Venn

Cassian Venn is:
- publicly legitimate;
- commercially competent;
- experienced with real port crises;
- able to point to genuine coordination failures;
- willing to engineer instability to prove the need for the authority he wants.

He should be persuasive before he is proven guilty.

He does not need to overpower Luck physically.

His strength is:
- contracts;
- timing;
- distributed agents;
- reputation;
- emergency procedure;
- money;
- plausible deniability;
- the ability to weaponize true facts.

# 6. Why Luck Gets Involved

Luck needs:
- money;
- lodging;
- future passage;
- a way to work despite identity failure.

His missing registry identity makes him valuable for irregular work.

Greywake rumors make him interesting.

Luck enjoys:
- being difficult to categorize;
- being hired because normal systems cannot process him;
- the reputation advantage.

That vanity/convenience leads to the central mistake.

# 7. Locked Luck Mistake — CH035

Luck and Nessa move sealed replacement counterweight brake assemblies through an off-manifest route.

Luck knows:
- inspection is intentionally bypassed;
- the route is deliberately unofficial;
- the pay is high because normal accountability is being avoided.

Luck does not know:
- the assemblies are altered for sabotage;
- the later exact target.

The arc must preserve both truths.

# 10. Fortune Distortion Use

Volume 2 may use probability to influence:
- which already-loose component fails;
- whether a line snags or clears;
- timing of a wake/tide/gust inside plausible conditions;
- which document survives;
- which witness sees something;
- whether an attacker or saboteur makes a bad choice;
- whether Luck finds the right route by accident;
- whether an existing safety mechanism catches at the useful moment.

It may not:
- conjure braces;
- create barriers;
- lift cargo supernaturally;
- regenerate Luck;
- repair machinery instantly;
- prove Cassian's guilt;
- replace Samir's engineering;
- replace Iria's legal work.

# 11. Rescue Design

Rescues use:
- ropes;
- wedges;
- counterweights;
- capstans;
- cranes;
- winches;
- emergency brakes;
- boats;
- access ladders;
- Harbor Watch procedure;
- trained dock crews.

Luck may receive an improbable opening.

A specialist must still identify or execute the correct solution when expertise is required.

# 12. Major Reversal — CH042

A crowded cargo-transfer system fails.

Required consequences:
- at least one death remains real if retained by roadmap;
- injuries remain;
- infrastructure damage remains;
- surviving evidence links altered components to the off-manifest chain;
- Luck's deliberate ignorance becomes publicly relevant;
- Cassian gains political momentum from real chaos.

Luck cannot undo the casualty with luck.

# 13. CH043 Choice

Luck has a plausible route to disappear.

Leaving would:
- protect some of his freedom;
- abandon Nessa to carry more blame;
- let Cassian define the story;
- leave his own choice unresolved.

Luck chooses to stay.

This is not conversion into a conventional hero.

It is a character-consistent refusal to let someone else own the meaning of his decision.

# 14. Climax Requirements

The climax must require:
- Iria's authority/evidence;
- Samir's technical diagnosis;
- Nessa's routes/access;
- Dalen's logistics/pattern knowledge;
- ordinary workers;
- Luck's physical action and probability-created openings.

Luck cannot be everywhere.

Winning requires choosing where he matters and trusting specialists elsewhere.

# 15. Resolution Requirements

Merrowgate may:
- expose Cassian's network;
- reject or reshape the charter;
- create a local witnessed-contract workaround for Luck's chosen name;
- preserve contradictory public opinions about Luck.

It may not:
- create a native System identity for him;
- erase the worker death/injuries;
- absolve Luck of the off-manifest choice;
- solve all corruption;
- reveal Fortune Distortion to Luck.

# 16. Mystery Boundary

F-010 remains planned for CH038 and becomes canon only when manuscript prose and QA plant it.

M-002 may be reinforced through cross-jurisdictional identity failure.

M-011 may surface only in bounded old-terminology evidence.

No full cosmology reveal belongs in Merrowgate.
