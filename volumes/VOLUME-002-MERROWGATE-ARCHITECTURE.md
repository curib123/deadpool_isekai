# 1. Volume Purpose

Merrowgate moves the series from frontier logistics into commercial systems, reputation, liability, sabotage, and privatized authority.

Local question:

**Who controls a free port when every service that keeps it functioning can be bought?**

Luck's personal question:

**What happens when a reputation he enjoys becomes evidence, liability, and a commodity other people can price?**

# 2. Opening State

Luck arrives alone after several travel days.

He carries forward:
- unresolved System identity failure;
- inability to read Veyran;
- healing Greywake injuries;
- finite money;
- contradictory Greywake rumors;
- no conscious knowledge of Fortune Distortion.

# 3. Merrowgate Identity

The port is defined by:
- layered docks and chain bridges;
- cargo cranes and dry docks;
- bonded/unbonded warehouses;
- merchant houses;
- Harbor Watch;
- private security;
- brokers;
- migrant labor;
- shipwrights;
- competing commercial authority.

People care about contracts, solvency, guarantees, cargo, risk, and reputation.

# 4. Antagonist

## Cassian Venn

Cassian is a maritime assurance/security magnate and Harbor Council actor.

He exploits real port weaknesses by engineering or amplifying disruption, then converting fear into:
- distressed debt;
- assurance leverage;
- storage/security contracts;
- political support for centralized emergency authority.

His proposed Unified Harbor Security Charter offers genuine coordination benefits while concentrating dangerous private power.

He is dangerous because his plan is distributed, legal-looking, economically useful to some people, and supported by real insecurity.

## VII — Merrowgate Stays Open (CH047–CH050)
harbor crisis; evidence route; public/private authority conflict; aftermath; responsibility; local identity workaround; departure.

# 6. Major Structural Beats

**Luck's key mistake — CH035:** he knowingly helps bypass ordinary inspection for money/convenience but does not know the cargo is sabotaged.

**Midpoint — CH039:** separate incidents form a consistent Venn-linked profit/authority pattern.

**Major reversal — CH042:** altered brake assemblies contribute to a deadly cargo-transfer failure; one worker dies, others are hurt, infrastructure is damaged, and Luck's off-manifest involvement becomes public.

**Decision — CH043:** Luck chooses not to flee the consequences.

**Climax — CH047–CH048:** specialists and public responders keep the harbor moving while evidence is secured and private seizure is resisted.

**Resolution — CH049:** Cassian's scheme is exposed without pretending every security concern was fake; Luck and Nessa retain responsibility for their choices.

**Departure — CH050:** a narrow witnessed-contract workaround accepts Luck's declared identity locally without solving his System anomaly.

# 7. Fortune Distortion Rules

No planned chapter requires a supernatural display.

If Fortune Distortion appears:
- variables are staged first;
- outcomes remain physically/socially possible;
- Luck does not activate it;
- specialists still own specialist solutions;
- rescue does not erase casualties;
- law/evidence/reputation cannot be lucked away for free.

# 8. Core Cast Functions

- **Iria Voss:** public authority, procedure, rescue command, evidence custody.
- **Samir Kesran:** engineering/shipwright expertise.
- **Nessa Quill:** brokerage, informal routes, plausible-deniability flaw.
- **Dalen Marr:** logistics, risk pricing, commercial records.
- **Cassian Venn:** distributed antagonist using real incentives and systems.
- **Luck Everhart:** irregular worker, accidental legend, bad-job participant, catalyst—not sole solution.

# 9. End State

Merrowgate survives with:
- damaged infrastructure still under repair;
- one death and injuries not erased;
- Cassian's network exposed and constrained;
- security reform still needed;
- Nessa facing consequences;
- Luck's involvement publicly complicated;
- local witnessed-contract status, not System identity;
- Volume 3 still unselected.

# Final Lock

Merrowgate is a commercial-conspiracy and responsibility arc. Luck cannot solve it by power, regeneration, or reputation alone.
