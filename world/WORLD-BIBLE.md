# 1. World Identity

## 1.1 Veyr

**Veyr** is a materially spherical world whose known civilizations occupy only part of one hemisphere. Scholars call the surrounding metaphysical enclosure the **Closed Loom**: a boundary of reality within which ordinary matter, souls, magic, the World System, Fate, and the Great Design interact.

The World System does **not** possess a complete map of Veyr. Its registries are strongest where settled populations, recognized roads, temples, guilds, and administrative anchors have existed for generations. Remote regions can be physically real while remaining poorly indexed.

### Known World
The best-mapped territories are the central and southern reaches of **Avarra**, the western and northern basins of **Kharad**, the coastal and rootland communities of **Namarra**, and the fortress belt along the southern edge of **The Outer Pale**.

### Unexplored World
Large sections of the far western ocean, the northern polar interior, the eastern rootlands, the deep southern salt wastes, and the far hemisphere remain unmapped or only mythically described. The world is deliberately larger than the current known political map.

### Polar Regions
The northern polar landmass, **The Outer Pale**, contains glacial shelves, black-ice valleys, auroral storms, and regions whose distances appear inconsistent when measured repeatedly. The southern pole is mostly oceanic and ringed by seasonal pack ice.

### Divine Territories
No entire continent belongs to the gods. Instead, divine influence concentrates in **sanctified enclaves**, ancient temple-cities, high-altitude shrines, and a small number of places where prayer, Fate, and local magical law overlap unusually strongly.

### Demon Territories
“Demon territory” is a political and ecological category, not a separate planet. Demonic peoples, monster polities, corrupted regions, and hostile magical ecologies exist inside Veyr. Some are recognized states; others are wilderness zones beyond ordinary governance.

### Major Anomaly Zone — Ashfall Belt
The **Ashfall Belt** is a discontinuous chain of burned valleys, glassed ridges, ruined observatories, and dead System nodes running from central Avarra toward the western sea. The Belt is older than current states. Local System readings are unreliable, and some magical phenomena behave as if remembered rules are more important than current ones.

### Dimensional Boundary — Night Seam
The **Night Seam** is the common scholarly name for regions where the boundary between Veyr and whatever lies beyond the Closed Loom becomes unusually thin. It is not a single place. Known Seam events include black-sky intervals, impossible shadows, objects appearing without local origin, and temporary failures of distance.

# 2. Macro Geography

## CONTINENT-01 — Avarra

**Position:** Central known hemisphere.  
**Climate:** Temperate coasts, wet western highlands, cold northern uplands, warm southeastern lowlands.  
**Defining Landform:** The **Crownspine Mountains**, created by an ancient continental collision, divide western rain from the drier interior.

### Major Features
- **REGION-01 Crownspine:** High mountains, iron, silver, mana-crystal veins, fortress roads, avalanche country.
- **REGION-02 Sable Basin:** Fertile river basin centered on the **Sable River**; dense agriculture and old cities.
- **REGION-03 Red Fen:** Warm southeastern marshland rich in alchemical plants, insects, peat, and disease.
- **REGION-04 Hollow March:** Northern frontier of broken forests, ruined roads, monster migration routes, and contested settlements.
- **REGION-05 Ashfall Belt — Avarran Reach:** Burned valleys and glassed slopes crossing abandoned ancient sites.

### Settlement Logic
The Sable River supports the highest population density in the known world. Crownspine passes determine military and commercial traffic. The Hollow March remains sparsely settled because monsters migrate south during harsh northern seasons.

### Political Distribution
The central Sable Basin is dominated by **NATION-01 Valedorn**. Independent mountain holdings and frontier charters weaken direct royal authority toward the Crownspine and Hollow March.

---

## CONTINENT-02 — Kharad

**Position:** South of Avarra across the Brasswater Sea.  
**Climate:** Arid interior, salt basins, seasonal monsoon coast, high desert plateaus.  
**Defining Landform:** Ancient inland seas evaporated over millennia, leaving mineral-rich salt depressions and deep aquifers.

### Major Features
- **REGION-06 Serekh Wells:** Oasis corridor linking northern ports to the interior.
- **REGION-07 Tazuun Basin:** Irrigated river-and-canal heartland supporting imperial grain and horse breeding.
- **REGION-08 Salt-King's Stair:** Terraced escarpment descending toward the white salt flats.
- **REGION-09 Ember Table:** Volcanic plateau with obsidian, sulfur, fire-aspected mana vents.
- **REGION-10 South Monsoon Coast:** Humid ports, spice cultivation, storm seasons.

### Settlement Logic
Cities cluster around stable wells, canals, monsoon ports, and caravan junctions. Control of water matters more than nominal border lines.

### Political Distribution
The **Serekh League** controls the northern oasis-city trade network. The **Tazuun Dominion** controls the central irrigation basin and claims old rights over much of the southern interior.

---

## CONTINENT-03 — Namarra

**Position:** East of Avarra beyond the Glass Current.  
**Climate:** Rain-heavy coast, cloud forests, warm rootlands, stormy eastern capes.  
**Defining Landform:** Enormous ancient trees and mana-reactive root systems reshape soil and water flow over centuries.

### Major Features
- **REGION-11 Rootlands:** Forest floor broken by giant exposed root ridges, suspended lakes, fungal caverns, and living bridges.
- **REGION-12 Lumenfall Coast:** Sea cliffs where bioluminescent mineral-waterfalls descend at night.
- **REGION-13 Green Vault:** Dense interior forest with poor conventional mapping and strong druidic traditions.
- **REGION-14 Eaststorm Cape:** Violent seasonal storms and shipwreck coast.
- **REGION-15 Mirror Marsh:** Still-water wetlands whose mana reflections interfere with navigation spells.

### Settlement Logic
Large cities are rarer than federated grove-towns, river platforms, and cliff settlements. Roads are often living or seasonal rather than permanently paved.

### Political Distribution
The **Rootbound Concord** is the largest recognized political federation but governs by negotiated stewardship rather than territorial ownership in the Avarran sense.

---

## CONTINENT-04 — The Outer Pale

**Position:** Far north.  
**Climate:** Polar; glacial coast, tundra valleys, auroral storm belts.  
**Defining Landform:** Massive ice sheets over older buried terrain. Deep magic and mineral heat create isolated habitable basins.

### Major Features
- **REGION-16 Pale March Belt:** Chain of fortress communes along the southern ice edge.
- **REGION-17 Black-Ice Shelf:** Dark mineral-rich glacier dangerous to cross.
- **REGION-18 Aurora Wastes:** Open tundra beneath recurrent magical auroras.
- **REGION-19 White Labyrinth:** Ice canyons whose routes change after major storms.
- **LANDMARK-01 The Bell Under Snow:** Buried pre-Concord structure emitting a low vibration during auroral peaks.

### Map Irregularity
Repeated expeditions report distance disagreements of up to fifteen percent in some northern zones. Scholars dispute whether this is navigational error, magical refraction, or a Night Seam effect.

# 3. Oceans, Seas, and Islands

## SEA-01 — Brasswater Sea

Separates Avarra and Kharad. Warm southern currents meet colder Avarran runoff, producing fog banks and seasonal storms. Copper-colored algae blooms give parts of the sea its name.

**Major routes:** Valedorn ports ↔ Serekh northern cities; Kharad monsoon coast ↔ western Avarra.  
**Primary hazards:** Reef wyrms, sudden fog, privateers, storm walls.  
**Economic role:** Grain, salt, copper, spices, textiles, mana glass.

## SEA-02 — Glass Current

East of Avarra, running toward Namarra. The current contains suspended translucent mineral flakes that catch moonlight and disrupt some divination magic.

**Major routes:** Eastern Avarra ↔ Lumenfall Coast.  
**Primary hazards:** Storms, mirrorfish swarms, false-light navigation.  
**Economic role:** Rare dyes, timber, medicinal fungi, crystal resin.

## SEA-03 — Widow's Reach

Cold northwestern sea bordering the Outer Pale. Dangerous even in summer.

**Primary hazards:** Pack ice, sea drakes, freezing spray, dead-calm zones.  
**Economic role:** Whale-oil substitutes, coldstone, rare fish, polar relic salvage.

## ISLAND-01 — Morrowchain

A crescent chain of seven inhabited islands in the Brasswater Sea. Governed by competing harbor councils bound by a defensive compact. Known for shipwrights, dry-dock cities, smugglers, and mixed Avarran-Kharadi culture.

## ISLAND-02 — Lantern Atoll

Low coral-and-limestone atoll in the Glass Current. Night-blooming reef organisms illuminate the lagoon. Governed by temple-merchants who regulate anchorage and freshwater.

## ISLAND-03 — The Unmoored Isle

A rarely sighted island west of Avarra whose charted position changes by dozens of kilometers between reliable sightings. No recognized state claims it. It remains **UNUSED** for future planning.

# 4. Nations and Political Powers

## NATION-01 — Kingdom of Valedorn

**Type:** Elective monarchy with hereditary river nobility.  
**Continent:** Avarra.  
**Capital:** **PLACE-01 Veyrhold**, on a fortified bend of the Sable River.  
**Leadership:** Monarch elected for life by the Nine River Houses from eligible royal bloodlines.  
**Succession:** Not strictly parent-to-child; elections often produce political bargaining and occasional civil tension.  
**Population:** Mixed human majority with substantial dwarrow, riverkin, and migrant minorities.  
**Language:** Valic with regional dialects.  
**Religion:** Household river rites, civic saint traditions, and formal temples of the Seven Witnesses.  
**Currency:** Crown and mark.  
**Magic level:** Moderate-high institutional magic; licensed colleges and court mages.  
**Military:** River levies, armored cavalry, house retainers, Crownspine wardens.  
**Economy:** Grain, iron, river trade, textiles, craft guilds.  
**Current problem:** Border settlements in the Hollow March demand greater autonomy while noble houses compete over frontier taxation.  
**System relationship:** Extensive registration through guilds, temples, army rolls, and birth records.  
**Fate relevance:** Royal elections attract prophecy traditions but no crown is officially considered divinely predetermined.

## NATION-02 — Serekh League

**Type:** League of chartered oasis cities.  
**Continent:** Kharad.  
**Capital:** No permanent capital; council rotates among five senior cities.  
**Leadership:** Merchant-charter councils, well-keepers, caravan syndicates.  
**Succession:** Civic offices selected through property, guild, and well-right systems that differ by city.  
**Religion:** Road shrines, ancestor contracts, sun-and-shade dual rites.  
**Currency:** Salt-weight and accepted foreign marks.  
**Magic level:** Practical sigil magic, water-finding, contract wards.  
**Military:** Caravan guards, hired companies, city militias, desert riders.  
**Economy:** Trade brokerage, salt, glass, copper, spices, transit fees.  
**Current problem:** Deep aquifers are falling in three major cities, threatening the balance of voting power.  
**System relationship:** Registration is decentralized and tied to contracts, guild identity, and civic rolls.

## NATION-03 — Tazuun Dominion

**Type:** Hereditary imperial dominion of provinces and canal districts.  
**Continent:** Kharad.  
**Capital:** **PLACE-02 Qas Tazuun**, at the junction of three irrigation channels.  
**Leadership:** Emperor/Empress of the Sun Canal dynasty with provincial governors.  
**Succession:** Dynastic, but confirmed by provincial oath and army command.  
**Religion:** State cult of the First Irrigator alongside local ancestor faiths.  
**Currency:** Solar dinar and grain tallies.  
**Magic level:** High in engineering, heat control, stonework, and irrigation enchantment.  
**Military:** Heavy cavalry, canal infantry, engineer corps, bonded war-mages.  
**Economy:** Grain, horses, ceramics, obsidian, taxation of irrigation.  
**Current problem:** Southern provinces accuse the capital of diverting water and magical maintenance to favored districts.  
**System relationship:** Highly bureaucratic; class and skill certifications influence offices but do not legally determine caste.

## NATION-04 — Rootbound Concord

**Type:** Federation of forest communities and speaker houses.  
**Continent:** Namarra.  
**Capital:** No singular capital; **PLACE-03 Lumenfall** is the largest diplomatic meeting site.  
**Leadership:** Rotating seasonal Conclave of Speakers.  
**Succession:** Community selection; offices are temporary and recallable.  
**Religion:** Ancestral grove rites, memory-trees, local spirits, limited formal priesthood.  
**Currency:** Lumen tokens, barter contracts, resin tallies.  
**Magic level:** High ecological magic, healing, growth-shaping, memory-binding.  
**Military:** Ranger circles, ward-growers, beast partners, local militias.  
**Economy:** Resin, medicine, rare timber under strict harvest law, dyes, living craft.  
**Current problem:** Coastal trade towns want permanent representation proportional to population; interior groves resist centralized power.  
**System relationship:** Registration exists but many communities distrust class-based social ranking.

## NATION-05 — Pale Marches

**Type:** Confederation of fortress communes.  
**Continent:** Outer Pale.  
**Capital:** None; the seasonal military assembly moves among fortress towns.  
**Leadership:** Elected winter captains and civilian quarter councils.  
**Succession:** Seasonal election based on service and trust.  
**Religion:** Last-light rites, ancestor lamps, survival oaths.  
**Currency:** Cold marks, ration chits, imported crowns.  
**Magic level:** Specialized wards, heat retention, storm prediction, preservation.  
**Military:** Universal militia tradition, scout skiers, ward engineers.  
**Economy:** Mining, relic salvage, coldstone, preserved fish, imported grain.  
**Current problem:** Black-ice migration is cutting two traditional supply routes.  
**System relationship:** Registration is practical but incomplete in mobile hunting communities.

### Smaller Powers
- **Free Port of Merrowgate:** Independent Brasswater trade city between Valedorn and Serekh influence.
- **Crownspine Holds:** Semi-independent mountain communities owing limited military service to Valedorn.
- **Ember Principalities:** Competing fortified states on Kharad's volcanic plateau.
- **Stormcliff Houses:** Maritime clans on Namarra's Eaststorm Cape.

# 5. Cultures

## CULTURE-01 — Valic River Culture

**Values:** Reputation, oath-keeping, hospitality, public competence.  
**Family:** Extended households tied to guild or river district.  
**Hierarchy:** Noble houses, chartered guilds, free citizens, tenant communities.  
**Clothing:** Layered wool, river-blue sashes, house pins.  
**Food:** Bread, river fish, root vegetables, fermented sauces.  
**Architecture:** Stone lower floors, timber upper stories, flood stairs.  
**Etiquette:** Guests are offered water before business. Refusing twice is polite; refusing three times is insulting.  
**Festival:** Turning of the Sable, marking seasonal flood retreat.  
**Funerals:** Ash scattered into tributaries unless family tradition forbids.  
**Magic tradition:** Written sigils, guild certification, practical enchantment.  
**Historical trauma:** The River Crown Wars.

## CULTURE-02 — Serekh Caravan Culture

**Values:** Negotiation, memory, measured generosity, water responsibility.  
**Family:** Kin networks commonly extend across multiple oasis cities.  
**Hierarchy:** Well rights and caravan reputation matter more than hereditary nobility.  
**Clothing:** Loose layered cloth, veils, reflective beadwork, travel wraps.  
**Food:** Flatbread, dates, spiced legumes, preserved meat, tea.  
**Architecture:** Courtyard houses, wind towers, shaded markets, underground cisterns.  
**Etiquette:** Water is never wasted during formal hospitality.  
**Festival:** First Shade, celebrating the return of seasonal trade after the hottest months.  
**Funerals:** Stone cairns outside cultivated water zones.  
**Magic tradition:** Contract seals, heat management, water-finding.  
**Historical trauma:** Salt Schism and the Wells Famine.

## CULTURE-03 — Tazuuni Basin Culture

**Values:** Continuity, irrigation duty, disciplined service, public works.  
**Family:** Multi-generational compounds registered to canal districts.  
**Hierarchy:** Imperial office, provincial service, military and engineering rank.  
**Clothing:** Linen, dyed shoulder mantles, rank cords.  
**Food:** Rice-like canal grain, roasted lamb, herbs, sour yogurt, dates.  
**Architecture:** Sun-dried brick, glazed canal gates, shaded colonnades.  
**Etiquette:** Formal titles matter in public administration but are relaxed inside households.  
**Festival:** Opening of Gates, when irrigation channels are ceremonially released.  
**Funerals:** Sealed family vaults above flood level.  
**Magic tradition:** Stone shaping, heat redirection, hydraulic enchantment.  
**Historical trauma:** Collapse of the Old Canal Dynasty.

## CULTURE-04 — Rootbound Grove Culture

**Values:** Stewardship, memory, consensus, reciprocity with place.  
**Family:** Biological and chosen kin recognized equally by many communities.  
**Hierarchy:** Temporary speakers, craft elders, healers; authority is task-specific.  
**Clothing:** Woven bark-fiber, dyed cloth, living ornaments grown rather than cut.  
**Food:** Mushrooms, fruit, river crustaceans, smoked game, fermented roots.  
**Architecture:** Platforms, shaped living wood, stone anchors, suspended bridges.  
**Etiquette:** Visitors state purpose before lineage.  
**Festival:** Remembering Rain, when names of the dead are spoken into seed vessels.  
**Funerals:** Bodies returned to protected root gardens.  
**Magic tradition:** Growth-shaping, healing, memory-binding, beast empathy.  
**Historical trauma:** Root War against foreign timber concessions.

## CULTURE-05 — Pale March Survival Culture

**Values:** Reliability, communal duty, understatement, preparation.  
**Family:** Household membership is flexible; winter survival groups can become legal family units.  
**Hierarchy:** Seasonal captains, quartermasters, healers, hunters.  
**Clothing:** Layered fur, treated cloth, rune-heated liners.  
**Food:** Preserved fish, root mash, fungal bread, imported grain.  
**Architecture:** Low insulated stone, communal heat halls, underground storage.  
**Etiquette:** Boasting about supplies is considered irresponsible.  
**Festival:** Last Sun Feast before deep winter.  
**Funerals:** Cremation when fuel allows; otherwise sealed ice tombs.  
**Magic tradition:** Heat wards, preservation, storm-reading, light storage.  
**Historical trauma:** The Forty-Day Whiteout.

# 6. Major Factions

## FACTION-01 — Ledger of Measures

**Type:** Transnational System-certification and standards organization.  
**Public purpose:** Standardize measurements, class certifications, guild records, and magical safety.  
**True agenda:** Mostly public, but senior auditors secretly collect unusual System failures.  
**Resources:** Archives, inspectors, testing halls, political influence.  
**Hidden knowledge:** Multiple historical cases of partial registration errors, none matching Red Jackal's total absence.  
**Red Jackal relationship:** None established in story; institutionally, he would be considered a priority anomaly.

## FACTION-02 — Ash Choir

**Type:** Esoteric religious movement.  
**Public purpose:** Preserve songs and relics from Ashfall Belt communities.  
**True agenda:** Believes reality periodically “forgets” parts of itself and seeks evidence.  
**Resources:** Pilgrims, relic caches, oral archives.  
**Hidden knowledge:** Some songs describe events absent from official historical records.

## FACTION-03 — Open Hand Caravans

**Type:** Merchant and transport confederation.  
**Public purpose:** Safe cross-border trade.  
**True agenda:** Profit, route control, and political neutrality.  
**Resources:** Wagons, ships, guards, informants, warehouses.  
**Hidden knowledge:** Maintains unofficial maps of roads the System marks incorrectly.

## FACTION-04 — Crownspine Wardens

**Type:** Frontier and mountain defense order.  
**Public purpose:** Keep Crownspine passes open and control dangerous creatures.  
**True agenda:** Same as public mission, though local commanders often resist royal politics.  
**Resources:** Forts, scouts, engineers, monster records.

## FACTION-05 — Daughters of the Last Bell

**Type:** Pale March religious-military order.  
**Public purpose:** Rescue travelers and maintain warning beacons.  
**True agenda:** Investigate the Bell Under Snow and recurring auroral anomalies.  
**Resources:** Fortified shrines, cold-weather specialists, ancient fragments.  
**Hidden knowledge:** The Bell predates every recorded Pale settlement.

# 7. World History

## Era I — First Weathering

Earliest recoverable period. Civilizations were local and written records fragmentary.

### HIST-001 — First Weathering Settlements
**Cause:** Climate stabilization after an older unknown upheaval.  
**Participants:** Early river, coast, and rootland peoples.  
**Consequence:** Permanent agriculture and city foundations.  
**Public version:** Beginning of civilization.  
**Hidden truth:** Ruins beneath several sites are older than the supposed beginning.  
**System link:** No confirmed System inscriptions survive from the earliest layers.

## Era II — River Crowns

Large Avarran river states and early Kharadi irrigation kingdoms rose.

### HIST-002 — River Crown Wars
Competing dynasties fought for control of the Sable Basin. The war ended when merchant guilds and lesser houses forced a settlement creating the basis of Valedorn's elective crown.

**Long-term consequence:** Deep distrust of unconditional hereditary succession.

## Era III — Salt Schism

Kharad's trade cities and inland dynasties fought over water, salt taxes, and sacred well ownership.

### HIST-003 — Salt Schism
**Public version:** Religious war over well rites.  
**Hidden truth:** Water infrastructure failure and debt politics were equally important.  
**Consequence:** Birth of the Serekh League and long rivalry with inland powers.

## Era IV — Root War

Foreign logging concessions and Namarra's grove communities entered a prolonged conflict.

### HIST-004 — Root War
**Cause:** Resource extraction, conflicting property concepts, and magical damage to living root networks.  
**Consequence:** Formation of the Rootbound Concord and strict harvest law.  
**False belief:** The forest itself destroyed an entire invading army in one night.  
**Hidden truth:** Coordinated sabotage, storms, and growth magic caused the disaster.

## Era V — Quiet Registration

Approximately seven centuries before present, System registries became dramatically more consistent across settled regions.

### HIST-005 — Quiet Registration
**Public version:** The System “awakened fully” and blessed civilization.  
**Expert view:** Registration infrastructure and a deeper metaphysical change happened together.  
**Hidden writer truth:** The modern World System is a later regulatory layer, not the origin of reality or magic.  
**Consequence:** Levels, classes, skill records, and guild certification became socially important.

## Era VI — Concord Era

Marked from the treaty network that standardized trade and calendars after several regional wars.

### HIST-006 — Concord Calendar Adoption
Year 0 CC. Major states adopted a shared calendar and measurement standards without political unification.

### HIST-007 — Forty-Day Whiteout
Year 411 CC. Outer Pale storms cut every southern route for forty days. Several communes disappeared.

### HIST-008 — Ashfall Survey Collapse
Year 573 CC. A Ledger expedition into the Ashfall Belt returned with inconsistent maps and three mutually contradictory journals from the same team.

# 8. Economy and Travel

## Currencies

- **Crown:** Valedorn gold-backed high-value coin accepted widely in Avarra.
- **Mark:** Silver trade coin used across Avarra and Brasswater ports.
- **Salt-weight:** Serekh standardized trade weight represented by stamped exchange bars and paper contracts.
- **Solar dinar:** Tazuun state coin.
- **Lumen token:** Rootbound trade token backed by resin, medicinal stock, and federation exchange houses.
- **Cold mark:** Pale March stamped metal ration-credit used locally.

Exchange rates vary seasonally. War, crop failure, and transport disruption matter. Magic does not eliminate inflation, scarcity, or debasement.

## Major Trade Flows

Avarra exports grain, iron, textiles, tools.  
Kharad exports salt, copper, glass, horses, spices, obsidian.  
Namarra exports medicine, resin, dyes, rare woods under quotas.  
Outer Pale exports coldstone, preserved oils, relic salvage, rare minerals.

## Travel Benchmarks

- Good road on foot: 25–35 km/day.
- Mounted courier: 60–90 km/day with remount stations.
- Merchant caravan: 20–30 km/day depending on terrain.
- River barge downstream: 60–100 km/day.
- Ocean vessel in favorable conditions: 120–220 km/day.
- Crownspine crossing: usually 8–20 days depending on pass/weather.
- Brasswater crossing: 5–12 days by route and season.
- Avarra to Namarra by Glass Current: typically 12–24 days.

## Magical Transport

Long-range teleportation exists only through **licensed gate complexes** built on stable anchor sites. Gates are expensive, limited in mass, require matched anchors, consume large mana reserves, and cannot target arbitrary locations. Gate routes can close during magical storms or anchor instability.

## Borders

Major states use customs posts, guild seals, travel documents, cargo inspection, and magical checks inconsistently. Frontier and wilderness borders may have no fixed checkpoint.

# 9. Monster Ecology

## CREATURE-01 — Crownhorn Grazer
Large mountain herbivore of the Crownspine. Feeds on mineral-rich moss and stores trace mana in horn ridges. Preyed upon by cliff drakes and hunted carefully for horn plates.

## CREATURE-02 — Sable Mirejaw
Semi-aquatic ambush predator in southern Sable tributaries and Red Fen. Important apex predator controlling giant rodent populations. Dangerous near fishing settlements.

## CREATURE-03 — Glassback Jackal
Kharadi pack predator with translucent mineral growths along the spine. Hunts small desert ungulates and scavenges caravan kills. Not inherently demonic.

## CREATURE-04 — Rootmoth
Large nocturnal insect of Namarra whose larvae consume dead magical wood. Essential decomposer; overharvesting luminous wing dust damages forest nutrient cycles.

## CREATURE-05 — Frost Lantern
Floating colonial organism of the Outer Pale producing pale heatless light. Feeds on ambient mana and microorganisms. Large blooms attract predators and can confuse navigation.

## CREATURE-06 — Ash Hound
Rare anomaly-linked predator found near the Ashfall Belt. Appears physically normal until stressed, when its position can “skip” short distances. Ledger classification remains disputed.

# 12. Foundation World Logic

- Mountains, rivers, currents, and climate constrain settlement and trade.
- Magic modifies logistics but does not erase distance, scarcity, weather, politics, or ecology.
- Political borders are historical and practical rather than perfectly geometric.
- No state controls an entire continent.
- Cultures can cross borders and states can contain multiple cultures.
- Dangerous wilderness remains plausible because monster reproduction, terrain, magical hazards, and limited manpower make permanent control expensive.
- The known world is not the whole world.
- Unused locations exist independently of future story planning.
