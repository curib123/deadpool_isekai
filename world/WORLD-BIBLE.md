# 3. Nations and Politics
## NATION-[ID]
Official name; common name; type; continent; capital; government; ruler/leadership; population; peoples/races; language; religion; currency; technology; magic level; military; economy; resources; cities; regions; borders; allies; enemies; trade partners; internal factions; laws; history; current political problems; System relationship; Fate relevance.

# 4. Culture
## CULTURE-[ID]

# 7. Economy and Travel
Define currencies, trade routes, merchant hubs, guild fees, rewards, resources, roads, sea routes, caravans, passes, magical transport, teleportation limits, border controls, checkpoints, and approximate travel times.

Large distances cannot disappear unless an established transport rule permits it.

# 8. Monster Ecology
## CREATURE-[ID]
Name; classification; habitat; range; diet; behavior; reproduction; predators; prey; magical properties; threat; economic uses; materials; civilization relations; migration; System interaction; counters.

Monsters should belong to ecosystems unless supernatural rules explain otherwise.

# 9. Knowledge Layers

# 10. World Map Logic
Mountains influence climate/travel; rivers shape settlements/trade; ports need reasons; borders respond to geography/history/resources; supernatural zones change settlement and economics.

# Foundation QA Gate
Before locking the destination roadmap verify:
- major continents named and defined,
- major seas/oceans established,
- major states established,
- world history established,
- core systems established,
- cultures/factions differentiated,
- travel/economy coherent,
- enough unused places exist for a long-running journey.

If major fields are missing, destination roadmap remains PROVISIONAL.
